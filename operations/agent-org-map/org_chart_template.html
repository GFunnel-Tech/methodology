GFunnel Agent Org Chart

Reporting hierarchy for the task-specific agents that run a business on the GFunnel Methodology. Solid lines are chain of command. Dotted lines (audit, gates, escalation) are listed on each agent's card. Search for a deliverable to find the manager accountable for it. Select any agent to see its full role and team.

Governance Orchestration High Yang hubs (Marketing, Sales, Operations) High Yin hubs (Strategy, Finance, HR & Culture, Content) Bridging hubs (Technology, Client Success)

Derivation, not canon: the methodology names the nine hubs, their functions and the algorithms; the reporting lines and autonomy levels are this map's choices and can be redrawn without moving any responsibility.
