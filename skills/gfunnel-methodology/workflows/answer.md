# Workflow — Answer a Framework Question

Use for: "what does the framework say about X", "explain the Dynamic Middle", "what is Layer V", "is Y proven", definitions, comparisons between layers or versions.

## Steps (in order)

1. **Route.** Name the layer, domain, register or version the question lives in.
   - Concepts and layers → `framework/layers.md` (one line per layer + the v5.1 section name).
   - Domains → `framework/domains.md`.
   - Terms → `docs/glossary.md`, then v5.1 *Glossary of Framework Terms* (`SECTIONS.md`).
   - "Is it proven / open / falsifiable" → `framework/registers.md`, then v5.1 Appendix B (Variable Registry) and Appendix C (Falsifiability Predictions).
   - v5.4 "gap N" → `framework/gaps.md` (the numbering is reconstructed there).
   - Meta-tier ⊙, Forcing Test, Anti-Operation → v5.3 §1–§3.
   - Line-Field / Knot-Line / Lamina / frequency → v5.4 §1–§7 and §4a.
   - Cross-cutting questions → v5.1 *Cross-Reference Index*.
2. **Read the canon, not just the map.** Maps are an index; they "restate nothing". Look up the section in `SECTIONS.md` and read that line range of the version file.
3. **Check later versions for movement.** A variable v5.1 held open may have been narrowed in v5.2 §7, v5.3 §4, or v5.4 §12, and the ledgers are in v5.2 §4, v5.3 §5, v5.4 §13 and `framework/registers.md`. For v5.4 §14 tests, `framework/tests/README.md` supersedes the canonical wording.
4. **Answer with the three statuses kept apart:**
   - **Measured / empirically settled**: say so.
   - **Structurally derived**: say it is the framework's derivation.
   - **Held open**: say it is open, what bounds it (what *is* measurable on either side), and do not fill it.
   Use the Forcing Test outcome labels where the canon assigns one: *forced-fill*, *live hypothesis*, *stipulation*, *proven-open*.
5. **State Correspondence** if the concept runs at another scale (one up, one down). v5.1 *Civil Infrastructure as Correspondence Proof* and *The Universal Process Registry* are the canonical examples.
6. **Flag empirical gaps.** If the question needs outside facts (prices, dates, measurements), say so and retrieve them or tell the user to.
7. **Cite.** Name the section(s) and version(s) read. Attach the credit line from `SKILL.md` §5 when you reproduce or closely paraphrase.

## Watch for

- The count "17 layers" = 16 numbered/symboled layers (0 → ◇) + the meta-tier ⊙. ◇ was re-tiered in v5.3 as the Container's falsifiability component; its content is unchanged.
- Religious and metaphysical material (God as total system, Source C, the Third Kingdom) is part of the canon. Report what the canon says and its status; do not upgrade or dismiss it.
- `audit/`, `containers/`, `substrate/` and `tasks/` are **work in progress, not canon**. If you cite them, say so.
- If the user asks your opinion of the framework's truth, you may give it, but separate it clearly from what the framework says about itself.
