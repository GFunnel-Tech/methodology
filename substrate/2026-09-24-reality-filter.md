## §0 · Why this document exists

The methodology is an **explicit log**: the written record of what is known (`versions/`, `framework/`). Reality keeps an **implicit log**: it records itself in its own structure. Element abundances record stellar history. DNA sequences record what survived. Rock strata record elapsed time. Nobody holds reality directly. What can be held is a set of reproducible observations of it.

The Reality Audit compares the two logs. This document states the rules for that comparison. It is written down so the audit's governing model is loaded as substrate, not carried in a conversation that the next run cannot read.

---

## §1 · The governing model (eight items)

### 1. Reality is the authority. The methodology adapts.

When observation differs from what the methodology predicts, the methodology changes. Reality does not.

*Relation to canon:* v5.1's Falsifiability Register (Layer ◇) already states the criterion *"A framework that cannot be disproved by any conceivable evidence is not a framework — it is a belief,"* and names what would force revision. This item states the direction of that revision explicitly: the written log moves, the observation stays.

### 2. The methodology is the ever-growing log of the known.

It forms to reality the way surface tension forms to a liquid. The boundary is made by the substance itself; it is never forced onto it; and it adapts continuously as the substance grows. The log grows without bound. **Completeness is a direction, not a state.**

*Relation to canon:* v5.1 Appendix D already says the registry is *"a working set — comprehensive enough to constrain v6, incomplete enough to require v6 to extend it,"* and that completeness in all categories is, per Gödel, *"structurally guaranteed not."* Whether this item is the same structure as v5.3's Container ⊙, a refinement of it, or in conflict with it is **open** (FINDINGS F-004, Phase 1).

### 3. Concentration discipline (molarity).

The log must not claim more than its content supports: a film that is too thin bursts. It must not hold content it has not integrated: pressure builds. Growth keeps the ratio intact.

*Operational reading:* a claim's stated status must not exceed its evidence (no over-claim), and an observation taken into the log must be integrated — mapped, graded, and linked — not left as an unprocessed note (no un-integrated intake). The audit's exclusion diagnosis `beyond-depth` exists to apply this rule (see [`../audit/SCHEMA.md`](../audit/SCHEMA.md) §1).

### 4. No outside models imported.

External theories are not brought in as structure. They may be **discovered** through the process: if they are real, the audit will reach their observations. Names and narratives are interpretations. They survive only if reality forces them.

> **Observations stay. Narratives go.**

### 5. Reference points, filtered.

External material may be used only as a source of **measurements**. Strip the interpretation; keep the measured quantity, its uncertainty, and where it was measured.

### 6. Every solved or gained item becomes a container.

A container is open-ended and carries an algorithm or an equation. Each container is the fast starting point for future work, so no one has to re-study the item from zero. Every container carries at least one open edge (v5.3's Anti-Operation: every fill opens a conjugate gap).

*Relation to canon:* v5.1 Master Meta-Algorithm step 11 (*"if it is not written, it does not exist as a process"*) and v5.4's conjugate-gap discipline imply such a unit; canon gives it no standard form (FINDINGS F-007).

### 7. The base-code rule is scoped. *(Author decision — governance departure.)*

"The base code does not change" becomes:

> **"The base code holds until reality contradicts it."**

This is a departure from [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](../CONTRIBUTING.md) ("The one rule") and [`AGENTS.md`](../AGENTS.md) ("Do not alter the base code"). It is recorded here as an **operator decision**. It is not applied to those files by this document. The replacement wording is drafted in Phase 1 (PR 2) and applied only in the v5.5 draft (Phase 8), where it is logged as a departure that operates *on* the base, the same way v5.3 logged the Container.

*What the scoping does not do:* it does not license edits to released version files. `versions/vX.Y/` stay immutable. A contradiction by reality is recorded in a **new** version that loads the old one as substrate; the old text remains in the lineage.

### 8. Weak observations cannot force change.

The Variable Principle applies to the reality log exactly as it applies to claims. An observation that has not been reproduced goes to **Open**, carries a review date, and cannot adjust the methodology.

## §2 · What this document does not claim

- It does not claim any of the eight items is forced. Phase 1 classifies them.
- It does not claim the methodology currently conflicts with reality anywhere. That is what the audit measures.
- It does not claim external theories are false. It declines to import them as structure; their observations remain admissible as measurements (item 5).
- It does not change the base code, any released version, `CONTRIBUTING.md`, or `AGENTS.md`.
