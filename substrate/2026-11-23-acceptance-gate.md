## What it changes

1. **A gate before entry.** Every new fill passes an eight-stage filter modelled on cellular quality control (membrane → receptor → self/non-self → checkpoint → proofreading → mismatch repair → folding QC → transcription) before it is written into the methodology as its own. Procedure: [`skills/gfunnel-methodology/workflows/accept.md`](../skills/gfunnel-methodology/workflows/accept.md).
2. **Imported material is non-self.** A formula, constant or rule from outside is labelled *borrowed* and quarantined; it may be used and published, not transcribed.
3. **Independence is required.** At least three kinds of filter (reality, a different reasoner, canon consistency). An AI may run the gate; it may not mark a fill accepted on its own.
4. **Working documents stay editable; sealed records do not.** New information updates tests, substrate notes, the ledger and pages freely. A sealed prediction (`audit/prediction/`) is updated by appending below the seal, never by rewriting it, because the seal's value is that it cannot change after the data.
