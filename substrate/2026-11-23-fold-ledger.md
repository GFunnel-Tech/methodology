## 4. Correction proposals against released wording (not applied; versions are immutable)

1. **v5.4 §4 rule 5** — "*fusion cold in either environment is forced-no*" is too strong. Muon-catalysed fusion runs at cryogenic temperatures and has been measured since 1956 (Alvarez), up to ~150 fusions per muon. It is an instance of **rule 4 (mediation)**. Proposed wording: *cold fusion with net energy gain: not achieved; cold fusion via a mediator: measured.* Class: conflict → correction.
2. **v5.4 §5** — "*CMB 2.7 K … stretched 1100×*" states expansion, the one ingredient the canon imports rather than derives. Under R10 the frame-neutral wording is *"CMB read 1100× red relative to present nodes"*. Class: stipulation (frame choice).
3. **v5.1 Layer I.E, Practical Implication** — "*same answer at every scale. Zero exceptions*" holds for the *operation* (gravitational folding) but not for *structure*: the universe is homogeneous above ~100 Mpc. Class: conflict, resolved by reading Correspondence as operation, not shape.
