# Author Clarification — the level at which "no information is discarded" is claimed

*Substrate document · 2026-09-28 · Clarification given by the author (Cameron Garlick) in response to audit finding F-067 · Loads v5.1–v5.4 and the Reality Audit as prior substrate*

> **Status: substrate, not canon.** It records what the author says the claim means. Whether the clarification rescues the claim is graded in [`../audit/claims/CLAIM-021.md`](../audit/claims/CLAIM-021.md), not here.

---

## The clarification, as given

> *"The no information discarded would be established on a quantum level via quantum superposition and structure stability."*

## What the audit had found (the finding this answers)

Audit finding **F-067** graded Layer I.G's *"nothing is lost / nothing is discarded"* and Core Axiom 14 **forced-no** at molecular scale, on measured bacterial machinery: replication fidelity is bought by three serial discard stages, excision repair releases and degrades the damaged strand, and DNA that fails to recombine is destroyed by RecBCD ([`../audit/sweep/SWEEP-012.md`](../audit/sweep/SWEEP-012.md), [`SWEEP-020.md`](../audit/sweep/SWEEP-020.md)).

## What the clarification changes

It says the audit tested the claim at the wrong level. The claim is **physical**, about the quantum substrate, not about whether a cell keeps a nucleotide. On that reading, a discarded base is not annihilated: its quantum state persists, and the stable structures that carry records persist.

This is a legitimate move and it is **not** a retreat — it names a level, and that level has established physics behind it (unitarity; environment-induced superselection; quantum Darwinism, now experimentally observed). See CLAIM-021 for sources.

## What it costs, and what the audit must now separate

The clarification forces one claim into two, which the framework's text currently runs together:

| | Claim | Level |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **(a)** | The information physically persists — it is not annihilated, and stable structure is what carries records forward. | quantum / physical |
| **(b)** | The failed path's information is **integrated into the surviving system** and is load-bearing for what follows. | functional / informational |

Canon uses **(b)**. Layer I.G's mechanism is the slime mold retracting protoplasm *back into the network*; Axiom 14 says failures are *load-bearing*; the Capsule says each cycle *integrates* all prior cycles. That is a claim about what the surviving system carries, not about what the universe still contains.

**(a) does not entail (b).** Decoherence spreads the record into environmental degrees of freedom: conserved globally, inaccessible to the system. The cell cannot read the discarded nucleotide. So the clarification secures (a) and leaves (b) where the measurements left it.

Two further consequences the author may want to weigh:

1. **(a) is established physics, not a framework result.** Grounding I.G on unitarity makes it a restatement of something already known — consistent with v5.4's *"zero novel predictions"*, but it means I.G stops being evidence *for* the framework.
2. **(a) inherits an open problem.** Canon's own Appendix D lists *Information Conservation [Black Hole Information Paradox]*, which the audit graded **proven-open** ([`../audit/constants/CONST-information-conservation.md`](../audit/constants/CONST-information-conservation.md)). If I.G rests on unitarity, I.G rests on something canon itself files as unresolved. That is honest, but it must be stated rather than presented as settled ground.

## Held open

- **What "structure stability" denotes precisely.** The audit's charitable reading is einselection — the environment-selected stable states that survive decoherence and get redundantly recorded. If that is the intended meaning, the framework is pointing at real physics with a real literature. If something else is meant, the author should say so, because the grading in CLAIM-021 depends on it.
- **Whether canon should keep asserting (b) at all,** or restate it as: *the discriminating structure is conserved; the discarded item is not retained by the system.*
