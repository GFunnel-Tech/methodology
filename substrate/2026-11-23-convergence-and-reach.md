## 1. Reach: bookkeeping vs dynamics

Every calculation in the Fold Ledger session was sorted by whether the canon could produce it from its own contents plus measured inputs.

| Group | What it covers | Canon can do it? | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **A. Bookkeeping** | balances, directions, what is conserved, how much is released, what is relative to what | **yes** — E = mc² and (mc²)² = E² − (pc)² (v5.4 §5), exact conservation (§3), node-relative readings (§4a) | D–T yield 17.6 MeV from untie/retie; the neutron's 80% share; the ratchet toward iron/nickel; ripple size ~10⁶ per layer deeper; Big Bang everywhere; expansion ≡ consolidation (uniform case) |
| **B. Dynamics** | rates, thresholds, distances | **no** — needs an equation of motion | Hawking temperature and lifetime; Peters inspiral; Chandrasekhar; relaxation times; Eddington/Bondi; Friedmann distances; Bekenstein–Hawking entropy |
| **C. Measurements** | observed values | not a question of theory | CMB temperature; GPS drift; neutron lifetime; Hulse–Taylor decay |

**Finding:** the canon is **level on balances and directions, behind on rates.** Every group-B row is a rate, threshold or distance; every group-A row is a balance or direction. That is the signature of the one input the canon names as missing — the **tension law** (Gap 2), the line's equation of motion.

## 2. Convergence: where the canon reached mainstream's answer by its own route

v5.1 counts convergence as evidence (*"the convergence itself constitutes evidence"*; *"two independent derivations, same structure"*). The v5.4 §6 Garlick Convergence guard requires the routes to be independent, and warns that a convergence may prove something more general than the model that found it (2/3 proves 3D space, not the trefoil).

| Result | Canon route independent? | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Leak permits the fall ↔ virial theorem / negative heat capacity | yes (canon §5 states the rule; virial theorem absent from canon) | **convergence** |
| Ratchet (v5.1 spiral, from process observation) ↔ nuclear binding curve | yes (different domains) | **convergence** |
| Consolidation, not expansion ↔ Wetterich frame (2013) | yes (user intuition) | **convergence** |
| Folds stretch the space between ↔ backreaction / timescape | yes (user intuition) | **convergence** |
| Fusion 17.6 MeV by untie/retie ↔ mass-difference Q-value | different route, same data and conservation law | cross-check only |
| Big Bang everywhere | no (adopted from cosmology) | not convergence |

**Four genuine convergences.** They show the shared structures are real. They do not show the line-and-knot picture is the right picture of them, and they make the canon **equal**, not ahead.

## 4. Verdict on "is mainstream ahead?"

Ahead on equations and on every confirmed prediction; the canon is level on open questions and on structure, reached partly by independent routes. The only route to *ahead* is a number mainstream lacks that later proves right. The neutrino prediction is the first such number written down; JUNO's ordering result is its first decisive test.
