### IG-01 — Is information conserved? — **already graded by the Reality Audit; this area adds eukaryotic evidence**

> **Correction (2026-10-03).** The first version of this entry called the point a new "candidate conflict" to route to the Reality Audit. That was an error. The audit had already graded it, on bacterial machinery, as finding **[F-067](../../../audit/FINDINGS.md)** / **[CLAIM-021](../../../audit/claims/CLAIM-021.md)**. The author clarified the claim's level on 2026-09-28 ([substrate](../../../substrate/2026-09-28-conservation-level.md)). That work reached this branch through the same merge as this area and was not loaded when the entry was written. The entry is re-formed below; nothing in the audit is changed.

**Where it stands (from the audit, not re-graded here).** The author clarified that "no information is discarded" is meant at the **quantum level**, via superposition and structure stability. The audit therefore split the claim in two:

| | Claim | Audit grade |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **(a)** | The information physically persists: it is not annihilated. | bounded / supported (unitarity, einselection, quantum Darwinism), with the record held **in the environment**, not in the system |
| **(b)** | The failed path's information is integrated into the surviving system and is load-bearing. | **forced-no** at molecular scale |

The audit's proposed restatement of (b): *the system conserves the **discriminating structure**, not the discarded item.*

**What this area adds: eukaryotic instances, bearing on (b) only.** Nothing here tests (a).
- **Yeast nuclear DNA.** Proofreading and mismatch repair discard ~160–1000-fold and ~65–250-fold of copying errors (N1, N2). The audit's bacterial result holds in a eukaryote.
- **Human genome.** About 8.2% is under purifying selection, and only 2.2% stayed constrained along both human and mouse lineages (N5). The sequence that is not maintained is not kept by the system.
- **Erasure built in.** Protein degradation, RNA decay and apoptosis (C1–C6).
- **Consistent with the audit's restatement.** The proofreading and repair machinery *is* the retained discriminating structure; the rejected nucleotides are not retained.

**What this area should not claim.** Its earlier wording said that information conservation "does not hold" without naming a level. Under the author's clarification, that sentence is true only of (b), at the level of the system.

**Open:** whether F-067's eukaryotic evidence should be logged in the audit itself (for example, as a sweep record) is the author's call.

### IG-02 — Filter at intake, or capture everything? — **held open**

Canon's *Gather* step (Shepherd's Way 03; Master Meta-Algorithm step 3) says capture without filtering. Its failure mode is filtering during gathering.

The cell does **both**, at different scales:
- **Sensing filters at intake.** Receptors are selective and switches have thresholds (A1).
- **Variation enters unfiltered.** Mutation is not screened in advance (F1); selection filters afterwards (F3). That matches *Gather* then filter.

**Gap:** canon has no rule for *which* intake should be unfiltered. A candidate reading, unchecked: intake that updates the long-term record (variation) is gathered unfiltered and selected later; intake that triggers action now (sensing) is filtered first.

### IG-03 — Overwrite vs. re-form — **held open (counter-instance)**

The container rule says *re-form, never overwrite; append, never delete*. The cell's record works the other way: repair restores the template and degradation removes the old state (B1, C1–C3). The only long-term traces are what persists in the genome (N6).

**Question for the author:** is "never overwrite" a rule for a *log of knowledge* that the cell simply does not need, or a correspondence that fails at cell scale? Not filled.

### IG-04 — Is the mutation rate an optimum? — **held open**

Canon (v5.1 Yin/Yang table) gives evolution's dynamic middle as *"Optimal mutation rate,"* between excess variation (chaos) and excess selection (stasis).

**Measured:** the human rate is 1.20 × 10⁻⁸ per nucleotide per generation (N3). That rate is the residue left after the fidelity pipeline (N1, N2). So the cell's *cleaning* machinery is what sets its *variation* rate (pattern P-06).

**Already in the audit:** [F-068](../../../audit/FINDINGS.md) found the same collision: an optimum over how much the cell discards, against Layer I.G's "nothing is discarded". It is held for the author there; this entry adds the eukaryotic numbers.

**Not shown:** that this rate is an optimum, as opposed to as low as the pipeline can reach. Settling it would need measurements across species that this run does not have, and **no outside theory is imported to fill it**.

### IG-07 — Structure areas vs. function areas — **solved by derivation, awaiting author**

The atlas began organized by structure (membrane, then nucleus, ER and so on). Information processing crosses every structure, and so would energy, building and division.

**Proposed:** two axes.
- **Structure areas** list *where* things happen.
- **Function areas** (information, energy, building, division) list *what* the cell does.
- Each L2 item links to both.

This area is the first function area.
