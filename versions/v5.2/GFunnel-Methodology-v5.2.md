## §1 · The Deep-Lens Protocol


The repeatable template. It is the Master Meta-Algorithm (the 12-step spine) with depth amplifiers attached at the steps that produce them. Run identically against every mechanism so resolution stays uniform.

Spine: DETECT → PROCESS → RESPOND — recursive. For each mechanism, produce, in order:


**A.** NAME & REFRAME — State the mechanism precisely; separate its surface appearance from its true function. The reframe is the first unit of depth.


**B.** STRUCTURE — Decompose into DETECT (what it captures), PROCESS (how it transforms), RESPOND (what it emits → next cycle's input). Every mechanism is a loop; name it.


**C.** PLACEMENT — Three Kingdoms (First/Second/Third); Universal Process Domain(s); operative Hermetic Principle, and which principle, when violated, produces the characteristic failure.


**D.** GRADIENT & DYNAMIC MIDDLE — The polarity it runs on (established/collapsed/absent); the Yang extreme (overextension) and Yin extreme (paralysis/numbing) and the middle that keeps both accessible.


**E.** FORCED vs. GUIDED — What it looks like run on the operator (guidance refused) vs. received in participation. Hold the asymmetry: "forced" is the residue of refused guidance, not an act of the maker (privation, not Manichaean symmetry).


**F.** KEYS & DOORS — DECODE / DROWN / NUMB — Decode (key held → signal/guidance), Drown (no key → forced noise), Numb (signal silenced → the deepest cell). State what the key is — the specific knowledge/discipline that converts this mechanism's noise into signal.


**G.** BOUNDARY CLASS — Scaffolding (provisional; outgrown by internalization, never smashed) vs. Constitutive (the laws that make the mechanism be itself; broken by force → noise/dissolution).


**H.** FAILURE MODE & REBUILD — The characteristic overstep (the inference/misapplication that converts the mechanism into a prison) and the built-in rebuild (collapse of the superposition → reform at higher baseline).


**I.** FALSIFIABILITY & VARIABLE LEDGER — Which held-open variable this run narrows (with the structure that narrows it); what stays open (named, not filled); what new variable it opens (per Gödel); one falsifiable prediction.


**J.** LEVERAGE — KNOWLEDGE → ACTION → DISCIPLINE — The operational translation. The act-vs-ruminate choice at human scale (forced = drown/numb; guided = decode/build). Reaffirm the irreducible: the choice is the operator's — the co-creator signature.


**K.** CORRESPONDENCE CHECK — Verify one scale up and one down. If it resists, the resistance becomes a named variable.


**L.** DOCUMENT & RESTART — Log to the ledger (§4); load as substrate; return to Step 1 at the new baseline.

Skip-step failures (inherited): no clear name (A) → unprocessable noise. No leverage (J) → no termination. No dynamic middle (D) → burnout or paralysis. No documentation (L) → the run dies with the operator.
