# Registers — Variables, Falsifiability, Iteration Ledger

The framework's honesty apparatus. This is what lets people and AI *verify* the methodology rather than take it on faith — and what makes iteration disciplined. Three registers:

1. **The Variable Registry** — every quantity the framework holds open rather than filling with assumption.
2. **The Falsifiability Register (Layer ◇)** — what would prove each claim wrong.
3. **The Iteration Ledger** — the running record of variable movements across versions.

---

## 1. The Variable Principle (the rule these registers enforce)

> *"Where measurement ends, create a variable. Hold it open. Map what IS measurable on either side. As the surrounding process is traced, the variable's range narrows. Sometimes it resolves by logical necessity. Sometimes it remains open. Never fill a variable with assumption. This document applies this standard to itself."* — Cameron Garlick

Every claim in the framework carries one of three statuses. Keep them distinct:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Measured / empirically settled** | e.g. process density, error-compounding math, sales conversion rates. |
| **Structurally derived** | e.g. co-creator identity, Three Kingdoms hierarchy, the dynamic middle. |
| **Held open** | Named, not filled. e.g. the hard problem; experiential-identity continuation across substrate dissolution. |

## 4. What the framework explicitly does *not* claim

Honesty is load-bearing. As of v5.3, the framework does **not** claim:

- that the Anti-Operation is a forced law (it is a **live hypothesis** with an attached falsifier);
- that the Container is the terminal meta-tier (that is **proven-open**);
- that the source / self-dual center is solved (it is **proven-unforceable** — sealing it would make the tier imperfect);
- that Layer ◇ must be physically renumbered (the functional re-tiering is forced; the presentation is a soft choice).

As of v5.4, the framework additionally does **not** claim:

- that lines are physical (LQG/string class — **live**), or that knots are particles (**live**; Skyrme partial);
- that the trefoil derives Koide (the form is derived; the partition identity is a **stipulation**);
- that the model predicts anything the Standard Model or General Relativity does not (**zero novel predictions** to date);
- that folds are an energy source (**forced-no**; folds store);
- that device D3 is buildable (two **proven-open** locks: W1 and macroscopic coherence);
- that the first boundary, the black-hole interior, sub-Planck geometry, the vacuum-density discrepancy, or the measurement problem are resolved.

See [v5.4 §11](../versions/v5.4/GFunnel-Methodology-v5.4.md) for the canonical statement of this list, and §12 for the per-item outcome classes.

Anyone — human or AI — using the framework should preserve these boundaries rather than quietly "completing" them.
