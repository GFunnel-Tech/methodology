## The Master Meta-Algorithm

The general engine. When no algorithm is pre-specified for your domain, run this to *derive* one — and label the result a derivation, not a primary algorithm.

**Read it:** [v5.1 → The Master Meta-Algorithm](../versions/v5.1/GFunnel-Methodology-v5.1.md#the-master-meta-algorithm).

## The invariant run form (applies to every algorithm)

1. Identify which algorithm to run.
2. Execute the numbered steps **in order** — no skipping, no inverting.
3. If a step cannot be completed, **stop**: the blocker is the location of the work.
4. **Document the run** (Layer VI Step 6).
5. Integrate what deviated (Layer I.G) — it becomes substrate for the next run.
