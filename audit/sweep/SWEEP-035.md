## Canon says

v5.1 Appendix A, Stage 25:

> "Multicellularity | Cells stop competing and start specializing. Apoptosis becomes governable. Refusal to participate becomes cancer."

Stage 25 sits after Stage 24 (Ion Gradients / Membrane Potentials) and before Stage 26 (Tissue Differentiation / Gene Expression). Stages 14–24 are the single-cell arc: LUCA, glycolysis, fermentation, photosynthesis, endosymbiosis, the Krebs cycle, the ETC, ATP synthase, ATP hydrolysis, ion gradients.

**Split (RUNBOOK rule 4).** *Factual core graded here:* specialisation among cells begins at multicellularity, i.e. after the single-cell arc of Stages 14–24 is complete.

*Framework mapping (not graded; see Narrative stripped):* "Cells stop competing"; "Apoptosis becomes governable"; "Refusal to participate becomes cancer."

Canon has no biofilm stage, no matrix, and no item for spatially differentiated bacterial communities anywhere this audit reached.

## Scientific Inquiry run

1. Question (precise): Does a canonical item hold bacterial biofilm formation — matrix, differentiation and spatial structure — and does DETECT → PROCESS → RESPOND fit the measured mechanism?
2. What an answer must look like: canon's nearest stage quoted with its position in the progression; measured matrix composition, measured spatial differentiation, and a direct test of whether that differentiation is functional; plus a DPR assignment tested for boundaries.
3. Falsifiability condition: canon's placement of specialisation at Stage 25 fails if genetically identical cells of a Stage-14 organism are measured to differentiate into functionally distinct, spatially organised layers whose distinctness is necessary for the structure to survive. That is precisely what the promoter-replacement experiment demonstrates.
4. Variables: measurable / bounded / held open: **Measurable** — matrix composition, layer thicknesses, the consequence of abolishing stratification, electrical coordination, tolerance factor. **Bounded** — upper layer ~60 µm in *E. coli*, ~200 µm total in *B. subtilis*. **Held open** — whether competition ceases.
5. Test designed: find a measured case of differentiation in a bacterium, and specifically a study that *perturbs* the differentiation, because canon's claim is about specialisation being a property of a later stage — so what matters is whether bacterial specialisation is real and load-bearing, not merely observed.
6. Data (unfiltered): the table above. The decisive result is the negative one: forcing uniform matrix production destroyed the biofilm. Differentiation in *E. coli* is not a by-product of a nutrient gradient — that was the paper's own alternative hypothesis, and it was tested and rejected.
7. Variable Principle applied: canon's Stage 25 fills the variable "when does cellular specialisation begin" with a position in a numbered list. Measurement shows specialisation in an organism canon places at Stage 14, so the variable is not closed at 25. Canon's further clause ("cells stop competing") is left open rather than assumed to fail — the stratification result does not bear on it.
8. Model update (Capsule: what the failed parts contribute): canon needs an item for differentiated bacterial communities, and it cannot be inserted as a stage between 24 and 25 without conceding that the progression's numbers are not a dependency order — the biofilm organism has all of Stages 14–24 *and* the specialisation of Stage 25. The failed part contributes the same structural finding as SWEEP-025 and SWEEP-029, now from a third direction: Appendix A can express sequence, but not concurrency, branching, or constraint. Three independent components of one organism have now failed to fit for the same reason, which makes it a property of the form rather than of the entries.
9. Documented: this file. Attempted DPR assignment: **DETECT** → nutrient depletion in the upper layer, sensed through the RpoS sigma-factor pathway; **PROCESS** → CsgD-dependent transcription of curli and cellulose genes; **RESPOND** → matrix deposition and the resulting architecture. This assignment is real *at the single-cell level* — it is an instance of the SWEEP-033 signal-transduction pattern, with a named sigma factor, a named transcription factor and a measured output, and the promoter-replacement experiment is precisely a cut between PROCESS and its input. But it fails at the level the component actually is. The biofilm's architecture is a property of *two populations in a spatial relation*, and neither population detects, processes or responds to the architecture: the bottom layer's mechanical role (flexibly filling space under buckling regions) is a consequence of *not* expressing something, and no cell measures the colony's shape. The measured community-level behaviour that comes closest to information flow — potassium wavefronts — is modelled as fire-diffuse-fire propagation, which has no detector either. **DPR mapping: fits for the single-cell regulatory step it contains; does not fit the component itself.** Canon's Stage 25, which is about the community level, is the level at which the fit fails.
10. Next baseline: a measurement bearing on whether competition within a biofilm ceases, which would close the one held-open row. Re-review 2027-09-24.

## Forcing Test

- Test A (Ground): Grounded, including a perturbation experiment that tests the functional claim rather than only observing the pattern.
- Test B (Uniqueness): Canon's placement is not unique — the same facts fit a reading in which specialisation is available to any lineage with cell–cell adhesion and a stationary-phase regulator, which is most of the bacterial world.
- Test C (Direction): Measured and signed: abolish the asymmetry and the structure fails. Canon predicts no direction here, because it does not hold the component.
- Test D (Falsifiability): Falsifiable. The placement of specialisation at Stage 25 fails.
- **Outcome:** `stipulation` for the stage placement — a presentation choice, not a forced result — with `forced-no` for the specific factual reading that cellular specialisation begins at multicellularity. Graded `conflict` on the factual core.

## Anti-Operation (the gap this opens)

The functional stratification result opens a gap in Layer I.G (the Capsule / Slime Mold Integration Principle), which holds that "every path explored is integrated; inefficient paths redistribute their information. Nothing is lost." Here the *less* differentiated configuration — every cell doing the productive thing — is the one that fails, and the cells that contribute by *not* producing matrix are the ones that hold the colony together. Canon has no item for a division of labour in which one party's contribution is abstention. Naming it would require distinguishing an inefficient path from a necessary complement, and neither Layer I.G nor Layer III+ supplies that distinction.

## Narrative stripped (if any)

Removed: "Cells stop competing and start specializing" — the second half is measured in bacteria and the first half is unmeasured (recorded as held-open), so the two clauses cannot travel together as one claim. Removed: "Apoptosis becomes governable. Refusal to participate becomes cancer." Neither has a bacterial referent (see SWEEP-034), and in the biofilm the cells that "refuse to participate" in matrix production are the ones the structure depends on — the analogy inverts against the measurement.
