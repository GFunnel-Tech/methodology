## Scientific Inquiry run

1. Question (precise): Does a canonical item hold the measured physical compaction and organisation of the bacterial chromosome, and does DETECT → PROCESS → RESPOND fit the measured mechanism?
2. What an answer must look like: canon's nearest wording; a measured compaction ratio and nucleoid volume fraction; the measured mechanism that holds it compact; and a DPR assignment tested for boundaries.
3. Falsifiability condition: the `methodology-gap` grade fails if a canonical item names physical chromosome compaction or segregation. Domain 9's "compressed" is explicitly informational ("Second Kingdom compressed into First Kingdom form"), and no stage names segregation, so the grade stands.
4. Variables: measurable / bounded / held open: **Measurable** — macromolecule concentration, nucleoid volume fraction, contact maps, per-protein copy numbers. **Bounded** — compaction ~10³ in linear dimension; crowding 0.3–0.4 g/ml. **Held open** — canon's composite complexity metric; a total-protein-per-µm³ figure from a single primary source.
5. Test designed: find a measured compaction quantity and a measured crowding quantity, because the audit's `CLAIM-003` recorded that Layer III's density claim is unfalsifiable for lack of any metric. Anything measured and per-volume is therefore worth recording here.
6. Data (unfiltered): the table above. The result most useful to the audit is also the one least convenient for canon: the dominant organiser of the nucleoid is measured to be *macromolecular crowding* — a purely physical, non-informational, non-regulated effect.
7. Variable Principle applied: canon's "compressed" is used in one sense (information) where measurement supplies another (physical volume). These are two variables. Treating canon's word as covering the measured phenomenon would fill the physical variable with an informational assumption. **Special attention (a):** two measured per-volume quantities for a bacterium are recorded — macromolecule concentration 0.3–0.4 g/ml, and nucleoid occupancy ~50% of cytosolic volume. Neither is canon's metric, and neither is offered as a substitute; they are offered as the raw material a definition would have to use.
8. Model update (Capsule: what the failed parts contribute): Domain 9 should distinguish informational compression (which it has) from physical compaction (which it lacks), and canon needs an item for chromosome segregation. The failed part contributes the first measured numbers this audit has been able to attach to a per-volume density claim about a bacterium — and they point the other way from canon's reading, because 0.3–0.4 g/ml is a *crowding* figure, and crowding organises the cell by excluded volume, not by organised interdependence.
9. Documented: this file. Attempted DPR assignment: **DETECT** → nothing; **PROCESS** → entropic and excluded-volume forces plus supercoiling and nucleoid-associated protein binding; **RESPOND** → the compacted, phase-separated nucleoid. This is the clearest negative case in the sweep. Phase separation driven by crowding is a thermodynamic equilibrium property of a concentrated polymer solution. There is no detector, no signal, no set-point and no response — the state simply *is* the free-energy minimum given the concentrations. Assigning DPR here is not merely arbitrary, it misdescribes the mechanism by implying an information flow that measurement places nowhere. **DPR mapping: does not fit.**
10. Next baseline: a definition of canon's metric that can be computed from the rows above. Re-review 2027-09-24.

## Forcing Test

- Test A (Ground): Grounded for crowding concentration and per-protein copy numbers; the compaction ratio and nucleoid volume fraction are grounded as review-level syntheses, graded `derived`.
- Test B (Uniqueness): Canon's informational reading of "compressed" is not unique and does not cover the measured physical phenomenon; crowding is uniquely identified by the cited work as the dominant organiser.
- Test C (Direction): Measured: raise crowding (osmotic shock), the nucleoid compacts. A signed, reproducible perturbation–response relation with no regulatory step in it.
- Test D (Falsifiability): Falsifiable. The gap claim is falsifiable by producing a canonical item; canon's own Layer III density claim remains unfalsifiable for want of a metric, per `CLAIM-003`.
- **Outcome:** `live-hypothesis`. Canon's informational claim is sound but does not reach this component; its per-volume density claim now has candidate measurables and still no definition.

## Anti-Operation (the gap this opens)

If the most prominent organisational principle of the bacterial cytosol is measured to be crowding, then canon's Layer III ranking is in a worse position than `CLAIM-003` recorded. The metric was missing; now the leading candidate for a per-volume quantity — macromolecule concentration — is one on which a dried protein pellet, a crystal, or an oil droplet would beat a cell. Any metric canon adopts must therefore distinguish *concentration* from *organisation*, and canon has no measured definition of organisation. That is the gap this record opens and cannot close.

## Narrative stripped (if any)

Removed: "Second Kingdom compressed into First Kingdom form" — a Three-Kingdoms mapping reality neither forces nor contradicts. Removed: "DNA (Masculine — blueprint) + ribosomes (Feminine — manifestation machinery)" (Layer III Gender row). Beyond being an interpretation, it is contradicted in detail by the measured nucleoid: the nucleoid is defined by ribosome *depletion*, so the two elements canon pairs are measurably spatially segregated within the same cell, and the ribosome-rich phase is what does the compacting.
