v5.1 Appendix A, Stage 26: "Tissue Differentiation / Gene Expression | Same DNA, different expression. Second Kingdom directs First Kingdom according to Third Kingdom regulatory context."

v5.1 Layer VI (Shepherd's Way) supplies canon's only ordering discipline: a 7-step operating cycle, "if it isn't written, it doesn't exist as a process."

Canon names no assembly order, no secretion system, and no checkpoint that gates transcription on the state of a structure.

**Split.** *Factual core:* gene expression runs signal → transcription/translation → protein. *Framework mapping:* DNA as "Second Kingdom compressed into First Kingdom form"; regulatory context as Third Kingdom.

## Scientific Inquiry run
1. Question (precise): Does canon's gene-expression item hold flagellar assembly order and its type III export machinery, and does DETECT → PROCESS → RESPOND fit an assembly process whose *structure* gates its own transcription?
2. What an answer must look like: the measured ordering mechanism, the direction of the coupling between structure and transcription, and an explicit DPR assignment with the boundary question answered.
3. Falsifiability condition: canon's "signal → transcription/translation → expressed protein" fails as a complete account if transcription is measurably gated by the completion state of the protein structure it produces, i.e. if the arrow also runs backwards within one build.
4. Variables: measurable / bounded / held open: measurable — gene and operon counts, the three-class cascade, FlgM secretion coupling, growth rates, channel diameter, subunit count. Bounded — the growth-rate law. Held open — the length dependence of growth, where two measurements disagree.
5. Test designed: fetch the regulon transcription analysis, the assembly-coordination review, and the two independent filament-growth measurements; compare the direction of coupling against canon's arrow.
6. Data (unfiltered): ~50 genes in ≥13 operons, three transcription classes, cascade order paralleling assembly order. FlgM, the anti-sigma factor that holds back class III, is removed by *secretion through the structure* once the hook-basal body is complete. The filament reaches up to 20,000 subunits, threaded through a 2 nm channel, exported using pmf, initially at ~1,700 amino acids per second. E. coli growth is independent of filament length; Salmonella growth decreases with length; the two disagree and the disagreement is recorded as held open. Broken filaments regrow.
7. Variable Principle applied: **Measured** — counts, classes, coupling, rates, channel diameter. **Structurally derived** — none load-bearing. **Held open** — the length-dependence law. The DPR mapping: DETECT = arrival of FlgM at the export channel, i.e. the cell detecting *its own structural completeness*; PROCESS = the sigma-28/FlgM balance setting class III transcription; RESPOND = expression of filament genes and export of flagellin. Each of these is a distinguishable, measurable event, so the boundaries are real. What does *not* fit is canon's arrow: the stated universal is "signal → transcription → protein", a one-way pipeline, whereas the measured system closes the loop *inside a single build* — the protein structure is the sensor for its own transcription. Canon's Layer 0 does say "the response becomes the next cycle's input", so the loop is asserted at the level of the algorithm; the gap is that Domain 9's gene-expression item states the pipeline without the return path, and canon has no item at all for an assembly checkpoint.
8. Model update (Capsule: what the failed parts contribute): the DNA-as-base-code row should read "signal → transcription/translation → expressed protein, with the state of the assembled product available as a further signal (secretion-gated checkpoint)". The failed one-way reading contributes the sharpest available instance of canon's own Layer 0 loop claim — a cell measuring its own construction progress with no external observer. That is stronger evidence for Layer 0's loop than any example canon currently gives, and canon does not use it.
9. Documented: this file.
10. Next baseline: canon's arrow is incomplete rather than wrong; the checkpoint is unmapped. Held open: the length dependence of filament growth, pending a study that reconciles the E. coli and Salmonella results.

## Forcing Test
- Test A (Ground): grounded for the cascade and the checkpoint; the growth-rate law is grounded twice and inconsistently, and is recorded `held-open`.
- Test B (Uniqueness): the existence of a structural checkpoint is forced by FlgM secretion. The assignment of DETECT to FlgM export rather than to the hook-basal body's completion is not forced — they are the same event described at two levels.
- Test C (Direction): canon's arrow is one-way; the measurement shows a return path within one build. Directions differ, and the measurement's direction is the one canon's own Layer 0 asserts.
- Test D (Falsifiability): the condition at step 3 was met — transcription is gated by structural completion.
- **Outcome:** `stipulation`. Canon has no item for assembly order, secretion-coupled checkpoints, or type III export; the nearest item states a pipeline that measurement shows to be a loop. The DPR mapping fits at real boundaries but only after canon's own arrow is corrected. `methodology-gap`, diagnosis `not-reached`.

## Anti-Operation (the gap this opens)

- The cell detects its own structural state with no dedicated sensor: the sensor *is* the unfinished structure. Canon has no category for detection without a detector, and Layer I.F (observation as intake organ) presumes an organ.
- Two independent measurements disagree about whether growth rate depends on length. The audit has no procedure for a reality-side conflict between two measurements, only for reality versus canon. That procedure is missing from SCHEMA.md.
- Canon's Layer VI says "if it isn't written, it doesn't exist as a process". Here the order *is* written — in the operon structure — and the writing is what enforces the order. Whether that is a correspondence or a coincidence is untested, and canon offers no test.

## Narrative stripped (if any)

- "DNA is Second Kingdom compressed into First Kingdom form" and "Third Kingdom regulatory context": the measured regulator is an anti-sigma factor whose concentration is set by whether a hole in a structure is open. No observation forces or forbids the Kingdom labels; they add no measurable content here.
- "Gene expression is *the* universal algorithm at molecular level": the word universal is stripped. Flagellar assembly runs on gene expression *plus* a post-transcriptional secretion checkpoint plus self-assembly at a distal tip with no energy source at the growing end.
