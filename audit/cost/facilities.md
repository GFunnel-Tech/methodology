Machine-readable version: [`facilities.json`](facilities.json). Figures carry their own `source_url` / `source_type` when they differ from the facility's main source.

Conventions: amounts as stated by the source; **price year** is shown only when the source states it (otherwise `—` = not stated, usually then-year/nominal). "Primary" = agency, audit office, operator or collaboration document; "secondary" = press / encyclopedia.

## Held open

- **COBE actual cost** — only the $30M approval *cap* (secondary) was fetchable; NASA press kit, APPEL case study, NASA Historical Data Books V and VIII give no COBE total. A "$160M" figure seen only in search-engine summaries was not confirmed on any fetched page and is not recorded.
- **WMAP actual/final cost** — only a secondary $150M *proposed* budget; no primary NASA figure found.
- **Hubble lifetime incl. shuttle servicing** — NASA's $16B (2021$) excludes shuttle operations; only SM3A ($205M) and the SM4 estimate ($1.7–2.4B) are sourced per-mission.
- **CMS individual cost** — CMS bulletin failed TLS / returned 503; only a shared 475 MCHF ceiling and ~80 MCHF magnet figure found.
- **LIGO total NSF investment (~$1.4B often cited)** — Caltech fact sheet failed TLS / 503. **Virgo construction cost** — not found (only 2023 operating budget, €11.5M, secondary).
- **KamLAND-Zen** — no cost found. **Borexino** — no cost found.
- **ISS current cumulative total** — latest fetched OIG total is through 2014 (U.S. only).
- **NIST FY2025/FY2026 enacted** — not found on fetched nist.gov pages; FY2024 recorded.
- **Planck** — whether national instrument funding is inside ESA's €700M is not stated.

## Where sources disagree

- **Super-Kamiokande:** 8.7 billion yen (≈$62M) — CERN Courier 1991 via OSTI — vs ≈$100M — Wikipedia.
- **Cassini:** $3.9B (NASA, full life incl. partners) vs $3B (JPL, 2004) vs $3.26B (Wikipedia). Mostly scope/date differences.
- **LHC construction:** 4,332 MCHF materials (CERN's own table) vs ≈$4.75B (Forbes via IBTimes) vs "about 3000 million Euro" machine only (lhc-closer.es). The secondary numbers are not CERN figures.
- **Hubble:** $1.2B at completion (OIG, then-year) vs $16B lifetime (NASA, 2021$). Both are correct; they cover different scope.
- **ISS:** $75B through FY2013 (IG-14-031) vs $78B through 2014 (IG-15-021). Consistent; the difference is the extra year.
- **Human Genome Project:** $2.7B (FY1991$, genome.gov) vs derived $3.72B then-year sum of DOE/ORNL annual table FY1990–2003. The ORNL table covers broader genomics activity.
