## Scientific Inquiry run
1. Question (precise): (a) Does Bostrom derive that reality is computational? (b) Is Shannon entropy an "unmeasured variable", and does Shannon's formula quantify the Variable Principle? (c) Is the mapping of the three sub-domains onto DETECT → PROCESS → RESPOND forced?
2. What an answer must look like: (a) Bostrom's own statement of his conclusion; (b) Shannon's definition and scope; (c) a named observation that would count as non-mapping for each sub-domain.
3. Falsifiability condition: (a) canon's claim fails if Bostrom's conclusion is a disjunction rather than a derivation that we are simulated; (b) fails if Shannon entropy is a computed property of a known distribution rather than an unmeasured quantity, or if Shannon excludes meaning; (c) canon names no failing observation.
4. Variables: measurable / bounded / held open: measurable: the texts of Bostrom and Shannon. Bounded: whether reality is computational (Bostrom leaves it as one horn of three). Held open: social-conditioning claims (no measure named).
5. Test designed: Fetch Bostrom's abstract from the author's site; extract Shannon's definition and scope statement from the 1948 paper; fetch a survey of the philosophy of information.
6. Data (unfiltered): Bostrom: a trilemma, "at least one" of three propositions is true; the simulation horn is one of three. Shannon: H = −Σ pᵢ log pᵢ measures information, choice and uncertainty; semantic aspects are "irrelevant to the engineering problem". SEP: Shannon information does not capture meaning.
7. Variable Principle applied: (a) Canon's "Bostrom derives it from probability theory" contradicts Bostrom's text: he derives a disjunction, not the conclusion that reality is computational, so there is no "same structural conclusion" to converge with. (b) Shannon entropy is not an unmeasured variable: it is computed exactly from a specified probability distribution. What is unknown is *which message* will be selected, not the entropy. Shannon explicitly excludes meaning, so it cannot quantify "exactly" a principle about meaning-laden assumptions. The weaker reading ("entropy quantifies uncertainty over outcomes; receipt of a message reduces it") is consistent. (c) Held open for 4b.
8. Model update (Capsule: what the failed parts contribute): 4a should state Bostrom's result as a trilemma, with the simulation horn one of three open options. The "Two independent derivations. Same structure." claim loses its first derivation. 4c should say "Shannon entropy is a computed measure of uncertainty over a known set of possible messages", and drop "exactly".
9. Documented: This file.
10. Next baseline: 4a: canon's description of Bostrom forced-no; the simulation hypothesis stays held open. 4c: accounted in the weak reading. 4b: unobserved, open.

## Forcing Test
- Test A (Ground): Bostrom's text and Shannon's text are the ground. Canon's 4a sentence does not match its source.
- Test B (Uniqueness): Bostrom's argument leaves three candidates standing; canon selects one. Selecting one of three without new evidence fills a variable with assumption.
- Test C (Direction): Canon runs from Mentalism to Bostrom (reads Bostrom as confirming Mentalism). Bostrom's paper does not run that way.
- Test D (Falsifiability): The 4a claim was falsifiable against the source and is falsified. The mapping claim for all three sub-domains names no failing observation.
- **Outcome:** forced-no for "Bostrom derives [that reality is computational]" and for "Two independent derivations. Same structure." (the source states a trilemma). 4c: accounted in the weak form. 4b: unobserved-claim. Mapping: stipulation. Intake is `open`, not `adjusted`, because the contradicting evidence is a single primary document (graded measured-single); the proposed adjustment is given in step 8.
