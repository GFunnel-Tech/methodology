## Canon says

v5.1 Layer I.C, Domain 6:

- *Three Kingdoms:* "Third (intended meaning) → Second (encoded language) → First (sound waves or text) → Second (decoded) → Third (integrated understanding). Five-step chain. Failure possible at every link."
- *Algorithm:* "DETECT: Communicative intent. PROCESS: Encoding → transmission → decoding through receiver's filters. RESPOND: Updated shared understanding, or named misalignment."
- *The Variable in Every Word:* "every word is a variable. No two humans share an identical internal definition for any word."
- *Language as 2nd Kingdom Architecture:* "Every language encodes a worldview in its structure (Sapir-Whorf). Second Kingdom linguistic structures constrain Third Kingdom expression."
- *Propaganda as Algorithm:* "The same linguistic process that transmits truth transmits untruth at identical structural fidelity."

**Split.** *Factual core:* communication runs intent → encoding → physical transmission → decoding → understanding, with failure possible at each link; word meanings differ between speakers; every language encodes a worldview and constrains thought (Sapir-Whorf); the channel is indifferent to truth. *Framework mapping:* the Kingdom assignments, "every word is a variable", Vibration / rapport step, business equivalent.

## Scientific Inquiry run
1. Question (precise): (a) Does the five-step communication chain match the primary model of communication? (b) Does every language encode a worldview that constrains thought (Sapir-Whorf as canon states it)? (c) Is the DETECT → PROCESS → RESPOND mapping forced?
2. What an answer must look like: (a) the primary source model; (b) cross-linguistic experiments on language effects on perception or thought, and the status of Whorf's original claims; (c) a named observation that would count as non-mapping.
3. Falsifiability condition: (b) canon's strong reading fails if cross-linguistic universals exist in the domains Whorf claimed and if measured effects are small, task-bound, and removable by verbal interference. (c) canon names no failing observation.
4. Variables: measurable / bounded / held open: measurable: colour discrimination times, colour-term inventories, tense marking. Bounded: language effects exist but are modest and online. Held open: "internal definition" identity across speakers; whether every language encodes a worldview (undefined "worldview").
5. Test designed: Extract Shannon's system description; fetch Winawer et al. 2007; fetch SEP's summary of the empirical record on linguistic relativity.
6. Data (unfiltered): Shannon: source → transmitter → channel → receiver → destination; meaning excluded from the engineering problem. Winawer 2007: a measurable, language-specific category advantage in colour discrimination, abolished by verbal interference. SEP: evidence for universal colour terms; Hopi has tense contrary to Whorf; modern relativity claims are much more modest.
7. Variable Principle applied: (a) The five-step chain is consistent with Shannon's five-part system; the mapping of Shannon's engineering chain onto *meaning* goes beyond Shannon, who excludes semantics. (b) "Every language encodes a worldview ... constrain[s] ... expression" in the strong sense conflicts with the record (universal colour terms, Hopi tense). A weak form survives: language categories measurably affect some perceptual tasks, online and removably. (c) Held open.
8. Model update (Capsule: what the failed parts contribute): Replace "Every language encodes a worldview in its structure (Sapir-Whorf). ... constrain Third Kingdom expression" with "Language categories measurably influence some perception and memory tasks (weak linguistic relativity); the strong form, that language determines worldview, is not supported." Mark "No two humans share an identical internal definition" as an untestable claim or give it a measure.
9. Documented: This file.
10. Next baseline: Communication chain: accounted (engineering sense). Strong Sapir-Whorf: conflict. Weak relativity: live hypothesis with measured support.

## Forcing Test
- Test A (Ground): The chain has a primary-source ground (Shannon). The strong Sapir-Whorf clause does not; the weak form does (Winawer).
- Test B (Uniqueness): Several readings of "constrain" survive (weak, online influence); canon's text reads as the strong one. Not unique.
- Test C (Direction): Canon cites Sapir-Whorf as settled and builds on it (Second Kingdom constrains Third). The evidence runs toward universals plus modest local effects.
- Test D (Falsifiability): The weak form is falsifiable (category-advantage effects absent under controlled tasks) and survives so far. The mapping names no failing observation.
- **Outcome:** live-hypothesis for weak linguistic relativity (falsifier: category-advantage effects absent or not removable by verbal interference in replications). The strong clause as written is in conflict; intake `open` because the fetched evidence is one primary experiment plus a secondary survey. Mapping: stipulation.

## Anti-Operation (the gap this opens)

- Shannon's model excludes meaning. Canon's chain runs *meaning* (Third Kingdom) through Shannon's structure. What measures fidelity of meaning, as opposed to fidelity of signal? Open.
- "Every word is a variable" is a methodological stance, not a measured claim; canon states it as fact.
- If the channel carries truth and untruth "at identical structural fidelity", the chain cannot itself distinguish them. Where in the algorithm does truth-checking happen? The Domain 6 Algorithm has no such step.

## Narrative stripped (if any)

- **Kingdom assignments (meaning = Third, language = Second, sound = First)**: classification choice; reality neither forces nor contradicts it.
- **"Per Vibration: frequency match precedes content transmission"** (Algorithm step 7): framework interpretation of rapport; no measurement.
- **Mapping verdict: stipulation, with genuine structural fit.** Of the ten domains, this one fits best: communication has a real, primary-sourced encoder → channel → decoder structure. The fit is real, but it does not test the universal claim, because canon names no observation that would count as failure. (The DETECT slot is also the weakest here: "communicative intent" is the *source*, not a detection.)
