## Canon says

v5.1 Layer I.C, Domain 9:

- *Algorithm:* "DETECT: Internal deviation or external threat. PROCESS: Cellular response — gene expression, protein synthesis, metabolic shift, immune activation. RESPOND: Adapted state, corrected equilibrium."
- *Homeostasis:* "The organism continuously monitors deviation, processes the signal through cascading regulatory pathways, and responds to correct. ... The organism that achieves stasis is dead."
- *Immune Process:* "DETECT: Foreign antigen. PROCESS: Recognition, amplification, clonal expansion, targeted antibody production. RESPOND: Pathogen elimination + memory cell formation."
- *Apoptosis:* "Cells that have served their function self-terminate. A cell that refuses apoptosis becomes cancer."
- *DNA as Base Code:* "Gene expression is the universal algorithm at molecular level: signal → transcription/translation → expressed protein."
- *Yin/Yang:* "Sympathetic (Yang) vs. parasympathetic (Yin)."
- Domain 9 Algorithm step 7: "Per HRV (Heart Rate Variability): high variability indicates capacity for full Yang and full Yin. Low variability indicates collapsed dynamic middle."
- *Three Kingdoms:* "Third (homeostatic regulatory intelligence) → Second (genetic code, signaling pathways, immune memory) → First (cellular machinery ...)."

**Split.** *Factual core:* homeostatic feedback; adaptive immune response with memory; apoptosis as programmed cell death; failure of apoptosis produces cancer; HRV as an index of autonomic flexibility. *Framework mapping:* Kingdom ordering ("regulatory intelligence" as Third Kingdom), Garlick Equilibrium, Shepherd's Way steps, Yin/Yang assignment.

## Scientific Inquiry run
1. Question (precise): Are canon's biological statements (homeostasis, immune memory, apoptosis → cancer, HRV) consistent with primary literature, and is the DETECT → PROCESS → RESPOND mapping forced?
2. What an answer must look like: primary or review evidence for each statement; for "refuses apoptosis becomes cancer", evidence on whether apoptosis failure is sufficient for cancer; a named observation that would count as non-mapping.
3. Falsifiability condition: "A cell that refuses apoptosis becomes cancer" (read as sufficient) fails if cancer requires further acquired capabilities beyond resisting cell death, or if cancer cells still undergo apoptosis.
4. Variables: measurable / bounded / held open: measurable: apoptosis in tissues and tumours; hallmark capabilities; immune memory; HRV indices. Bounded: mechanism of long-term immune memory (debated). Held open: "regulatory intelligence" as a Third Kingdom entity; HRV Task Force content (not fetched).
5. Test designed: Fetch Kerr et al. 1972 (apoptosis defined), Hanahan & Weinberg 2011 (hallmarks), Ahmed & Gray 1996 (immune memory), Shaffer & Ginsberg 2017 (HRV).
6. Data (unfiltered): Apoptosis occurs spontaneously in untreated malignant tumours (Kerr 1972). Tumours require six acquired capabilities in multistep development; resisting cell death is one (Hanahan & Weinberg 2011). Immune memory is real, its maintenance mechanism debated. HRV reflects adaptive flexibility; norms are context-dependent.
7. Variable Principle applied: Homeostasis, immune sequence, gene expression: consistent in outline. Apoptosis: "A cell that refuses apoptosis becomes cancer" overstates. Resisting cell death is one of several capabilities a tumour acquires, and tumour cells still undergo apoptosis. Evasion of apoptosis is a contributing hallmark, not a sufficient cause. HRV: "high variability indicates capacity for full Yang and full Yin" is a framework reading of an index whose norms depend on context; "higher is better" without context is not what the review states. Held open, not graded false.
8. Model update (Capsule: what the failed parts contribute): Replace "A cell that refuses apoptosis becomes cancer" with "Resisting cell death is one of several capabilities cells acquire on the way to cancer (Hanahan & Weinberg 2011)." Add to Algorithm step 7 that HRV norms depend on recording length, age, and sex.
9. Documented: This file.
10. Next baseline: Apoptosis sentence: conflict (proposed wording). Immune memory: accounted, mechanism open. HRV: framework reading held open. Homeostasis: accounted in outline (primary homeostasis source not fetched this run).

## Forcing Test
- Test A (Ground): Primary histology (Kerr 1972) and a major synthesis (Hanahan & Weinberg 2011) ground the apoptosis correction.
- Test B (Uniqueness): Several capabilities are required; apoptosis resistance alone is not the unique route to cancer.
- Test C (Direction): Canon runs "refuse apoptosis → cancer" as sufficient; the data run "cancer → has acquired, among others, resistance to cell death".
- Test D (Falsifiability): The sufficiency reading was falsifiable and fails.
- **Outcome:** forced-no for "A cell that refuses apoptosis becomes cancer" read as a sufficient condition. Intake `open`, not `adjusted`: the contradicting rows are a single histological report (measured-single) and a review synthesis (derived). Mapping: stipulation.

## Anti-Operation (the gap this opens)

- "The organizational equivalent requires no further elaboration" (Registry, Apoptosis row): the biological premise is weaker than stated, so the organizational inference needs elaboration.
- HRV is used as a measure of the "dynamic middle". Which HRV index, over what recording length, and against which norm? Open.
- Biology is the domain where DETECT has the clearest physical referent (receptors, sensors). This makes biology the model the mapping was built from; it does not show the mapping generalizes.

## Narrative stripped (if any)

- **"Third (homeostatic regulatory intelligence)"**: "intelligence" imports agency into feedback regulation. Reality does not force it.
- **"Memory formation = Shepherd's Way Steps 06–07 at biological level"**, **"Homeostasis as Garlick Equilibrium"**: framework analogies.
- **Sympathetic = Yang, parasympathetic = Yin**: a labeling choice.
- **Mapping verdict: stipulation, with genuine fit.** Receptor → signalling cascade → effector is the standard description of biological regulation, and here "detect" has a literal referent (receptors, sensors). The fit is real. It still does not test universality, and canon names no observation that would count as failure to map.
