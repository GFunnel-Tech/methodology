## Canon says

v5.1 Layer I.C, Domain 10:

- *Three Kingdoms:* "First Kingdom variation filtered by Second Kingdom selection pressure toward progressive Third Kingdom expression."
- *Algorithm:* "DETECT: Variation in population. PROCESS: Environmental selection — differential reproduction. RESPOND: Selected traits increase in frequency → next generation's baseline."
- *Convergent Evolution:* "Unrelated lineages independently evolving the same structural solution: eyes evolved 40+ times. Per Correspondence: identical inputs + environment processed through the universal algorithm produce identical outputs."
- *Punctuated Equilibrium:* "Long stability interrupted by periods of rapid change. Cambrian explosion followed Snowball Earth. Mammalian radiation followed K-Pg extinction. Maximum contraction enabled maximum subsequent expansion."
- *Evolution of Consciousness:* "the trajectory from single-celled to multicellular to nervous systems to language is the progressive increase of Process Density culminating in consciousness recognizing itself."
- Domain 10 Algorithm step 3: "identical inputs + environment produce identical outputs across substrates."

**Split.** *Factual core:* variation + differential reproduction → frequency change; eyes evolved independently 40+ times; identical inputs give identical outputs; punctuated equilibrium; the Cambrian explosion followed Snowball Earth; the mammalian radiation followed the K-Pg extinction. *Unobserved:* evolution is progressive toward "Third Kingdom expression" / consciousness recognizing itself. *Framework mapping:* Kingdoms, Correspondence, Yin/Yang, Process Density.

## Scientific Inquiry run
1. Question (precise): Are canon's evolutionary facts (eye origins, "identical outputs", punctuated equilibrium, Cambrian after Snowball Earth, mammalian radiation after K-Pg) consistent with primary literature, is evolution progressive toward consciousness, and is the DETECT → PROCESS → RESPOND mapping forced?
2. What an answer must look like: published estimates of independent eye origins; evidence on whether convergent outputs are identical; the originators' definition of punctuated equilibrium; radiometric ages for the events; a measure of "progress"; a named observation that would count as non-mapping.
3. Falsifiability condition: "identical inputs + environment produce identical outputs" fails if convergent solutions differ in structure or components. "Punctuated equilibrium" as canon uses it fails if the originators define it as a species-level pattern, not mass-extinction rebounds. "Followed" claims fail if the intervals are large enough that "followed" carries no causal content.
4. Variables: measurable / bounded / held open: measurable: ages (635 Ma, ~544 Ma, K-Pg synchrony); eye-origin estimates (40–60+). Bounded: independence of eye origins (debated: Pax6 shared control vs co-option). Held open: placental timing (contested); directionality toward consciousness.
5. Test designed: Fetch eye-evolution reviews and the Pax6/eyeless experiment; Gould & Eldredge 1993; radiometric ages for the Marinoan end, the Cambrian base, and K-Pg; O'Leary 2013 and its technical comment.
6. Data (unfiltered): Eyes: at least 40–60 origins, debated; shared Pax6 control; only a handful of eye types; lens proteins arose separately and are diverse. PE: stasis within species histories and species-level sorting within clades. Marinoan end 635 Ma; Cambrian base ~544 Ma (1993; later revised, not fetched); interval ≈ 91 Myr. K-Pg extinction synchronous with Chicxulub within 32 kyr; placental orders after K-Pg per O'Leary, contested.
7. Variable Principle applied: "Eyes evolved 40+ times": consistent with the published range, with the caveat that independence is debated. "Identical inputs ... produce identical outputs": contradicted: convergent eyes use separately arisen lens proteins and differ in type; the outputs are similar, not identical. Punctuated equilibrium: canon's examples (Cambrian explosion, post-K-Pg radiation) are mass-extinction or radiation events; the originators define PE as stasis within species histories plus species sorting. The label is misapplied. "Cambrian explosion followed Snowball Earth": true in sequence, but separated by roughly 90 Myr with the Ediacaran between; "Maximum contraction enabled maximum subsequent expansion" is not forced by the ages. "Mammalian radiation followed K-Pg": supported by one major study, contested by another; held open. Progress toward consciousness: no measure; held open.

(iii) State the ~90 Myr interval between the end of the Marinoan glaciation and the Cambrian base. (iv) Mark progression toward consciousness as unobserved.

## Forcing Test
- Test A (Ground): Radiometric ages, eye-development experiments, and the originators' own definition ground the corrections.
- Test B (Uniqueness): Convergent eyes are several types, not one; outputs are not unique or identical.
- Test C (Direction): Canon reads events as "maximum contraction enabled maximum expansion"; the ~90 Myr gap and the Ediacaran interval do not force that causal direction.
- Test D (Falsifiability): "Identical outputs" was falsifiable and fails. The progress claim has no falsifier (no measure). The mapping names no failing observation.
- **Outcome:** forced-no for "identical inputs + environment ... produce identical outputs" and for the attribution of mass-extinction radiations to punctuated equilibrium. Intake `open`: the contradicting evidence is measured-single or review-level (derived). Mapping: stipulation, and this is the domain where it fits least literally (see below).

## Anti-Operation (the gap this opens)

- Evolution has no detector. No agent "detects" variation; variation arises and selection is the differential outcome. DETECT here is a relabeling of "variation exists". By canon's own rule ("if any domain resists the mapping cleanly, it becomes a named variable"), this should be a named variable; canon names none and declares "Zero Exceptions".
- Deep homology (shared Pax6 control) vs independent origin of eyes: the count "40+" depends on which is counted. Open.
- The Cambrian base date has been revised since 1993; a later run should reach the ICS chart.

## Narrative stripped (if any)

- **"toward progressive Third Kingdom expression"**, **"culminating in consciousness recognizing itself"**: teleology. Reality does not show a direction toward consciousness; no measure is given. Removed from the factual core.
- **"Per Correspondence: identical inputs ... universal algorithm"**: Correspondence reading of convergence; the factual part (convergence under physical constraint) stands without it.
- **Variation = Yang, Selection = Yin**: labeling choice.
- **Mapping verdict: stipulation.** Variation → selection → frequency change is an accurate outline of natural selection, but it is a population-level statistical outcome, not a sense → compute → act loop. Fitting it to DETECT → PROCESS → RESPOND requires treating "variation exists" as detection. Canon names no observation that would count as failure to map, so this domain does not test the universal claim.
