## Canon says

v5.1 Layer I.C, Domain 2:

- *Three Kingdoms:* "Founding revelation (Third) → doctrine, narrative, ritual (Second) → institutional practice, culture, civilizational architecture (First)."
- *Algorithm:* "DETECT: Human need for meaning, moral framework, community. PROCESS: Doctrine, liturgy, ritual practice, community structure. RESPOND: Behavioral architecture, culture, civilizational values."
- *Universal Arc:* "Every major religion: founding revelation → codification into text and doctrine → institutional propagation. The founding signal degrades in predictable ways as it encodes."
- *Yin/Yang:* "The institution that tips into Yang loses the transcendent core. The one that tips into Yin loses social coherence."
- Domain 2 Algorithm step 2: "codification is necessary but always degrades the signal somewhat"; step 5: "Does the tradition's structure match the structures of independently developed traditions? Convergence is evidence."; step 8: "convergence across traditions indicates one underlying structure."

**Split.** *Factual core (observable):* religions have texts, doctrines, rituals, and institutions; many trace themselves to a founding figure or event. *Unobserved claims:* "every major religion" follows the arc; a "founding signal" exists and "degrades in predictable ways"; convergence "indicates one underlying structure". *Framework mapping:* DETECT = human need, PROCESS = doctrine and ritual, RESPOND = culture; Kingdom ordering; business equivalent.

Note on referent: the Registry describes the *religious process* (what religions do). The Domain 2 Algorithm (8 steps) is a different object: a procedure for an *analyst studying* a religion (identify signal, apply falsifiability test, compare traditions). The two are filed under one domain name.

## Scientific Inquiry run
1. Question (precise): Is there evidence that (a) every major religion follows founding revelation → codification → institutional propagation, (b) a founding signal degrades in predictable ways, and (c) the DETECT → PROCESS → RESPOND mapping is forced by observation?
2. What an answer must look like: (a) a defined population of religions and historical data per member; (b) a measure of "signal" and its change over time, with a prediction made before the data; (c) a named observation that would count as non-mapping.
3. Falsifiability condition: (a) fails if a major religion lacks a founding figure / revelation (the arc needs one) or never institutionalized; (b) fails if degradation is not predictable under a stated measure; (c) canon names no failing observation.
4. Variables: measurable / bounded / held open: measurable in principle: presence of texts, clergy, institutions per tradition. Bounded: nothing this run. Held open: "founding signal" (undefined), "degrades" (no metric), "predictable" (no prediction stated), "transcendent core" (undefined).
5. Test designed: Search for a measurement of signal fidelity or a comparative dataset; fetch the scholarly status of the convergence thesis that Domain 2 steps 5 and 8 rely on.
6. Data (unfiltered): No measure of "founding signal" exists to fetch. The convergence thesis is disputed in the scholarly literature (SEP "Mysticism", perennialism vs constructivism). The universality of the arc was not tested: no primary historical dataset was reached.
7. Variable Principle applied: "Degrades in predictable ways" is stated as a fact but has no measure and no stated prediction; it is held open, not graded true or false. "Convergence is evidence ... of one underlying structure" is a contested inference, held open.
8. Model update (Capsule: what the failed parts contribute): The Universal Arc is a descriptive schema. To become testable it needs (i) an operational definition of the signal (for example, a coded set of founding teachings), (ii) a metric of divergence across later texts, and (iii) a prediction of the degradation pattern before the data are read.
9. Documented: This file.
10. Next baseline: Factual core (religions have doctrine, ritual, institutions): trivially observable, not in dispute. Arc universality and signal degradation: unobserved-claim, open.

## Forcing Test
- Test A (Ground): No measurement grounds "founding signal" or its degradation. The observable ground (religions have texts and institutions) does not force the arc.
- Test B (Uniqueness): Other schemas fit the same observations (for example, religions that grew from pre-existing practice without a single founding revelation; traditions whose doctrine precedes institutions or vice versa). The arc is one candidate, not the only one.
- Test C (Direction): Direction runs from the schema to the religions: "every major religion" is asserted, not surveyed.
- Test D (Falsifiability): As stated, not falsifiable: no measure of signal, no stated prediction. The DETECT → PROCESS → RESPOND mapping names no failing observation.
- **Outcome:** stipulation. The arc and its degradation claim are a presentation choice (a descriptive lens), not a forced or measured result. Held open with review date.

## Anti-Operation (the gap this opens)

- Canon has no operational definition of "signal" for Domains 1, 2, and 6, although "signal degradation" is central to all three. Open.
- Domain 2 Algorithm step 5 counts convergence as evidence without a control for comparer-imposed structure (see DOMAIN-01).
- Registry vs Algorithm referent mismatch: the Registry describes what religions do; the Algorithm describes what an analyst does. "One algorithm" is claimed for both.

## Narrative stripped (if any)

- **"Founding revelation (Third Kingdom)"**: a theological category; reality does not force it. Observable replacement: "founding figure, text, or event claimed by the tradition".
- **"The founding signal degrades in predictable ways"**: no measurement; removed from the factual core; held open.
- **Mapping verdict: stipulation.** DETECT (human need) → PROCESS (doctrine, ritual) → RESPOND (culture) fits any social institution (law, schools, clubs, companies; canon itself says "A company with a clear founding mission is a religious structure"). A lens that fits every institution does not test the claim that the religious process runs the Universal Algorithm. Canon names no observation that would count as failure to map.
- Yin/Yang tipping claims: framework interpretation; no measurement.
