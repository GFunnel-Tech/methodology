## Scientific Inquiry run

1. Question (precise): Is Layer II an outside model imported as structure (substrate item 4), and does its name/subtitle assert empirical status ("observable, testable … structural laws") that measurement supports?
2. What an answer must look like: The provenance of the seven-principle set (dated record), and for each principle whether a measurement tests it as stated.
3. Falsifiability condition: The "structural law" status fails for any principle that has no measurement able to contradict it; the "independent convergence" claim fails without a record of independence.
4. Variables: measurable / bounded / held open: Measurable: the 1908 publication. Bounded: some principles have measurable counterparts in physics under other names (e.g. atomic motion for Vibration; periodic processes for Rhythm), not fetched here. Held open: Mentalism; the pre-1908 history of the set; the independence of the framework from the book.
5. Test designed: Fetch bibliographic records for the source canon names; read each principle for a stated measurement.
6. Data (unfiltered): The set is recorded in a 1908 book (two catalogues). Canon's glossary names this source. Layer II gives each principle a statement, a resolution, a formula and a Yin/Yang link; none gives a measurement. Mentalism is the ground of the Three Kingdoms and is held open by canon itself. Layer I claims the framework "arrived at" the principles; no record of independence is given.
7. Variable Principle applied: Provenance is measured: one named external book (1908). The subtitle's "observable, testable" and "structural laws" are not supported for the set as a whole; held open principle by principle.

## Forcing Test

- Test A (Ground): Grounded only for provenance (1908). Not grounded for the principles as laws.
- Test B (Uniqueness): Other organizing sets would serve the same presentation role (canon itself says Yin/Yang "restates" Polarity); not forced.
- Test C (Direction): No measurement points to these seven, in this form, as the structure of reality.
- Test D (Falsifiability): Mentalism has a stated falsifier in ◇ (consciousness shown identical to its substrate), but no feasible test; the set as a whole has none.
- **Outcome:** stipulation (an operator's choice of organizing structure, imported from a named external source). The label's "laws" status: unobserved. ARCHITECTURAL question for the author.

## Anti-Operation (the gap this opens)

Substrate item 4 says external theories may be *discovered* through the process, if reality forces them. Layer II was imported, not discovered. The gap: for each of the seven, what observation would have forced it if the framework had never read the 1908 book? Any principle with an answer survives as an observation-based lens; any without one remains an interpretation. This re-grounding is the author's decision.

## Narrative stripped (if any)

- "Hermetic": names a tradition. The dated source is a 1908 book; its claim to ancient Egyptian and Greek origin is its own narrative and is not a record fetched here.
- "Observable, testable … structural laws" and "not … mystical assertions": not supported by any measurement in the layer.
- "Seven independent traditions … arrive at the same structure" (Mentalism) and "The Omni Process … arrived at them": independence is unrecorded; convergence without independence is not evidence.
- Principle-level mappings (rock "vibrating at a specific frequency" as the ground of resonance between people; "Hate … can be transmuted into love"; Gender as Masculine/Feminine): interpretations; reality neither forces nor contradicts them.
- Old name kept in lineage; nothing renamed in canon.
