## Scientific Inquiry run

1. Question (precise): Does the label, with its subsection name "The Zhongyong Principle", correctly identify its sources, and does it import an outside model as structure (substrate item 4)?
2. What an answer must look like: The textual location of the term zhongyong, and a check of whether the Dao De Jing contains it.
3. Falsifiability condition: "The Tao Te Ching's zhongyong" is false if the term is absent from the Dao De Jing and located in another text.
4. Variables: measurable / bounded / held open: Measurable: where 中庸 occurs in the received texts. Held open: whether the Taoist and Hermetic accounts are independent; whether a shifting set-point between two opposed extremes is a general law.
5. Test designed: Fetch the Zhong Yong and the full Dao De Jing from the Chinese Text Project; locate the term.
6. Data (unfiltered): Zhong Yong is catalogued as a chapter of the Liji under Confucianism. The term 中庸 occurs 0 times in the fetched full Dao De Jing text. Canon's own glossary already calls zhongyong "the Confucian principle".
7. Variable Principle applied: The attribution "The Tao Te Ching's zhongyong" is ruled out by the text record and contradicts canon's own glossary. The structural idea (a centre that shifts between two extremes) is not graded by this; it remains a live hypothesis with ◇'s stated falsifier ("a domain where … extreme Yin or Yang is categorically superior").
8. Model update (Capsule: what the failed parts contribute): The failed attribution shows the layer draws on two traditions (Taoist Yin/Yang, Confucian zhongyong) and a third (Hermetic Polarity). An observation-based name that carries none of them is closer to what the layer operationally does: balance between two opposed tendencies with a set-point that moves.
9. Documented: This file. Correspondence: one scale down, physiology's autonomic balance (sympathetic/parasympathetic) is used by canon as the body-scale case (not fetched here, held open); one scale up, Layer V's "draw position" is the business-scale case.

## Anti-Operation (the gap this opens)

The layer's structure rests on "they arrived at the same law independently. Convergence." Canon does not document the independence of Hermetic Polarity, Taoist Yin/Yang and Confucian zhongyong, or of the framework from any of them. The gap: without an independence record, convergence cannot count as evidence (same gap as LABEL-102, LABEL-109).
