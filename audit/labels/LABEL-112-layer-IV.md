## Scientific Inquiry run

1. Question (precise): Is "Co-Creator Identity" — a human as a local instance of a cosmic creating Mind — supported by measurement, as the layer's "Structural Proof" asserts?
2. What an answer must look like: A measurement bearing on whether individual minds are expressions of a cosmic mind, or a derivation from measured premises.
3. Falsifiability condition: The "proof" fails if its premises are unmeasured or its lines of reasoning are not independent.
4. Variables: measurable / bounded / held open: Measurable: none for the identity claim. Derived: the five lines share one source and one premise (Mentalism). Held open: Mentalism; the nature of the Omni Force (canon's own variables).
5. Test designed: Trace each of the five lines to its premise; compare with canon's held-open variables.
6. Data (unfiltered): Each line cites a Layer II principle; Mentalism is the premise of the first and is invoked in the others. Canon itself holds Mentalism unresolved (Part IX) and the Omni Force's nature open (Part V). The Five Modes and Immersion Model are business procedures with no theological premise.
7. Variable Principle applied: The co-creator claim is a theological claim resting on a premise canon holds open; "proof … not metaphorically" fills that variable. Held open. The operational content (propagation modes, immersion) is kept and does not depend on it.
8. Model update (Capsule: what the failed parts contribute): The layer splits into an unmeasured identity claim and a measured-in-practice business method. The name narrows to the method; the identity claim stays in lineage as a held-open variable.
9. Documented: This file. Correspondence: one scale up, the Omni Force (Layer I Part V), held open; one scale down, the operator's process that "outlasts the vessel" (Five Modes), operational.
10. Next baseline: Proposed for v5.5 (not applied): "Operator Role & Process Propagation". The co-creator claim inherits Mentalism's ◇ falsifier. Re-review 2027-09-24.

## Anti-Operation (the gap this opens)

The layer counts five lines as independent. The gap: the framework has no rule for when two lines of reasoning count as independent evidence. Without one, "convergence" (Layers I, I.D, II, IV) can count the same premise several times.
