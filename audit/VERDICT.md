## What works, and why

### 1 · Layer ⊙, the Container — the strongest result (`works-forced`)

Run on a real open question ("does the base code require an information-processing step?"), its four tests each did **non-redundant** work and reached a **stronger** verdict than the component sweep had reached without them. Test A refused a cheap "unresolvable" answer by showing the gap was closable from inside — canon had simply never written a criterion for individuating an operation. Test D disqualified the unfalsifiable rival. Outcome: forced, on the negative — the measured minimum is **two** operations, and canon's three-station reading survives only as a presentation choice.

**Why it works:** it is a classifier, and classifiers earn their keep by eliminating candidates. It did, on grounds that could have gone the other way. **It also corrected the audit**, not canon.

### 2 · Layer I.C, the Process Registry — (`works-forced`)

Its step 3 demands: *name the molecule that detects the quantity being regulated.* Run against cell division, that returned **none** — and the field agrees: the measured size-control rule is published as *"direct experimental evidence disproving the critical size paradigm."* The unprompted description ("cells divide when they've grown enough") is the model the data disprove.

**Why it works:** insisting on a named occupant per slot makes an absence visible. The falsifier is stated and still live.

### 3 · Layer ◇, the Falsifiability Algorithm — canon's best instrument (`works-descriptive`)

Its step 2 — *if no observation would falsify it, it is not a claim, it is a belief* — is a **working detector**, and in one step it convicts canon's own register. Everything the audit spent separate investigations proving about non-operational falsifiers, this algorithm finds immediately.

**Why it works, and the sharp point:** the instrument is sound; **canon never ran it on its own text.** The register's arrow is reversed — confident status first, falsifier back-filled to match. One real design gap: step 6's vocabulary has no slot for a step-2 failure, so a non-claim must be filed as "held open", destroying the exact distinction the Variable Principle exists to protect.

### 4 · The Scientific Inquiry Algorithm — it earned its ~270 uses (`works-descriptive`)

Steps 1–6 and 9–10 are ordinary scientific method. **Steps 7 and 8 are canon's real additions** and they paid on a live question: step 7 held 149 genes of unknown function open under pressure to round them away; step 8 put the *failed* design to work, which is the mechanism the minimal-genome programme's own authors credit for their result.

**One declaration owed:** step 6 says "run the experiment". This audit has executed it ~270 times as *retrieval*. Canon licenses that nowhere, and it is why one sealed prediction came back indeterminate.

### 5 · Layer I.E, Energy Continuation — beats canon's own stage table (`works-descriptive`)

Step 1 asks *"what polarity powers this?"* rather than *"where's the energy?"* — and returns the ion gradient. Canon's own Appendix A Stage 23 ("ATP … universal currency") returns the **wrong** answer for the flagellar motor. **The layer is right where canon's stage table is wrong.**

### 6 · Layer III+, Integration Density — (`works-descriptive`)

Its first four steps have a measured occupant each: CRISPR spacer acquisition is literally *map prior paths → extract the data → build substrate that holds them → start the next exploration from it*, and primed adaptation is step 4 measured. Capped because canon cites immune memory as its own example, so it is near-definitional.

## What is vacuous, and why

These are not false. **Nothing could make them false**, which is worse for an instrument.

### Layer I.B, the Four Pillars

Three of four pillars land on genuine measured features of a bacterium with no stretching — a three-stage *multiplicative* fidelity architecture, simultaneously maintained alternative respiratory routes, layered independent defence. **That is the strongest Correspondence content in the whole run.** But each pillar is also filled by a feature *and its opposite*, and the one falsifiable step ("the system fails at exactly the weakest pillar") is unreachable because canon supplies **no scale, units, or procedure for any pillar anywhere in v5.1**. The failure is the instrument's, not the pillars'.

### Layer I.D, the Dynamic Middle

The one measured moving optimum in a bacterium shifts by a factor 3.7 under stated perturbations — the middle demonstrably moves. But the algorithm, **run forward, does not predict the direction**, and the Three States table contains a diagnostic category ("Rigid Middle — Dysfunction") that converts a measured *fixed* optimum into a symptom of the system rather than a refutation of the layer. **A layer with a named diagnosis for its own falsifier cannot be falsified by it.** Also: the output is invariant under swapping the Yin and Yang labels. One sentence would fix the direction problem: *relaxing a constraint moves the middle toward the relaxed constraint.*

### Layer II, the Seven Hermetic Principles

Canon claims a cell instantiates all seven. Principle by principle: no measurement separates Mentalism's "homeostatic intent" from reaction kinetics; Gender's categories are assigned after inspection. Four of the algorithm's steps are blocked for want of any criterion for "honored" vs "violated". **Three rows of canon's own cell table are measurably false** (no circadian clock in this organism; intrinsic noise is measured, against "no randomness"; the nucleoid is *defined by* depletion of the partner Gender pairs with it).

## What fails, and why

### Layer 0, the Base Code

Its loop closes "at a *higher* baseline, informed by the two prior cycles". Both measured cross-cycle rules in this organism do the opposite: adapted response curves **collapse onto one curve** across a 50-fold range, and size control adds a **constant** increment with mother–daughter correlation of **−1/4** and memory decaying 2× per generation. The Fibonacci clause is measurably **negative-signed** here. Separately, 2 of its 6 steps are written in the second person and cannot be run on a system that cannot report on itself.

### Layer I, Three Kingdoms

The separable directing layer is real and well-evidenced. The one-way arrow (Second → First) is not: matter writes into code — foreign DNA integrates in up to 96% of recipients, immune memory is written into the chromosome, mutation runs at ~10⁻³ per genome per generation.

### Layer I.F, Multi-Level Observation

Three falsifiers were specified before the values were read, and **all three fired.** Twelve populations persisted 50,000 generations while missing the upper observation levels. Step 5's "perfect process or the draw" is contradicted by measured deliberate waste — and cannot be repaired without becoming vacuous: admit readiness as a third configuration and every configuration qualifies. **Credit where due: this layer made claims sharp enough to lose. That is better than being unfalsifiable.**

### Layer I.G, the Capsule

Four of seven steps require the functional half of "nothing is lost", which is contradicted. **Decisively, in canon's own organism:** *Physarum*'s memory is measured to be stored in *the surviving network's tube-diameter hierarchy* — the survivor's structure — **not** carried by the retracted material. That independently confirms the audit's proposed replacement ([CLAIM-021](claims/CLAIM-021.md)): the tube-diameter hierarchy **is** the discriminating structure.

### Layer III, Process Density

Its own cell row states "~10 micrometers", which is 120–350× too large in volume for a bacterium. Steps 2–6 are blocked: no counting rule, no threshold, no units. **The correction runs in canon's favour** — a bacterium is two orders of magnitude denser than canon's own number implies — but the ranking claim stays untestable. Layer III+'s step 5 is worse: it asks for a ratio of a part to *the whole it is part of* to equal 1.618, which lies in [0,1] and is **arithmetically unattainable for every system**.

### Layers V, VI, VII — and why ACE phasing does *not* rescue them

Canon claims these correspond to cell scale **explicitly**, at line 429: *"Per Correspondence: the same algorithm that runs a cell runs a city. **Not analogy. Same law at a different scale.**"* It goes further and assigns cellular occupants itself — "Memory formation = Shepherd's Way Steps 06–07 at biological level", Stage 20 "Shepherd's Way at biochemical scale". So `not-applicable` was **not available** to canon here. All three fail:

- **Layer V (BEAS), attempted honestly:** of 45 cells, **7 defensible, 8 contradicted, 25 arbitrary, 5 unfillable.** Scoring the arbitrary cells at 0 yields "Collapse Risk"; at 1 yields "Equilibrium — Functional". **A 25-point spread on a 45-point scale means 56% of the instrument's output is operator discretion on fixed data.** The grid predicts nine departments, delivers eight at best (three by definitional slippage), predicts one that does not exist, and misses many that do. Its verdict on a bacterium that copies 4.6 million base pairs at 10⁸–10¹¹-fold accuracy and doubles in 20 minutes: *"almost no documented systems, emergency blueprint session."*
- **Layer VI (Shepherd's Way):** step 1 ("Inputs: nothing yet") contradicts canon's own Layer 0, where detection is first. "Filter nothing" is contradicted by five independent measured discard layers. "Now and only now does creation begin" is contradicted by the ribosome translating an incomplete transcript **and setting the polymerase's speed**.
- **Layer VII (ACE):** three steps blocked (no counterparty that can misreport), and step 6 prescribed improving the lowest-conversion channel — which is the viral route whose inefficiency is *protective*. **Its best hit is real though:** sugar arrives inside already tagged, and one measured protein implements three separate canonical instructions.

## The pattern

**Canon's methodological instruments are markedly better than its content claims.** Both `works-forced` grades and three of four `works-descriptive` grades are instruments for *classifying, testing and holding open*. Every `fails` grade is a **content claim about how reality is structured** — a universal currency, a one-way arrow, a rising baseline, nothing discarded, a density ranking, nine departments.

**Two classification vocabularies are missing the same slot,** found independently by two instruments: *"the claim is not yet well-formed."* ◇ needs a `not-a-claim` status; the Forcing Test needs an `under-specified` output. Two one-line edits, no base-code change.

**The framework's strongest single element is the Variable Principle** — name what you don't know rather than filling it. It is the one thing carried unchanged into the design algorithm, and it is what caught the dominant risk in the first design run ([PRED-004](prediction/PRED-004.md), hit).

**What this does not say.** It does not say the framework is worthless: it organizes $158–177 B of measured knowledge ([`cost/`](cost/README.md)), its instruments work, and its corrected form produced a design that surfaced a real risk before any engineering source was consulted. It says the framework is **an Acquire-phase instrument that has been claiming Create-phase authority**, and that the content claims asserting universality are where it breaks.
