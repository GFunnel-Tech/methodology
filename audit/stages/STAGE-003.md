## Scientific Inquiry run

1. Question (precise): (a) Is there a measurement that directly shows an inflationary epoch? (b) Is electroweak symmetry breaking measured, and is its occurrence in the early universe measured?
2. What an answer must look like: (a) a detected primordial tensor spectrum or another observable only inflation produces; (b) a measured Higgs sector plus a cosmological observable that reaches T ~ 160 GeV.
3. Falsifiability condition: Inflation (single-field slow-roll class) would be disfavoured by n_s = 1 exactly, significant curvature, or large isocurvature / non-Gaussian signals. Electroweak breaking would be falsified by no Higgs. The v5.4 "false-vacuum decay" and "Kibble–Zurek defects, forced" claims would be falsified by a cross-over transition (no metastable phase) and by absence of defects.
4. Variables: measurable / bounded / held open: Measurable: n_s, Omega_K, Higgs mass (two experiments). Bounded: r < 0.036; G mu / c^2 < 1.3e-7; T_RH > 4.7 MeV. Held open: whether inflation occurred (live); the inflaton; whether the universe ever reached T ~ 160 GeV (data only bound T_RH from below at 4.7 MeV).
5. Test designed: Fetch inflation-constraint papers, the tensor limit, the Higgs discovery papers, the lattice nature of the SM transition, and the defect limits; compare to canon and to v5.4 §2's "forced" grades.
6. Data (unfiltered): n_s is below 1 at > 8 sigma and curvature is consistent with zero; both fit inflation but do not uniquely require it. Tensor modes are not detected. The Higgs exists (two independent experiments, masses 126.0 and 125.3 GeV). With the measured Higgs mass, the SM electroweak transition is a smooth cross-over (lattice), so there is no metastable (false) vacuum in the SM electroweak case. No cosmic strings are detected. No measurement reaches the ~160 GeV epoch.
7. Variable Principle applied: Observed: n_s, flatness, the Higgs. Modeled: inflation, and the cosmological electroweak epoch (an extrapolation of lab physics beyond the earliest probe). Both modeled parts are held open, not filled.
8. Model update (Capsule: what the failed parts contribute): The failed parts are useful: (i) the absence of tensor modes sets the ceiling r < 0.036 that any inflation model must meet; (ii) the lattice cross-over removes one mechanism (SM electroweak false-vacuum decay with defects) that v5.4 §2 names as forced.
9. Documented: This file. Resolves FINDINGS F-002 as proposed: tag live-hypothesis and split observed from modeled. Correspondence: one scale down, spontaneous symmetry breaking is observed in the laboratory (the Higgs; ferromagnets, not fetched); one scale up, the QCD transition (STAGE-004) is also a lattice cross-over.
10. Next baseline: Next B-mode releases (BICEP/Keck, Simons Observatory) tightening r. Re-review 2027-09-24.

## Forcing Test

- Test A (Ground): Inflation: indirectly grounded (n_s, flatness), not directly observed. Electroweak symmetry breaking: grounded in the lab (Higgs); its cosmic occurrence is not grounded by any cosmological measurement.
- Test B (Uniqueness): More than one candidate survives for the origin of the primordial spectrum (inflation classes, bounce / alternative scenarios). Not unique.
- Test C (Direction): Measurements bound inflation (r, n_s) but do not fix its direction or its field.
- Test D (Falsifiability): Inflation carries falsifiers (tensor amplitude, isocurvature, non-Gaussianity); none has falsified it. Falsifier attached: detection of a primordial spectrum incompatible with adiabatic Gaussian near-scale-invariant seeding.
- **Outcome:** live-hypothesis. This is the F-002 resolution. Not forced-fill.

## Anti-Operation (the gap this opens)

v5.4 §2 labels "false-vacuum decay" and "Kibble–Zurek defects ... knots that persist" as **forced** at Stage 3. Fetched data: the SM electroweak transition is a smooth cross-over at T_c = 159.5 +/- 1.5 GeV (arXiv 1508.07161), which has no false vacuum to decay and does not require Kibble–Zurek defects; Planck finds no cosmic strings (G mu / c^2 < 1.3e-7, arXiv 1303.5085). Proposed for the coordinator: downgrade the cosmic application of false-vacuum decay and Kibble–Zurek at Stage 3 from forced to live-hypothesis (the Higgs mechanism itself stays forced). Also held open: canon's "~10⁻¹² s" timing was not verified in this run (no primary source fetched states it).
