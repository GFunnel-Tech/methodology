## Scientific Inquiry run
1. Question (precise): Is the family the first social organizational unit, and is coupled processing between individuals observable?
2. What an answer must look like: Data on the composition of ancestral-type human groups; measurements of inter-individual neural coupling.
3. Falsifiability condition: 'First organizational unit' fails if groups larger than and not structured around the family are the base residential unit.
4. Variables: measurable / bounded / held open: Measurable: forager co-residence; neural coupling. Held open: the ancestral group structure before present-day foragers.
5. Test designed: Retrieve forager co-residence data and neural coupling data.
6. Data (unfiltered): Forager bands average 28.2 adults with most members unrelated; inter-brain coupling is measurable.
7. Variable Principle applied: Ancestral structure held open; present-day foragers are a proxy, not a measurement of the past.
8. Model update (Capsule: what the failed parts contribute): Coupling: accounted. 'Family as first unit': not forced — the measured residential unit is a multi-family band of mostly unrelated adults.
9. Documented: This file.
10. Next baseline: Relationships real; primacy of the family as organizational unit is a hypothesis.

## Forcing Test
- Test A (Ground): Measured (proxy).
- Test B (Uniqueness): Not unique: band-level organization is an alternative first unit.
- Test C (Direction): Order (family → community) not established by the fetched data.
- Test D (Falsifiability): Falsifier: evidence that residential groups are primarily nuclear families would support canon; the fetched data point the other way.
- **Outcome:** live-hypothesis for 'first organizational unit'; relationship coupling accounted.

## Anti-Operation (the gap this opens)
Canon orders Family (40) before Communities (41). The fetched forager data suggest the two co-occur in the base unit. Also open: social organization in non-human species predates both.

## Narrative stripped
Removed: 'Two Third Kingdoms in coupled processing' (Kingdom mapping). The measured analogue is inter-brain coupling during communication; reality does not force the Kingdom reading.
