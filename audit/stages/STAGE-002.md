## Scientific Inquiry run

1. Question (precise): (a) Do quantum fields show measurable zero-point fluctuation effects? (b) Is there a measurement placing a vacuum fluctuation as the first cosmological event after a pre-geometric state?
2. What an answer must look like: (a) a reproduced laboratory measurement of a vacuum-fluctuation effect with uncertainty; (b) a cosmological observable whose value requires a vacuum-fluctuation origin and fixes its ordering.
3. Falsifiability condition: (a) Casimir force absent or disagreeing with QED at the stated precision. (b) A detected primordial spectrum inconsistent with a quantum-vacuum origin (for example a strongly non-adiabatic or non-Gaussian spectrum) would count against the cosmic placement.
4. Variables: measurable / bounded / held open: Measurable: Casimir force (three independent groups); n_s, A_s of primordial perturbations. Bounded: rho_Lambda c^2 = 5.24e-10 J/m3 (derived). Held open: whether primordial perturbations originated as vacuum fluctuations (this is the inflationary account, STAGE-003); whether anything is "first"; the vacuum-density discrepancy (held open by v5.4).
5. Test designed: Fetch reproduced Casimir measurements; fetch primordial-spectrum parameters; check whether any measurement orders a vacuum fluctuation first.
6. Data (unfiltered): Casimir effect measured by three independent setups (1997, 1998, 2002) consistent with theory to 1% (sphere–plate) and 15% (parallel plates). Primordial perturbations exist with n_s = 0.9649 +/- 0.0042, a near but not exact scale invariance. No data orders events before the earliest thermal epoch. The derived dark-energy energy density 5.24e-10 J/m3 agrees with v5.4's "10⁻⁹ J/m³" to order of magnitude (v5.4 rounds up; the fetched value is 10^-9.28).
7. Variable Principle applied: (a) is measured. (b) is not: the primordial spectrum is consistent with a quantum-vacuum origin but does not force it, and "first" is unmeasured. The link (a) → (b) is held open.
8. Model update (Capsule: what the failed parts contribute): The failed part (no measurement orders the first event) shows that "First Vibration" is a sequencing claim, not an observation. What survives is a lab fact (zero-point effects) and a cosmological fact (a nearly scale-invariant primordial spectrum), joined by a model.
9. Documented: This file. Correspondence: one scale down, the same zero-point effect appears as the Lamb shift and spontaneous emission in atoms (not fetched here, named only); one scale up, the primordial spectrum n_s feeds the CMB anisotropies graded in STAGE-006.
10. Next baseline: Next CMB polarization releases (tensor modes) and any detection of primordial non-Gaussianity. Re-review 2027-09-24.

## Forcing Test

- Test A (Ground): Zero-point effects: grounded (reproduced Casimir measurements). Cosmic "first event": not grounded.
- Test B (Uniqueness): For the lab fact, one candidate survives (QED vacuum). For the cosmic placement, several survive (inflationary vacuum fluctuations, bounce scenarios, other seeding models); not unique.
- Test C (Direction): The lab fact gives no direction for cosmic ordering. The step "Casimir instance, therefore cosmic Stage 1→2 mechanism" moves from a lab-scale fact to a cosmological claim without a measurement in between.
- Test D (Falsifiability): Lab fact falsifiable and survived. Cosmic placement: falsifiable in principle through primordial-spectrum features; not yet tested decisively.
- **Outcome:** live-hypothesis (for the stage as a cosmological event). The component "quantum fields have zero-point fluctuations" is forced by measurement and is accounted.
