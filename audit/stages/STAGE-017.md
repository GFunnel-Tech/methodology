## Scientific Inquiry run
1. Question (precise): When did atmospheric oxygenation (GOE) begin and end, and when did oxygenic photosynthesis originate?
2. What an answer must look like: radiometric ages bracketing redox-proxy transitions; independent proxies (S-MIF, pyrite, Cr isotopes); phylogenetic inference for the origin of oxygenic photosynthesis.
3. Falsifiability condition: Canon's "~2.4 Bya" for the GOE is falsified if the onset of O2 > 10^-5 PAL lay outside roughly 2.3–2.5 Ga. The equation "oxygenic photosynthesis = ~2.4 Bya" is falsified if oxygenic photosynthesis is shown to be much older than the GOE.
4. Variables: measurable: radiometric ages; isotope proxies. Bounded: GOE onset (2.46–2.43 Ga), permanent oxygenation (~2.22 Ga). Held open: the origin date of oxygenic photosynthesis; the size of any pre-GOE oxygen.
5. Test designed: fetch primary geochronology and isotope papers for GOE onset and duration; fetch pre-GOE oxygen evidence; fetch a genomic origin study.
6. Data (unfiltered): table above. GOE onset ~2.43 Ga, reproduced by independent groups. Oxygen fluctuated for ~200 Myr; permanent oxygenation ~2.22 Ga. Evidence for O2 at ~3.0 Ga exists but is single and contested. Genomic data say photosynthesis was acquired late within Cyanobacteria but give no absolute date.
7. Variable Principle applied: GOE onset = measured (~2.43 Ga). GOE as one event = contradicted by a protracted ~200 Myr transition (measured-single). Origin of oxygenic photosynthesis = held open.
8. Model update (Capsule): Canon's ~2.4 Bya is consistent with GOE onset. Canon's item leaves out two things reality shows: the GOE was protracted (2.43 → ~2.22 Ga), and the origin of oxygenic photosynthesis is not dated by the GOE (it may be earlier).
9. Documented: this file.
10. Next baseline: independent reproduction of pre-GOE oxygen proxies (3.0–2.5 Ga) and of the ~2.22 Ga permanence date.

## Forcing Test
- Test A (Ground): GOE onset grounded (radiometric + isotope proxies). Origin date of oxygenic photosynthesis not grounded.
- Test B (Uniqueness): GOE onset: one value range survives (~2.46–2.43 Ga). Origin of oxygenic photosynthesis: multiple candidates survive (near the GOE; ~3.0 Ga or earlier).
- Test C (Direction): If the GOE's oxygen source is oxygenic photosynthesis (the standard inference; not separately tested in this run), the origin must precede or coincide with the GOE onset. How long before is not forced.
- Test D (Falsifiability): Yes. Falsifier: reproduced evidence of O2 production well before 2.5 Ga would separate the two dates that canon's label joins.
- **Outcome:** live-hypothesis (canon's joint claim "oxygenic photosynthesis ~2.4 Bya"). The GOE onset date alone is accounted.

## Anti-Operation (the gap this opens)
Canon's single row joins an origin (a biological innovation) and an event (atmospheric change) under one date. Separating them opens a variable canon does not name: the lag between oxygenic photosynthesis and atmospheric oxygenation, and what held O2 low during that lag. Also opened: Stage 18 (endosymbiosis) and the aerobic Stages 19–22 are ordered after this stage; the ~200 Myr protracted transition makes "after the GOE" a range, not a point.

## Narrative stripped (if any)
- "Yang-extreme intervention" and "Per Punctuated Equilibrium: maximum contraction enabled maximum subsequent expansion" are framework mappings. Reality neither forces nor contradicts them. The measured protracted transition (~200 Myr of oscillation) does not fit a single "intervention" reading, but that does not change the grade of the factual core.
- Terminology: the literature term is "Great Oxidation Event"; canon writes "Great Oxygenation Event". Either names the same measured transition.
- Label: "Oxygenic Photosynthesis (~2.4 Bya)" dates the innovation by the atmospheric event. Proposed label keeps the measured date on the event and marks the origin as held open.
