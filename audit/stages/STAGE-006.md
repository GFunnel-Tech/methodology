## Scientific Inquiry run

1. Question (precise): Is there a measured relic of an epoch when electrons bound to nuclei and radiation decoupled from matter, and do its properties match that account?
2. What an answer must look like: A measured near-perfect blackbody background with a temperature today, and an anisotropy pattern fixing the redshift of last scattering.
3. Falsifiability condition: A background spectrum measurably different from a blackbody; or no acoustic structure at a single last-scattering redshift.
4. Variables: measurable / bounded / held open: Measurable: T0, blackbody deviations, theta*. Derived: z* = 1089.92, t* = 371.8 kyr, T(z*) ~ 2973 K. Held open: none for the factual core.
5. Test designed: Fetch T0 from the CMB-temperature review, the FIRAS spectral limits, and the Planck/PDG decoupling parameters; check the blackbody and last-scattering predictions.
6. Data (unfiltered): T0 = 2.72548 +/- 0.00057 K. Spectrum blackbody to < 50 ppm. Last scattering at z* = 1089.92 +/- 0.25, t* = 371.8 +/- 1.0 kyr; radiation then at ~2973 K. Canon's content is consistent. v5.4 numbers: "stretched 1100×" vs 1 + z* = 1090.92 (agrees to ~1%); "380 kyr" vs t* = 371.8 +/- 1.0 kyr (v5.4 rounds; the fetched value is the optical-depth-unity age, and recombination is spread over a range, so the difference is rounding, not a conflict).
7. Variable Principle applied: The factual core is measured and derived with stated uncertainties. Nothing is filled by assumption.
8. Model update (Capsule: what the failed parts contribute): No part failed. The item is integrated as is.
9. Documented: This file. Correspondence: one scale down, the same bind-and-release is atomic recombination of hydrogen measured in the laboratory (the Lyman series; not fetched here, named only); one scale up, the decoupled photons carry the primordial spectrum n_s (STAGE-002, STAGE-003).
10. Next baseline: Next CMB spectral-distortion missions (a PIXIE-class instrument) would tighten the mu and y limits; no scheduled date fetched. Container candidate (forced-fill, integrated).

## Forcing Test

- Test A (Ground): Grounded in reproduced measurements (FIRAS, WMAP-recalibrated FIRAS, literature combination) and the Planck fit.
- Test B (Uniqueness): A blackbody to < 50 ppm with acoustic peaks at one angular scale leaves one candidate: a relic of radiation that was in equilibrium with matter and then decoupled at z* ~ 1090.
- Test C (Direction): Forced: coupled (ionized) → decoupled (neutral) as temperature fell below ~3000 K.
- Test D (Falsifiability): Falsifiable by spectral distortions; tested and survived.
- **Outcome:** forced-fill.
