## Canon says

v5.1 Appendix A, row 8:

> **8 · Supernova Seeding** — "Heavy elements ejected into the interstellar medium. Required precondition for biology."

**Factual core graded here:** elements made in stars are ejected into the interstellar medium by supernovae, and this enrichment is a precondition for later chemistry (Stages 9–10).

## Scientific Inquiry run

1. Question (precise): Is ejection of stellar nucleosynthesis products into the interstellar medium by supernovae measured, and are supernovae the only such channel?
2. What an answer must look like: Direct detection of freshly made radionuclides in supernova remnants and in interstellar material reaching Earth; direct detection of other ejection channels, if any.
3. Falsifiability condition: No supernova-made radionuclides in remnants or interstellar dust; or all heavy elements traceable to one channel only.
4. Variables: measurable / bounded / held open: Measurable: 60Fe influx epochs; 44Ti in Cas A; Sr in a kilonova; >= 0.05 solar masses of merger ejecta. Held open: per-element fractions from each channel.
5. Test designed: Fetch 60Fe deep-sea data, a supernova-remnant radionuclide map, and kilonova element identification; compare channels with canon's single channel.
6. Data (unfiltered): Supernova ejection is measured (44Ti in Cas A; 60Fe from "supernova explosions and winds from massive stars" deposited on Earth in two epochs). A second channel is measured: a neutron-star merger made and ejected neutron-capture elements (Sr identified; >= 0.05 solar masses of heavy elements). Winds from massive stars are named by the 60Fe source as a third channel.
7. Variable Principle applied: Supernova ejection is measured (fills). The claim that supernovae alone seed heavy elements is not supported: at least two other channels are measured. The per-channel shares are held open (not fetched).
8. Model update (Capsule: what the failed parts contribute): The single-channel reading fails. The failure adds channels canon omits: compact-object mergers (r-process) and stellar winds. The stage stays, broadened.
9. Documented: This file. Correspondence: one scale down, individual radionuclide decays (60Fe, 44Ti) are the measured markers; one scale up, the enriched gas collapses into new systems (STAGE-009), where short-lived radionuclides in meteorites carry the same signal.
10. Next baseline: Next multi-messenger kilonova detections (gravitational-wave observing runs) and per-element site analyses. Re-review 2027-09-24.

## Forcing Test

- Test A (Ground): Supernova ejection: grounded (Cas A 44Ti; 60Fe on Earth). Merger ejection: grounded (GW170817 kilonova).
- Test B (Uniqueness): For "stellar products are ejected into the ISM and enrich it", one candidate survives. For "supernovae are the channel", more than one channel survives (supernovae, mergers, winds): canon's single channel is not unique.
- Test C (Direction): Forced: made in stars / mergers → ejected → mixed into the ISM → incorporated into later systems (60Fe reaches Earth).
- Test D (Falsifiability): Falsifiable and survived for enrichment; the single-channel reading is falsified by the merger data.
- **Outcome:** forced-fill for ejection-and-enrichment; the stage wording is incomplete (methodology-gap).

## Anti-Operation (the gap this opens)

Canon names one channel. Reality shows at least three (supernovae, massive-star winds, neutron-star mergers). Exclusion diagnosis **not-reached**: canon's log predates the 2017 kilonova and 2019 strontium identification; nothing in canon conflicts with them, it simply does not hold them. Gap opened: which channel supplies which element used by biology (held open; per-element fractions not fetched in this run).

## Narrative stripped (if any)

- "Required precondition for biology": the factual part (elements heavier than lithium, needed by biology, come only from stellar processing and ejection; the PDG BBN review lists primordial yields only up to 7Li, STAGE-005) is consistent with measurement. The framing of biology as the purpose of the step is not forced.
- **Label.** "Supernova Seeding" is observation-based but names one channel. Proposed label: "Stellar Ejecta Enrichment (supernovae, winds, compact-object mergers)".
