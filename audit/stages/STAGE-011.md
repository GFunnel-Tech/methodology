## Scientific Inquiry run
1. Question (precise): Did an RNA-based self-replicating system precede DNA/protein-based life on early Earth, and is RNA shown to be capable of catalysis and self-replication?
2. What an answer must look like: (a) measurements showing RNA catalysis and RNA-templated replication; (b) evidence dating or directly recording an RNA-only stage in Earth history.
3. Falsifiability condition: The historical claim would be weakened if no RNA system could copy sequences of its own length under any prebiotically plausible conditions, or if a non-RNA origin of heredity were demonstrated. Capability claims are falsified by failed replication of the cited experiments.
4. Variables: measurable: ribozyme activities, rates, product lengths (lab). Bounded: which chemistries are sufficient. Held open: whether this happened on early Earth, when, and whether it preceded compartments (Stage 12) or metabolism (Stage 15).
5. Test designed: retrieve primary papers for (i) RNA catalysis in the extant ribosome, (ii) ribozyme polymerase product length, (iii) self-sustained RNA replication, (iv) a competing metabolism-first result.
6. Data (unfiltered): table above. Ribosome catalysis by rRNA measured; engineered polymerase ribozyme reached 95 nt; cross-replicating ligase ribozymes amplify exponentially without protein, but from four supplied oligonucleotide substrates (not from mononucleotides). Non-enzymatic glycolysis-like chemistry also measured.
7. Variable Principle applied: RNA catalysis and replication capability = measured. RNA-world history = held open. Order relative to Stages 10, 12, 15 = held open.
8. Model update (Capsule: what the failed parts contribute): The factual core splits: capability is accounted; the historical stage is a hypothesis. Laboratory ribozymes are products of in vitro selection/engineering, so they bound what RNA can do, not what happened.
9. Documented: this file.
10. Next baseline: an RNA replicator that copies itself (not supplied oligonucleotides) from activated monomers under prebiotic conditions would narrow the variable.

## Forcing Test
- Test A (Ground): Partly grounded. RNA catalysis (ribosome) and RNA-driven replication (Lincoln & Joyce) are measured.
- Test B (Uniqueness): Fails. Metabolism-first and co-evolution scenarios survive the same data (e.g. the Keller 2014 non-enzymatic network).
- Test C (Direction): Not forced. The data do not fix whether replicators preceded compartments or metabolism.
- Test D (Falsifiability): Yes, with a falsifier: sustained failure to obtain RNA self-replication from prebiotically plausible monomers, or a demonstrated non-RNA route to heredity.
- **Outcome:** live-hypothesis.

## Anti-Operation (the gap this opens)
Canon orders Stage 11 (replicators) before Stage 12 (compartments) and Stage 15 (glycolysis). The order is held open in reality, so the stage sequence 10 → 11 → 12 → 13 is itself a variable canon does not mark. Also opened: whether "self-replicating" in canon means template-copying from monomers (not yet demonstrated) or ligation of supplied oligonucleotides (demonstrated).
