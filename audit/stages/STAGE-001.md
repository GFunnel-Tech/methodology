## Scientific Inquiry run

1. Question (precise): Is there any measurement that reaches a state prior to spacetime (Stage 1), or that constrains its properties ("no spacetime, no separation, no measurement")?
2. What an answer must look like: A measured quantity whose value depends on physics before the earliest constrained epoch, with an uncertainty; or a documented limit showing where measurement ends.
3. Falsifiability condition: The "held open" status would be falsified by an observable (e.g. a detected primordial tensor spectrum, or a quantum-gravity signature) that fixes the state preceding the earliest thermal epoch. Canon's positive claims ("foundation, not absence", "informationally saturated by all prior cycles") have no stated falsifier.
4. Variables: measurable / bounded / held open: Measurable: CMB at z* = 1089.92; light-element abundances back to t ~ 1 s. Bounded: reheating temperature > 4.7 MeV; tensor ratio r < 0.036; Planck scale 1.6e-35 m / 5.4e-44 s marks where tested theory ends. Held open: the state (if any) before the hot phase; whether "before" is meaningful; any "prior cycles".
5. Test designed: Collect the earliest-reaching measurements and limits from primary sources; check whether any reaches a pre-geometric state.
6. Data (unfiltered): Table above. The earliest direct photons are from z* ~ 1090; the earliest abundance probe reaches t ~ 1 s; data bound the reheating temperature only from below (4.7 MeV); primordial gravitational waves are not detected (r < 0.036). Nothing reaches a pre-spacetime state.
7. Variable Principle applied: Measurement ends at the earliest constrained epoch. The Stage 1 variable is held open. Canon's descriptions of it ("no spacetime", "foundation, not absence", "informationally saturated") are not measured and are not filled here.
8. Model update (Capsule: what the failed parts contribute): The test fails to reach Stage 1, and that failure is the result: it gives the edge of the log (z* for photons, t ~ 1 s for abundances, T_RH > 4.7 MeV for thermal history). Those edges are the measurable boundary around the held-open variable.
9. Documented: This file. Consistent with v5.4 §2 / §12 (first boundary proven-open). Correspondence: one scale down, the same limit appears as the Planck scale (no tested geometry below 1.6e-35 m, v5.4 §5 "Below Planck: proven-open"); one scale up, black-hole interiors are held open by the same missing theory (v5.4 §5).
10. Next baseline: Next data able to move the bound: improved tensor-mode limits (CMB B-mode experiments). Re-review 2027-09-24.

## Forcing Test

- Test A (Ground): No measurement grounds any positive property of Stage 1. Only its edge is grounded (earliest probes listed above).
- Test B (Uniqueness): Many candidates survive (no prior state, a prior contracting phase, a quantum-gravity state, eternal inflation). Measurement leaves more than one; none is forced.
- Test C (Direction): No measurement supplies a direction from the observed epochs back into Stage 1.
- Test D (Falsifiability): The canon wording (potential, saturation by prior cycles) carries no falsifier. The held-open status itself is falsifiable only by a future pre-thermal observable.
- **Outcome:** proven-open. Agrees with v5.4 §12. Not resolved here.

## Anti-Operation (the gap this opens)

The item names Stage 1 but canon has no stated observable that would mark its end. The gap: specify which measurement, if any, would count as the first datum of Stage 2 (for example a detected tensor mode, or a lower bound on reheating temperature above the QCD scale). Until then, Stages 1–3 share one undivided unmeasured interval.
