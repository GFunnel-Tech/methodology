## Canon says
v5.1 Appendix A, table "Stages 23–49 — ATP Through Recognition To Cycle Restart", row 25:

> **Multicellularity** — Cells stop competing and start specializing. Apoptosis becomes governable. Refusal to participate becomes cancer.

**Factual core (graded):** multicellular organisms arise in which cells cooperate and specialize; programmed cell death is regulated within the collective; cancer is a failure of that cooperation.

**Framework mapping (stripped):** none explicit beyond the placement of multicellularity as a single step in one linear sequence.

## Scientific Inquiry run
1. Question (precise): Does the stage's factual core (cooperation, specialization, regulated cell death, cancer as defection) match measurement, and is multicellularity one transition or many?
2. What an answer must look like: Measured phylogenetic and fossil evidence on the number and timing of origins, and experimental evidence on programmed cell death and cancer-like phenomena across taxa.
3. Falsifiability condition: The core fails if multicellular cells show no division of labour, if programmed cell death is absent from multicellular organisms, or if cancer-like phenomena bear no relation to failure of cellular cooperation.
4. Variables: measurable / bounded / held open: Measurable: presence of multicellularity in many lineages; PCD markers; cancer-like phenomena across taxa. Bounded: earliest multicellular fossils (≥ ca. 1.2 Ga for Bangiomorpha as originally reported; 2.1 Ga if the Francevillian interpretation holds). Held open: exact number of origins; a single date for 'the' onset.
5. Test designed: Retrieve primary abstracts on origins (phylogenetic), PCD in unicellular eukaryotes, cancer across the tree of life, and the oldest proposed multicellular fossils.
6. Data (unfiltered): Several independent origins in eukaryotes (Parfrey & Lahr 2013); complex multicellularity evolved independently multiple times and cancer = cheating on cooperation (Aktipis et al. 2015); apoptosis markers in yeast (Madeo et al. 1997); 2.1-Gyr Francevillian structures (contested); Bangiomorpha ca. 1200 Ma.
7. Variable Principle applied: The canon note states no date, so no date is filled. The onset date is held open between the contested 2.1 Gyr and the ca. 1.2 Ga taxonomically resolved fossil.
8. Model update (Capsule: what the failed parts contribute): Core is consistent. Two refinements: (a) multicellularity is a repeated transition, not one stage; (b) apoptosis-like death already exists in unicellular yeast, so 'becomes governable' means 'becomes regulated for the collective', not 'first appears'.
9. Documented: This file.
10. Next baseline: Multicellularity = repeated, convergent transition; cooperation/defection framing of cancer supported by review literature.

## Forcing Test
- Test A (Ground): Measurements exist: multicellular organisms, PCD, cancer-like phenomena are observed.
- Test B (Uniqueness): The cooperation/defection description is one of several accounts but is the one the cited review adopts; the single-stage framing is not unique — reality shows many parallel origins.
- Test C (Direction): Direction consistent: unicellular → multicellular in each lineage where it occurred.
- Test D (Falsifiability): Falsifier named in step 3.
- **Outcome:** forced-fill for the factual core (multicellular cooperation and its breakdown are observed). The single-row framing is a methodology gap: the log holds one transition where reality shows several.

## Anti-Operation (the gap this opens)
How should a linear stage table hold a transition that happened independently many times? Canon has no convention for parallel/convergent stages. Also open: the onset date (2.1 Ga contested vs ca. 1.2 Ga), and whether 'apoptosis becomes governable' should be restated since PCD markers exist in unicellular yeast.

## Narrative stripped
No Hermetic principle or Kingdom is assigned in this row. The ordinal placement (one step in a single chain) is the only interpretive load; reality does not force it — it shows convergent origins.
