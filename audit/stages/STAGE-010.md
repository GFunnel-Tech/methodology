## Canon says

v5.1 Appendix A, row 10:

> **10 · Prebiotic Chemistry** — "Amino acids, nucleotides, lipids assemble. Le Chatelier's Principle as molecular Garlick Equilibrium."

**Factual core graded here:** (a) amino acids, nucleotides (or their components) and lipid-like molecules form by abiotic chemistry; (b) this assembly occurred on the early Earth as the step leading to Stage 11 (RNA world).

## Scientific Inquiry run

1. Question (precise): (a) Do amino acids, nucleotide components, and lipid-like molecules form abiotically? (b) Is their assembly on the early Earth, leading to life, measured?
2. What an answer must look like: (a) detections in material never exposed to biology, with isotopic or chirality evidence of abiotic origin; (b) a geological record of the early-Earth chemistry.
3. Falsifiability condition: (a) no such molecules in pristine extraterrestrial material, or only terrestrial-contamination signatures. (b) A demonstrated route to life that does not pass through these building blocks.
4. Variables: measurable / bounded / held open: Measurable: amino acids (14/20 in Bennu), all five nucleobases (Bennu; three meteorites), ribose (13C-enriched), self-assembling amphiphiles. Held open: complete nucleotides abiotically; the early-Earth pathway; whether delivered or in-situ material mattered.
5. Test designed: Fetch meteorite and returned-sample organic analyses and a lab-simulation record; compare with canon's three molecule classes.
6. Data (unfiltered): Amino acids: measured in Murchison (1970) and in pristine Bennu samples (14 of 20). Nucleobases: all five in Bennu and in meteorites. Ribose: in meteorites. Lipid-like amphiphiles: in Murchison, forming vesicles. Nucleotides as assembled molecules: not in fetched sources. Chiral non-protein amino acids in Bennu are racemic or nearly so. No record of the early-Earth process itself.
7. Variable Principle applied: (a) is measured for amino acids, nucleobases, sugars and amphiphiles; "nucleotides" is only partly supported (components, not the assembled molecule). (b) is not measured: held open.
8. Model update (Capsule: what the failed parts contribute): Two failures give the edge: assembled nucleotides are not observed abiotically in these sources, and the early-Earth step has no direct record. Canon's "assemble" should separate "form abiotically (measured)" from "assemble toward life on early Earth (live hypothesis)".
9. Documented: This file. Correspondence: one scale up, the same abiotic organic chemistry occurs in asteroid parent bodies (Bennu, "aqueous reactions") and, per source, in cold molecular clouds (15N enrichment); one scale down, RNA-world self-replication (STAGE-011) would need the missing assembled nucleotides.
10. Next baseline: Further Bennu and Ryugu sample analyses (ongoing). Re-review 2027-09-24.

## Forcing Test

- Test A (Ground): (a) grounded in pristine returned samples and meteorites. (b) not grounded: no early-Earth record.
- Test B (Uniqueness): For (a), abiotic formation is the one surviving candidate for material never exposed to biology. For (b), several pathways survive (in-situ synthesis, delivery by impacts, both); not unique.
- Test C (Direction): Measurements give formation of building blocks but not the direction from them to replication.
- Test D (Falsifiability): (a) falsifiable and survived. (b) falsifier attached: a demonstrated route to life that bypasses these building blocks, or a geological record showing the early Earth lacked them.
- **Outcome:** live-hypothesis for the stage as the early-Earth step toward life; the abiotic formation of the building blocks is accounted.

## Anti-Operation (the gap this opens)

Canon lists "nucleotides" with amino acids and lipids as if all three were equally measured. Fetched data show nucleobases and ribose separately, not assembled nucleotides. Gap opened: the step from components to nucleotides (the input Stage 11 needs) has no measurement in this run. Second gap: chirality. Bennu's non-protein amino acids are racemic or nearly so, so the single-handedness of life's amino acids is not explained by this stage (the source's own conclusion).

## Narrative stripped (if any)

- "Le Chatelier's Principle as molecular Garlick Equilibrium": framework mapping. Le Chatelier's principle is a measured chemical regularity; its identification with the Garlick Equilibrium is interpretation. Reality neither forces nor contradicts the mapping. It does not change the grade.
- The label "Prebiotic Chemistry" presumes the chemistry preceded life on Earth, which is the hypothesis. It is the standard field name and is observation-compatible for (a); no change proposed.
