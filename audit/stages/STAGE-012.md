## Scientific Inquiry run
1. Question (precise): Do amphiphile membranes form compartments that grow, divide and hold nucleic acids, and did such protocells form on early Earth after an RNA stage?
2. What an answer must look like: lab measurements of vesicle self-assembly, growth, division, encapsulation; historical evidence of timing and order.
3. Falsifiability condition: Lab claims are falsified if they do not replicate. The historical claim would be weakened if plausible early-Earth conditions (salinity, divalent cations) were shown to prevent vesicle formation for all candidate amphiphiles.
4. Variables: measurable: vesicle behavior in lab. Bounded: amphiphile types (the model systems use single-chain fatty acids, not the phospholipid bilayers of extant cells). Held open: the historical event, its timing, and its order relative to Stages 11 and 13.
5. Test designed: retrieve primary lab studies of protocell growth and division.
6. Data (unfiltered): table above. Fatty-acid vesicles grow, divide, and carry RNA through cycles. Clay speeds vesicle formation.
7. Variable Principle applied: compartment capability = measured. Historical protocell = held open. "Lipid bilayer" as the first boundary = held open (models use fatty acids; Stage 13 in canon itself proposes mineral compartments first).
8. Model update (Capsule): capability is accounted; the historical stage and its order are a hypothesis. Canon's "first boundary" conflicts internally with Stage 13, which places proton gradients across mineral membranes. Reality does not decide between them.
9. Documented: this file.
10. Next baseline: a lab protocell with an RNA replicator coupled to membrane growth would narrow the variable.

## Forcing Test
- Test A (Ground): Partly grounded (vesicle growth and division measured).
- Test B (Uniqueness): Fails. Mineral-pore compartments (Stage 13's own premise) and fatty-acid vesicles both survive as candidates for the first boundary.
- Test C (Direction): Not forced. Order relative to Stage 11 not fixed.
- Test D (Falsifiability): Yes. Falsifier: a demonstration that no prebiotically available amphiphile forms stable vesicles under plausible early-Earth conditions.
- **Outcome:** live-hypothesis.

## Anti-Operation (the gap this opens)
Canon places Stage 12 (lipid boundary) before Stage 13 (mineral membranes at vents). In the vent hypothesis the mineral compartment comes first and lipid membranes later (Lane & Martin 2012, see STAGE-013). The two stages carry an ordering that canon does not mark as held open.

## Narrative stripped (if any)
- "Detection becomes possible because there is now a self" is a framework mapping (DETECT, self). Reality neither forces nor contradicts it.
- Label: "Lipid Bilayer" does not match the measured model systems (fatty-acid vesicles; bilayer vs multilamellar varies). "Protocell Formation" asserts a historical event. Proposed label "Amphiphile Vesicle Compartments (protocell models)" keeps to what is observed.
