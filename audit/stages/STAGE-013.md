## Scientific Inquiry run
1. Question (precise): Do alkaline hydrothermal vents present natural proton gradients usable for chemistry, and did life's chemiosmosis originate from them around 4 Ga?
2. What an answer must look like: (a) measured vent-fluid pH and temperature; (b) lab demonstration that such gradients drive biologically relevant reactions; (c) historical evidence linking early life to vents and dating it.
3. Falsifiability condition: Hudson 2020 names its own control: no product when the gradient is reduced. The historical claim would be weakened if Hadean oceans and vent fluids were shown to lack a pH contrast, or if phylogenetics placed chemiosmotic machinery late.
4. Variables: measurable: modern vent chemistry; lab reactivity. Bounded: LUCA metabolism (inferred acetogen, compatible with the hypothesis but not proof of it). Held open: the historical origin at vents, the date (~4 Bya), and the claim that the ETC "refines" this mechanism.
5. Test designed: retrieve the discovery paper for an alkaline vent field, a lab gradient-driven CO2-reduction result, the hypothesis paper, and LUCA metabolic inference.
6. Data (unfiltered): table above. Modern alkaline vents exist (Lost City pH 9.0–9.8). Lab pH gradients across Fe(Ni)S drive CO2 → formate. The hypothesis is stated as a proposal by its authors. LUCA inferred to be an anaerobic acetogen.
7. Variable Principle applied: (a) measured for modern vents; (b) measured-single in lab; (c) and (d) held open.
8. Model update (Capsule): canon writes a live origin-of-life hypothesis as a stage that happened. The measured part (natural gradients exist; they can drive chemistry) supports it without forcing it.
9. Documented: this file.
10. Next baseline: demonstration of gradient-driven synthesis beyond formate (e.g. to acetyl-level products) under vent-like flow, or Hadean ocean-pH constraints.

## Forcing Test
- Test A (Ground): Partly grounded (vent chemistry, lab reactivity).
- Test B (Uniqueness): Fails. Other origin settings (surface ponds, other vent types) are not ruled out by these data.
- Test C (Direction): Not forced. The data do not show that biological chemiosmosis descends from vent gradients.
- Test D (Falsifiability): Yes. Falsifier: evidence that Hadean alkaline vents lacked a pH gradient against seawater, or that gradient-driven CO2 reduction fails beyond formate under all vent-like conditions.
- **Outcome:** live-hypothesis.

## Anti-Operation (the gap this opens)
Row 21 states "alkaline vents 4 Bya" as a dated fact. Nothing in this run measures vents at 4 Ga, and LUCA's inferred age (~4.2 Ga, 4.09–4.33 Ga; STAGE-014) would place LUCA at or before that date, so the stage order 13 → 14 and the date are both open. Canon should mark the date and the lineage claim as held open.
