## Scientific Inquiry run
1. Question (precise): Does language transfer internal content between individuals, measurably?
2. What an answer must look like: Evidence that a speaker's neural state is reproduced in a listener through speech and that this tracks comprehension.
3. Falsifiability condition: Fails if listener brain activity is unrelated to speaker brain activity during successful communication.
4. Variables: measurable / bounded / held open: Measurable: speaker–listener coupling. Bounded: H. sapiens clade ≥ 315 ± 34 ka. Held open: date of language origin.
5. Test designed: Retrieve primary speaker–listener coupling data.
6. Data (unfiltered): Stephens et al. 2010: coupling present in successful communication, absent in failed communication, scales with comprehension.
7. Variable Principle applied: Canon states no date; the origin date stays open.
8. Model update (Capsule: what the failed parts contribute): Core integrated.
9. Documented: This file.
10. Next baseline: Language as measurable transfer of neural state.
