## Scientific Inquiry run

1. Question (precise): (a) Is the quark-gluon plasma → hadron conversion measured? (b) Is its occurrence in the early universe measured?
2. What an answer must look like: (a) reproduced collider measurements plus a lattice-QCD transition temperature; (b) a cosmological observable requiring a past temperature above ~156 MeV.
3. Falsifiability condition: (a) heavy-ion data explained fully by hadronic models with no deconfined phase. (b) Data requiring the universe's maximum temperature to lie below ~156 MeV would falsify a cosmic QGP epoch.
4. Variables: measurable / bounded / held open: Measurable: QGP signatures at RHIC and LHC; lattice T_c = 156.5 +/- 1.5 MeV. Bounded: maximum cosmic temperature > 4.7 MeV. Held open: whether the universe ever exceeded 156 MeV (the standard model says yes; data do not yet force it).
5. Test designed: Fetch heavy-ion reviews from two colliders, the lattice transition temperature, and the reheating-temperature bound; compare the lattice T_c with the highest temperature data force on the universe.
6. Data (unfiltered): Two colliders report QGP formation (RHIC 2005 with an explicit caution that evidence was not yet unequivocal; ALICE, 2009–2018, describes QGP as created and characterized). Lattice QCD gives a cross-over (not a sharp phase transition) at 156.5 +/- 1.5 MeV. Data bound the cosmic maximum temperature only from below at 4.7 MeV, about 33 times lower than T_c. Protons and neutrons exist (trivially observed).
7. Variable Principle applied: (a) is measured and accounted. (b) is a derivation that extrapolates lab physics past the earliest probe. It is not filled: the gap between 4.7 MeV and 156.5 MeV is held open.
8. Model update (Capsule: what the failed parts contribute): The failed part (no cosmic observable above ~MeV) gives a precise edge: cosmic hadronization is supported by extrapolation, and the bound T_RH > 4.7 MeV is where measurement ends. Canon's arrow "→" is consistent with the lattice result that the change is a smooth cross-over.
9. Documented: This file. Correspondence: one scale down, confinement inside each proton (no free quarks observed); one scale up, nuclei formed from these hadrons at STAGE-005.
10. Next baseline: A cosmological probe of the QCD epoch (for example a stochastic gravitational-wave background at nanohertz frequencies, if tied to the QCD epoch) would move (b). Re-review 2027-09-24.

## Forcing Test

- Test A (Ground): (a) grounded: two colliders, lattice QCD. (b) not directly grounded: no cosmic measurement reaches 156 MeV.
- Test B (Uniqueness): For (a) one candidate survives (deconfinement cross-over). For (b), the standard hot history is one candidate; a universe whose hot phase began between 4.7 MeV and 156 MeV also fits the fetched bounds. Two candidates survive.
- Test C (Direction): Cooling direction (QGP → hadrons) is forced by lab physics; the cosmic timing is not measured.
- Test D (Falsifiability): Both parts are falsifiable (see step 3). Falsifier attached for (b): a bound forcing T_max < 156 MeV.
- **Outcome:** live-hypothesis for the stage as a cosmic epoch; the lab process is accounted. Not forced-fill.

## Anti-Operation (the gap this opens)

The standard chronology treats a cosmic QCD epoch as settled, but the fetched data force a hot universe only down to ~5 MeV. The gap: canon Stages 2–4 sit inside an interval (above ~5 MeV) that no measurement reaches; the ordering Stage 3 → Stage 4 is derived, not observed. v5.4 §2's "quarks (10⁻⁶ s)" timing was not verified in this run (no primary source fetched states it); held open.
