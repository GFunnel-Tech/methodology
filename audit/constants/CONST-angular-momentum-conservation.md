## Scientific Inquiry run
1. Question (precise): What does measurement show for this conservation law, and are canon's symmetry pairing and role claims correct?
2. What an answer must look like: Theorem pairing; check of each example against 'isolated system'.
3. Falsifiability condition: Pairing wrong; or an isolated system whose angular momentum changes.
4. Variables: measurable / bounded / held open: Derived: pairing. Measured: electron properties (PDG). Held open: none.
5. Test designed: Literature fetch; example-by-example check.
6. Data (unfiltered): Pairing correct. Examples: galaxies — approximately isolated, accounted. Hurricanes — not isolated (surface friction, inflow), their rotation is sustained by continuous torque/energy input and they decay when it stops; 'rotate stably because of conservation' is not correct. Electrons — spin is intrinsic, not a body rotating. Tai Chi — a human movement practice; not a conservation-law case.
7. Variable Principle applied: No variable open.
8. Model update (Capsule: what the failed parts contribute): Law survives; examples partly wrong.
9. Documented: this file.
10. Next baseline: Re-run on the next PDG edition (2026 Review of Particle Physics) or on the named experiment's next result (Phase 9).

## Anti-Operation (the gap this opens)
Canon lists open, driven systems (hurricanes) as examples of a law that applies to isolated systems. The missing isolation condition is the gap.

## Narrative stripped (if any)
Removed: 'Tai Chi' as an example — a cultural practice listed next to physical systems; reality does not force the link.
