## Scientific Inquiry run
1. Question (precise): Does the law, as canon states it, match its measured form and domain of validity?
2. What an answer must look like: Measured tests of F = ma; the domain boundary.
3. Falsifiability condition: Deviation from F ∝ a in the low-speed domain.
4. Variables: measurable / bounded / held open: Measured: F = ma to 5×10⁻¹⁴ m s⁻². Held open: none within the domain.
5. Test designed: Literature fetch.
6. Data (unfiltered): Consistent in the classical domain. At speeds near c the correct law is F = dp/dt with p = γmv; F = ma is its low-speed limit. At atomic scales it is replaced by quantum dynamics (Ehrenfest's theorem recovers it for expectation values).
7. Variable Principle applied: No variable open.
8. Model update (Capsule: what the failed parts contribute): Law survives with its domain stated.
