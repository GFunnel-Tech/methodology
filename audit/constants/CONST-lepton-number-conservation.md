## Scientific Inquiry run
1. Question (precise): What does measurement show for this conservation law, and are canon's symmetry pairing and role claims correct?
2. What an answer must look like: Tests of total and family lepton number; the actual reason for electron stability.
3. Falsifiability condition: Observed L violation (0νββ); or electron stability traced to L.
4. Variables: measurable / bounded / held open: Measured: family-number violation in oscillations; limits on total L. Held open: whether total L is violated (Majorana neutrinos).
5. Test designed: PDG 2024; KamLAND-Zen 2024; MEG II 2023.
6. Data (unfiltered): Lepton *family* number is violated (neutrino oscillations, measured). Total lepton number: no violation seen (0νββ > 3.8×10²⁶ yr). Canon's row does not distinguish the two. 'Why electrons persist': the decay e⁻ → ν_e γ conserves lepton number, so lepton number does not protect the electron; charge conservation does (the electron is the lightest charged particle) — PDG lists the electron lifetime under charge conservation.
7. Variable Principle applied: Total-L violation held open (live-hypothesis: Majorana neutrinos), not filled.
8. Model update (Capsule: what the failed parts contribute): Canon's row gains two corrections: family-number violation is measured; electron stability belongs to charge conservation.
9. Documented: this file.
10. Next baseline: Re-audit on next 0νββ results (LEGEND-200/1000, nEXO) and MEG II final (Phase 9).

## Forcing Test
- Test A (Ground): Measurement.
- Test B (Uniqueness): Family number: ruled out as exact. Total L: open (two candidates, Dirac vs Majorana).
- Test C (Direction): Measurement → text. Reality is the authority (substrate item 1); the canonical wording is what is tested.

- **Outcome:** live-hypothesis (total L exact); forced-no (family L exact)
