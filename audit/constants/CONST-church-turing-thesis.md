## Anti-Operation (the gap this opens)

Canon's Framework Role silently upgrades a thesis about human/mechanical calculation into a claim about all physical processes. The physical Church–Turing thesis is an open empirical question the registry does not list.

## Narrative stripped

Removed: 'any process that can be performed at all can be performed by a Turing machine. The substrate is irrelevant. The algorithm is universal.' This is the physical Church–Turing thesis, which SEP describes as an empirical conjecture distinct from Church's and Turing's thesis. Reality does not force it; it is an over-claim.
