## Scientific Inquiry run
1. Question (precise): Does the law, as canon states it, match its measured form and domain of validity?
2. What an answer must look like: Tests of Maxwell's equations (massless, neutral photon) and their domain.
3. Falsifiability condition: A non-zero photon mass or charge.
4. Variables: measurable / bounded / held open: Measured: photon mass and charge limits. Held open: none classically.
5. Test designed: PDG listings; CODATA.
6. Data (unfiltered): Consistent: photon mass < 10⁻¹⁸ eV. 'Govern all electromagnetic phenomena' is too broad: quantum effects (photoelectric effect, Lamb shift, electron g−2) need quantum electrodynamics, of which Maxwell's equations are the classical limit; in media they need constitutive relations.
7. Variable Principle applied: No variable open.
8. Model update (Capsule: what the failed parts contribute): Equations survive; 'all phenomena' narrows to 'all classical phenomena'.
9. Documented: this file.
10. Next baseline: Re-run on the next PDG edition (2026 Review of Particle Physics) or on the named experiment's next result (Phase 9).
