## Scientific Inquiry run

1. Question (precise): Are Hox genes conserved across bilateria?
2. What an answer must look like: Presence and gene losses across lineages.
3. Falsifiability condition: Bilaterian lineages lacking Hox genes entirely.
4. Variables: measurable / bounded / held open: Measurable: Hox gene complement per genome. Bounded: conserved arrays with some losses. Held open: exact divergence age.
5. Test designed: Check review plus a known exception.
6. Data (unfiltered): Conserved arrays (Carroll 1995); losses and cluster breakup in Oikopleura (Seo 2004).
7. Variable Principle applied: 'Same genes across all bilateria' is filled only as 'homologous Hox genes, with lineage-specific losses'.
8. Model update (Capsule: what the failed parts contribute): Precision: 'homologous Hox genes across bilaterians, with lineage-specific losses'.
9. Documented: This file.
10. Next baseline: Integrated.

## Anti-Operation (the gap this opens)

## Narrative stripped

Removed: 'integration of every prior body-plan failure into the surviving lineage'. Conservation reflects shared descent and constraint; extinct lineages' genes are not integrated into survivors. Reality does not force the Capsule reading and is in tension with it.
