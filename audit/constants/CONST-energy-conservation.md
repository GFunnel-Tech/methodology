1. Question (precise): What does measurement show for this conservation law, and are canon's symmetry pairing and role claims correct?
2. What an answer must look like: The theorem that pairs energy with time-translation symmetry; the domain where that symmetry holds; a precision test.
3. Falsifiability condition: Pairing wrong; or a precision energy balance that fails.
4. Variables: measurable / bounded / held open: Derived: Noether pairing. Measured: energy balance closing at 10⁻⁷ (Rainville). Held open: a global energy for an expanding universe.
5. Test designed: Literature fetch (Noether translation; GR energy FAQ by Weiss & Baez; Rainville et al. 2005).
6. Data (unfiltered): Pairing correct (Noether). In GR the local form holds exactly; a global conserved energy exists only for spacetimes with a time-like symmetry — an expanding FLRW universe has none, so 'total energy of the universe is conserved' is not a well-defined statement there (e.g. photon energy redshifts with expansion). Canon states 'Energy persists' without that domain limit, and Layer I.E ('Energy Continuation') is built on it.
7. Variable Principle applied: Held open, not filled: whether 'energy persists' applies at cosmological scale depends on a definition canon does not give.
8. Model update (Capsule: what the failed parts contribute): The law survives in its domain. Canon's unrestricted form contributes the gap: energy conservation is exact where time-translation symmetry holds, and undefined globally where it does not.
9. Documented: this file.
10. Next baseline: Re-audit when Layer I.E is audited (Phase 5) — the domain limit bears directly on 'Energy Continuation'.
