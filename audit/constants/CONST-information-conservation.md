## Scientific Inquiry run
1. Question (precise): Is information conservation in black-hole evaporation measured, and did Hawking's reversal confirm it?
2. What an answer must look like: An observation of black-hole evaporation, or a statement that only theory exists.
3. Falsifiability condition: An observed Hawking-radiation spectrum carrying (or not carrying) the infalling information.
4. Variables: measurable / bounded / held open: Measured: none. Derived: theoretical arguments on both sides. Held open: the resolution.
5. Test designed: Literature fetch (Hawking 2005; Almheiri et al. review).
6. Data (unfiltered): No measurement. Hawking's 2005 paper is a theoretical argument (valid, by its own statement, only in asymptotically AdS spacetimes). The 2019–2021 'island' calculations reproduce a unitary Page curve semiclassically; the mechanism and the status in our (non-AdS) universe are still debated. Unitarity is a postulate of standard QM, so 'QM requires it' is correct *inside* QM — which is exactly the premise in question.
7. Variable Principle applied: Held open, as the coordinator and canon's own label ('Paradox') require. Not filled.
8. Model update (Capsule: what the failed parts contribute): The 'confirms framework prediction' clause fails: a theorist's revised argument is not confirmation, and the framework's prediction is not stated in testable form.
9. Documented: this file.
10. Next baseline: Re-audit on any analogue-gravity or theoretical consensus change (Phase 9).
