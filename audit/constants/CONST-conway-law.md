## Scientific Inquiry run

1. Question (precise): Do systems always mirror their builders' communication structure?
2. What an answer must look like: Rate of mirroring across studies.
3. Falsifiability condition: Classes of projects where mirroring fails.
4. Variables: measurable / bounded / held open: Measurable: organisational ties vs technical dependencies. Bounded: prevalent. Held open: mechanism.
5. Test designed: Use the 142-study review.
6. Data (unfiltered): Prevalent in firms; not supported in open collaborative projects.
7. Variable Principle applied: Universal form is not filled.
8. Model update (Capsule: what the failed parts contribute): Adjust: 'Systems tend to mirror their builders' communication structure; open collaborative projects are a documented exception.'
9. Documented: This file.
10. Next baseline: Adjusted.
