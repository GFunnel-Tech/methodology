## Scientific Inquiry run

1. Question (precise): Are there measured stable configurations that are neither an optimum nor an equilibrium?
2. What an answer must look like: A measured persistent regime, plus a rule that classifies it as optimization, equilibrium, or neither.
3. Falsifiability condition: Canon's: a third stable configuration.
4. Variables: measurable / bounded / held open: Measurable: persistent oscillatory regimes. Held open: the classification rule.
5. Test designed: Take a measured persistent non-stationary regime (sustained oscillation) and try to classify it with canon's two categories.
6. Data (unfiltered): The repressilator oscillates persistently; it is not a fixed point (so not an equilibrium in the ordinary sense), and nothing in the data shows it optimizes anything. Canon gives no rule to decide whether it is "the draw" (a moving equilibrium) or a third configuration.
7. Variable Principle applied: Held open. Canon's item lacks a category (or a rule) that measured oscillatory regimes require.
8. Model update (Capsule: what the failed parts contribute): The test cannot be completed without a classification rule; the gap is that canon names two stable configurations but dynamical systems show at least one more candidate type (sustained oscillation) that canon neither includes nor excludes.
9. Documented: This file. Correspondence: canon's own Rhythm principle describes oscillation; whether Rhythm's cycles count as "the draw" is not stated.
10. Next baseline: A rule stating whether sustained oscillations count as "the draw". Re-review 2027-09-24.

## Forcing Test

- Test A (Ground): Sustained oscillation is measured; the two-configuration claim has no ground.
- Test B (Uniqueness): Oscillation can be read as a third configuration or as a moving equilibrium; not forced either way.
- Test C (Direction): Measurement points toward needing a third category or a wider definition of "the draw".
- Test D (Falsifiability): Falsifier present but not operational without the classification rule.
- **Outcome:** live-hypothesis; methodology-gap on the missing category.

## Anti-Operation (the gap this opens)

If "the draw" is widened to include every persistent regime, the claim becomes true by definition. If it is kept narrow, sustained oscillations are a candidate counterexample. The gap is that choice.

## Narrative stripped (if any)

- "The constraint", "perfect process", "the draw" (chess framing): mappings; reality neither forces nor contradicts them.
- "No counter-example found across 10 process domains": the domains are the framework's own, not a sample searched for counterexamples.
