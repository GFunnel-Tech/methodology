## Scientific Inquiry run

1. Question (precise): Is there measured evidence, at each named level, that persistence depends on "observation", with observation defined independently of ordinary physical, biological, or social processes?
2. What an answer must look like: Per level, an operational definition of observation and a comparison of systems with and without it.
3. Falsifiability condition: Canon's: a complex system that continues with no observation at any level. Requires a way to identify the absence of observation.
4. Variables: measurable / bounded / held open: Measurable: decoherence rates vs environmental coupling. Held open: "observation" at biological (defined as differential survival), cognitive, civilizational, cosmological levels.
5. Test designed: Check what the measured quantum-level phenomenon is; check whether the higher-level definitions allow any system to lack observation.
6. Data (unfiltered): At the quantum level, what is measured is loss of interference through physical coupling. It occurs with no conscious observer and is described without one. The "collapse" reading is one interpretation; the measurement problem is open (v5.4 caution). At the biological level canon defines observation as differential survival, i.e. natural selection renamed; any persisting lineage satisfies it by definition.
7. Variable Principle applied: Held open. "Confirmed" at four levels is not supported: at the quantum level the measured effect is interaction; at the others observation is defined so that persistence implies it.
8. Model update (Capsule: what the failed parts contribute): If "observation" means "interaction", the claim is measured at the quantum level but no longer about observers. If it means a conscious act, it is unmeasured. Canon must choose; either choice is informative.
9. Documented: This file. Correspondence: CLAIM-001 (same misreading used as evidence for consciousness); CLAIM-014 condition (1).
10. Next baseline: An operational definition of observation per level. Re-review 2027-09-24.

## Forcing Test

- Test A (Ground): Quantum-level interaction effects are grounded; "observation" as load-bearing at every level is not.
- Test B (Uniqueness): Decoherence data are explained by interaction without observers; the observer reading is not forced.
- Test C (Direction): None toward observers.
- Test D (Falsifiability): Not operational: a system with "no observation at any level" cannot be identified, because every interaction or survival counts as observation.
- **Outcome:** live-hypothesis. "Confirmed at quantum, biological, cognitive, civilizational levels" overstates the evidence.

## Anti-Operation (the gap this opens)

The recursive stack ("load-bearing all the way up") also asserts that cognition requires civilizational and cosmological observation. That upward dependence has no stated test; it is a new open item.
