## Canon says

- Layer I.G: *"Do not delete the failure record — preserve it as load-bearing structure that informs every future decision."* The named mechanism: *"The slime mold retracts protoplasm from inefficient tubes **back into the network**."*
- Core Axiom 14: failures are load-bearing, not discarded; *"Nothing is lost."*
- v5.2 row 3: information is conserved as substrate across collapse (I.E ≡ I.G).
- Appendix D lists *Information Conservation [Black Hole Information Paradox]* among the conservation laws.
- **Author clarification (2026-09-28, substrate):** the claim is *"established on a quantum level via quantum superposition and structure stability."*

The clarification requires the claim to be split, because canon's text runs two different claims together:

- **(a) Physical persistence.** The information is not annihilated; stable structure carries records forward.
- **(b) Functional integration.** The failed path's information is retained *by the surviving system* and is load-bearing for what follows.

## Scientific Inquiry run

1. Question (precise): Does the author's quantum-level grounding establish canon's conservation claim — and if so, which of (a) and (b)?
2. What an answer must look like: for each of (a) and (b), a statement of whether established physics or measurement supports it, with the supporting or contradicting observation named.
3. Falsifiability condition: (a) is refuted by a demonstration that information is physically annihilated; (b) is refuted by a measured case where a system discards information and cannot retrieve it. Conversely (b) is supported by a measured case where a system demonstrably retrieves a discarded path's information.
4. Variables: measurable / bounded / held open: **Measurable:** the redundancy of environmental records (quantum Darwinism experiments); Landauer's bound; the fate of excised DNA. **Bounded:** unitarity, on everything except black-hole evaporation. **Held open:** the black-hole case; what "structure stability" denotes precisely.
5. Test designed: grade (a) against the quantum-information literature; grade (b) against the bacterial measurements from the Phase 6 sweep; then ask whether (a) entails (b).
6. Data (unfiltered): Unitarity holds for closed systems. Einselection picks out stable states; quantum Darwinism says the environment carries redundant copies of them, and this has now been observed in an engineered system. So (a) has genuine physical backing, and the author's two named ingredients map onto real constructs: superposition → unitary evolution; structure stability → einselected pointer states. **But every one of those results places the surviving record in the environment, not in the system**, and Landauer quantifies the cost of that export. Measurement shows no cellular mechanism that retrieves a discarded base. So (b) is untouched by (a).
7. Variable Principle applied: (a) moves from ungraded to **bounded** (established physics, with the black-hole case held open). (b) stays contradicted at every scale where it has been measured. Neither is filled by assumption; the entailment (a) → (b) is examined rather than granted.

## Forcing Test

- Test A (Ground): (a) borders the Gödel ground only at the black-hole case; otherwise within-system. (b) is within-system and measurable.
- Test B (Uniqueness): For (a), one reading survives: information is physically conserved and stable structure carries the record — but **in the environment**. For (b), two readings entered — "the system retains it" and "the universe retains it" — and measurement eliminates the first. They are not the same claim, which is the whole finding.
- Test C (Direction): The clarification runs bottom-up (quantum substrate → biology). That direction is legitimate, but it does not carry the functional claim upward: decoherence is precisely the loss of the system's access to what the environment now holds.
- Test D (Falsifiability): The falsifier for (b) fired — measured, reproduced, and in the domain canon calls its strongest Correspondence case. The falsifier for (a) did not fire.
- **Outcome:** **live-hypothesis** overall. **(a) bounded and supported** (established physics, one held-open case). **(b) forced-no as written** — but the honest restatement below is live and testable.

## Anti-Operation (the gap this opens)

If the conservation that holds is environmental rather than systemic, then **what does the surviving system actually carry forward?** That is now the framework's real question, and it is a better one: measurable, and not yet answered. Candidate answer the audit can state: *the system carries the **discriminating structure** — the machinery that distinguishes good from bad — not the discarded item.* A bacterium's proofreading domain is exactly that: the retained lesson of countless rejected nucleotides, none of which it keeps.

Second gap: grounding I.G on unitarity ties it to the black-hole information problem, which canon files as open. I.G cannot be firmer than its foundation.

## Narrative stripped (if any)

Two things are removed from the claim's graded core, and both survive elsewhere:

1. **"Nothing is lost"** as a statement about systems. It is true of the universe (bounded), false of any open system that discards — which is every cell measured. Kept as a physical claim, withdrawn as a functional one.
2. **The slime-mold image** ("retracts protoplasm *back into the network*") is doing rhetorical work the physics does not support: retraction into the network is exactly what decoherence does **not** do. The measured slime-mold behaviour ([LABEL-108](../labels/LABEL-108-layer-I.G.md)) is real; the generalisation to information conservation is the part that does not carry.
