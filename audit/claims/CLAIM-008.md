## Scientific Inquiry run

1. Question (precise): Is there measured evidence that energy gradients renew through collapse at every scale, including the cosmological, and is the "Resolved" status in Appendix B supported?
2. What an answer must look like: Measurements of gradient formation following gradient collapse at each scale; for the cosmological scale, data on the long-term fate of expansion.
3. Falsifiability condition: Canon's: a self-sustaining process without polarity establishment and collapse. Terms ("polarity", "self-sustaining") are not defined in measurable units.
4. Variables: measurable / bounded / held open: Measurable: present expansion history (Ω_m, dark-energy equation of state). Bounded: dark-energy evolution at 2.8–4.2σ (not settled). Held open: the far-future state; any renewal after it.
5. Test designed: Retrieve current cosmological constraints bearing on the long-term fate; check whether any measurement addresses renewal after heat death.
6. Data (unfiltered): Planck 2018 fits base ΛCDM; DESI DR2 prefers evolving dark energy at up to 4.2σ. Neither measures renewal. The future of expansion is itself not settled by data.
7. Variable Principle applied: Heat-death finality stays held open, as canon holds it. Appendix B's "Resolved" for the continuation mechanism is not supported by measurement and conflicts with canon's own Synthesis row "Total System Question Is Open". Local examples (stars, photosynthesis) are real but do not show renewal "at every scale".
8. Model update (Capsule: what the failed parts contribute): The search shows where measurement stops: the present expansion history. The "49 stages" evidence cannot count as counterexample search because the stages were written by the framework.
9. Documented: This file. Correspondence: the same claim appears as Axiom 12 (one scale up, principle) and Stage-level energy notes in Appendix A (one scale down; audited by the stage worker).
10. Next baseline: Next DESI data release and CMB/SNe updates on w0, wa. Re-review 2027-09-24.

## Forcing Test

- Test A (Ground): Local gradient formation after collapse is observed in specific systems; the universal and cosmological parts have no ground.
- Test B (Uniqueness): For the cosmological future, several candidates survive (ΛCDM asymptotic expansion, evolving dark energy, cyclic models); not unique.
- Test C (Direction): Current data (DESI) move the dark-energy question but not in a direction that decides renewal.
- Test D (Falsifiability): Falsifier present but not operational ("polarity establishment and collapse" has no measurement). Condition (2) of the revision list requires observing a "measurable cycle" end, which cannot be done from inside one cycle.
- **Outcome:** live-hypothesis; heat-death finality stays proven-open (canon's own status). Appendix B "Resolved" overstates the evidence.

## Anti-Operation (the gap this opens)

If dark energy evolves (DESI's preference), the long-term fate of expansion changes class. Canon's gradient-renewal claim should say which expansion histories it is compatible with, so that data on w0/wa can bear on it.
