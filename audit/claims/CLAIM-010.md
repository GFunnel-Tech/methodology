## Scientific Inquiry run

1. Question (precise): (a) Is the Feynman path integral an empirical confirmation that "prior path data" are integrated? (b) What does Physarum measurably do? (c) Is "full information" of failed biological paths carried forward?
2. What an answer must look like: (a) A measurement that the path-integral picture predicts and standard QM does not. (b) Measured behaviours. (c) Measured retention of lineage information after extinction.
3. Falsifiability condition: For (a): if the path integral is mathematically equivalent to standard QM, no measurement can confirm it over the alternatives. Canon's falsifier ("cannot be explained by integration of prior path data") is not operational: any system has a history that can be cited after the fact.
4. Variables: measurable / bounded / held open: Measurable: Physarum behaviour; archaic ancestry fractions; QED observables. Held open: "integration" at cognitive, civilizational, cosmological levels.
5. Test designed: Retrieve Feynman's own statement of what the formulation is; retrieve primary Physarum measurements; retrieve measured archaic-ancestry retention.
6. Data (unfiltered): (a) Feynman states the formulation is "mathematically equivalent to the familiar formulation". Its "paths" are alternative histories of one particle between two points, summed as amplitudes; they are not previously explored attempts whose data are stored. QED is precisely confirmed (a_e), but that confirms QED, not a path-integration mechanism. (b) Physarum does use its own past (Reid 2012: avoids explored areas) and builds efficient networks (Tero 2010). (c) Archaic lineages persist only partly (about 1–6%), with some regions purged: partial retention, partial removal, not "full information".
7. Variable Principle applied: The cognitive, civilizational and cosmological levels stay held open. The quantum "Confirmed" is graded: it misdescribes a calculational formalism as an empirical confirmation.
8. Model update (Capsule: what the failed parts contribute): What survives: the literal slime-mold behaviour (use of path history) is measured in Physarum. What fails: the quantum confirmation and "full information" in biology. The failed parts give a sharper claim: "some complex systems use records of past exploration", testable level by level.
9. Documented: This file. Correspondence: one scale up, CLAIM-011 (cosmological prior-cycle integration); one scale down, CLAIM-012 (information conservation). Layer ◇ 'Sharpest Remaining Edges' offers the path integral as an angle for formalizing Quantum–Correspondence; this finding bears on that too.
10. Next baseline: Level-by-level operational definition of "integration of prior path data". Re-review 2027-09-24.

## Forcing Test

- Test A (Ground): Grounded for Physarum; not for "every level". The quantum ground cited is a formalism.
- Test B (Uniqueness): Emergence without integration of failed paths (e.g. selection with loss, as the purged archaic regions show) survives as an alternative.
- Test C (Direction): Measurement runs against "full information" and against "confirmed at quantum".
- Test D (Falsifiability): Not operational as written.
- **Outcome:** live-hypothesis. The quantum "Confirmed (Feynman 1948; QED)" conflicts with Feynman's own statement of equivalence (derived, not measured-reproduced, so intake stays open rather than adjusted).

## Anti-Operation (the gap this opens)

If "integration" is weakened to "partial use of past records", it becomes testable and is measured in Physarum, but it no longer supports Axiom 14's "full information" or the theological reading in Layer I.G. The gap: canon must choose the weak (testable) or strong (unmeasured) version.
