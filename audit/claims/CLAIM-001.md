> **Claim:** "Three Kingdoms — consciousness is irreducible". **What Would Disprove It:** "If consciousness were conclusively demonstrated identical to its physical substrate with no irreducible observer, Mentalism is wrong." **Current Evidence:** "Seven independent traditions converge on irreducibility. Quantum observer effect provides physics-level evidence." **Status:** "Bounded — held with convergence support."

v5.1 Layer I ('The Variable' row): "Which position is correct is not empirically resolved. Mentalism has convergence evidence from seven independent traditions plus quantum observer effect research. Materialism holds default status in current scientific consensus."

Factual core graded here: **the evidence statement** that the quantum observer effect is physics-level evidence for an irreducible (conscious) observer, and that the irreducibility question has measured support. The irreducibility claim itself is not resolved here.

## Scientific Inquiry run

1. Question (precise): Does the physics called the "quantum observer effect" supply evidence that an irreducible conscious observer exists, and does any measurement bear on whether consciousness is irreducible?
2. What an answer must look like: A measurement in which a quantum outcome depends on the presence of a conscious observer as distinct from a physical interaction; or a measurement that discriminates reducible from irreducible consciousness.
3. Falsifiability condition: The evidence statement fails if the "observer effect" is produced by physical interaction with no conscious observer present. Canon's falsifier for the main claim ("conclusively demonstrated identical to its physical substrate") names no measurement that could deliver it; as written it cannot fire.
4. Variables: measurable / bounded / held open: Measurable: decoherence rates under controlled environmental coupling. Bounded: neural correlates of conscious content (Cogitate). Held open: whether consciousness is reducible (the hard problem).
5. Test designed: Retrieve primary decoherence experiments and check what system caused the loss of interference; retrieve the most recent large preregistered test of consciousness theories.
6. Data (unfiltered): In all three decoherence experiments the "observer" is a physical environment (gas molecules, emitted thermal photons, an engineered reservoir). No experiment requires or measures a conscious observer. The consciousness test challenges both leading theories and does not address reducibility.
7. Variable Principle applied: The irreducibility variable stays held open. The evidence statement is not held open: it describes physics incorrectly and is graded against measurement.
8. Model update (Capsule: what the failed parts contribute): The failed evidence statement shows where the framework's Mentalism support leans on a misreading of quantum measurement. What survives: irreducibility as a live hypothesis with no measured support in either direction. Note v5.4's own required caution on the measurement problem: which interpretation of measurement is correct is open, but decoherence by environment without a conscious observer is measured.
9. Documented: This file. Correspondence: one scale up, the same misattribution recurs in Extended row 2 (CLAIM-009, "Observation ... confirmed at quantum"); one scale down, the Doc Variable Registry "Tightened toward foundational" status (CLAIM-015) rests partly on this evidence.
10. Next baseline: Further Cogitate-style adversarial tests; re-review 2027-09-24.

## Forcing Test

- Test A (Ground): Grounded for the evidence statement: measured decoherence without conscious observers (three groups). Not grounded for irreducibility: no measurement.
- Test B (Uniqueness): For irreducibility, several positions survive (physicalist identity, emergence, dual-aspect, idealism); none eliminated.
- Test C (Direction): Measurement runs against the evidence statement; it gives no direction on irreducibility.
- Test D (Falsifiability): The main claim's falsifier is not operational ("conclusively demonstrated identical" names no instrument or threshold). The evidence statement was falsifiable and fails.
- **Outcome:** live-hypothesis for "consciousness is irreducible" (not resolved here). The evidence statement "Quantum observer effect provides physics-level evidence" is contradicted by measured-reproduced decoherence and is proposed for removal (intake: adjusted).

## Anti-Operation (the gap this opens)

With the physics evidence removed, the row's only support is textual convergence. The gap: canon has no stated observable that would move this variable in either direction. A usable falsifier would name a measurement (for example, a result that a physical system's behaviour differs from its physics-only prediction only when a conscious subject is present). Until one is named, the "Bounded" status has no measurable boundary.

## Narrative stripped (if any)

- "Three Kingdoms" and "Mentalism" are framework mappings; reality neither forces nor contradicts them.
- "Seven independent traditions converge": convergence of doctrines is not a measurement of consciousness, and the traditions' independence is not demonstrated. It cannot bound a physical variable.
- "Observer" in quantum mechanics names a measuring interaction. The word does not refer to a conscious subject in any of the experiments above.
