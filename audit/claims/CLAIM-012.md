## Scientific Inquiry run

1. Question (precise): Is preservation of every path's information measured in cognitive and biological substrates, and is it guaranteed in physics?
2. What an answer must look like: Measurements of how much information from past learning (cognitive) or extinct lineages (biological) remains; the physics status of information conservation.
3. Falsifiability condition: Canon's: information of failed exploration genuinely deleted rather than redistributed. At the level canon cites (biological, cognitive), deletion from that substrate is measurable as loss of retention.
4. Variables: measurable / bounded / held open: Measurable: savings over time; archaic ancestry fractions; heat of erasure. Held open: whether microscopic information is preserved in all processes (unitarity, black holes).
5. Test designed: Retrieve reproduced forgetting-curve data; retrieve measured archaic-ancestry retention; retrieve physics status.
6. Data (unfiltered): Savings fall from about 0.44–0.58 at 20 minutes to about 0.04–0.26 at 31 days, in four subjects across three studies: most of the learned information becomes unavailable to the cognitive substrate, while some persists. Archaic ancestry persists at a few percent, with some regions purged. Erasing a bit moves heat and entropy into the environment (it does not vanish, but it is no longer information in the memory). Physics-level conservation is a theoretical principle whose black-hole case is an open problem.
7. Variable Principle applied: The physics-level question stays held open. The cognitive and biological "Confirmed" is graded against measurement and fails: those substrates lose most of the information measured.
8. Model update (Capsule: what the failed parts contribute): What survives is partial retention (savings > 0 at 31 days; archaic ancestry at a few percent). The claim "every path's data preserved" is replaced by a measurable one: "some information from past paths is retained; the fraction is measurable per substrate". "Redistributed" at the physical level (Landauer) is a different claim from "preserved in the substrate that explored".
9. Documented: This file. Correspondence: one scale up, CLAIM-019 (suffering tightening rests on this claim); one scale down, CLAIM-010 (biological row of the slime-mold stack).
10. Next baseline: Canon should state at which level (physical microstate vs biological/cognitive substrate) conservation is claimed. Re-review 2027-09-24.

## Forcing Test

- Test A (Ground): Loss and partial retention are grounded by reproduced measurement; universal preservation is not.
- Test B (Uniqueness): "Partial retention" is forced over "full preservation" at the cognitive level; at the physics level several positions survive.
- Test C (Direction): Measurement runs against "Confirmed in biological and cognitive substrates".
- Test D (Falsifiability): The falsifier fired at the cognitive level (information deleted from the substrate that learned it, measured-reproduced). It can be escaped only by moving the claim to the physical microstate level, where it is unmeasured.
- **Outcome:** live-hypothesis at the physics level (not resolved). The evidence "Confirmed in biological and cognitive substrates" and the statuses "guaranteed" / "confirmed" are contradicted; intake: adjusted (downgrade to "partial retention measured; full conservation held open").

## Anti-Operation (the gap this opens)

If conservation is claimed only at the physical microstate level, it no longer supports Layer I.G's theological reading ("the being who suffered ... contributed integration"), because microstate information dispersed as heat is not information any later being can use. The gap: canon must say which kind of conservation its downstream claims need.
