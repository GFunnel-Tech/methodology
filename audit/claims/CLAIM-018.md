## Scientific Inquiry run

1. Question (precise): Were the seven Hermetic principles articulated about 2,000 years before quantum mechanics?
2. What an answer must look like: Dated primary records of the articulation and of quantum mechanics.
3. Falsifiability condition: The "2,000y" statement fails if the enumerated principles first appear within decades of quantum mechanics.
4. Variables: measurable / bounded / held open: Measurable: publication dates. Held open: whether older texts contain the same principles in substance; whether mathematics is discovered.
5. Test designed: Retrieve bibliographic records of The Kybalion and of Heisenberg 1925.
6. Data (unfiltered): The Kybalion, which enumerates and names the seven principles, is dated 1908 in two independent library copies and has a 1908 LCCN. Matrix mechanics: 1925. Gap: 17 years. Canon's own Glossary gives 1908.
7. Variable Principle applied: The date is measured and graded. Whether older traditions held the same principles in substance is held open (not verified here). The mathematics variable stays open.
8. Model update (Capsule: what the failed parts contribute): The "2,000 years" figure is contradicted for the enumerated principles and is internally inconsistent with canon's Glossary. The "cross-substrate convergence" argument loses its time gap, and a 1908 text is not independent of the physics and philosophy of its era; the convergence cannot count as independent detection.
9. Documented: This file. Correspondence: the same "thousands of years" framing supports CLAIM-001 ("seven independent traditions").
10. Next baseline: If canon means older Hermetic sources, cite a dated primary text that states the principles. Re-review 2027-09-24.

## Forcing Test

- Test A (Ground): Dates grounded by bibliographic records.
- Test B (Uniqueness): The 1908 date is unique for the enumeration; the substance question has several candidates.
- Test C (Direction): Measurement contradicts "2,000y" for the enumerated principles.
- Test D (Falsifiability): The date claim was falsifiable and fails. The discovered/invented tightening has no falsifier.
- **Outcome:** proven-open for discovered vs invented (not resolved). The stated basis for "Tightened toward Discovered" is contradicted by measured-reproduced dates; intake: adjusted (replace "2,000y" with "1908 enumeration; older-source link held open", and drop the tightening's basis).
