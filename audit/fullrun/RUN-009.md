## Canon says

**The algorithm** (v5.1 Algorithm Index, Layer II). *Use when: diagnosing why a system, relationship, project, or process is stuck, failing, or producing unintended results.*

> 1. Run the seven-principle audit. For each of the seven principles — Mentalism, Correspondence, Vibration, Polarity, Rhythm, Cause and Effect, Gender — ask: is this principle being honored or violated?
> 2. Mentalism: is the system's output traceable to a clear Third Kingdom intent? Or is it First Kingdom reactivity dressed as intent?
> 3. Correspondence: is the same algorithm running at every scale of the system, or are sub-scales operating by contradictory rules?
> 4. Vibration: are the frequencies of components aligned, or is there frequency mismatch causing dissipation?
> 5. Polarity: is the system trying to eliminate one pole rather than recognize that opposites are degree variations along one axis?
> 6. Rhythm: is the system fighting its natural cycles or honoring them?
> 7. Cause and Effect: are causes being set deliberately, or are effects being chased after the fact?
> 8. Gender: is creative work integrating Masculine projection with Feminine reception, or is one principle missing?
> 9. The principle showing the deepest violation is the location of the systemic failure. Apply the corresponding principle's practical formula to correct.

**The layer** presents the seven as *"Observable, testable, convergent with independent discovery across physics, biology, systems theory, civil engineering, philosophy, chess, and business"* and *"Presented not as mystical assertions but as structural laws."*

## Algorithm run (literal, step by step) — target: a bacterial cell (*E. coli* K-12)

1. **Run the seven-principle audit.** Ran all seven below. *Produced:* seven answers, none of which a measurement selects — see step 9 and the falsifier column in the Anti-Operation table.
2. **Mentalism — "is the output traceable to a clear Third Kingdom intent, or First Kingdom reactivity dressed as intent?"** `BLOCKED: no measurement distinguishes the two alternatives.` The measured facts (chemotaxis responds to a rate of change; growth-rate-dependent proteome allocation) are compatible with either answer, and nothing in a bacterium is measurable as intent. Canon's cell row ("the homeostatic intent to maintain life drives all cellular processes") additionally mis-describes the best-measured bacterial control loop, which has **no set-point at all** — chemotaxis regulates a derivative (F-071). *Produced:* nothing decidable.
3. **Correspondence — "is the same algorithm running at every scale, or are sub-scales operating by contradictory rules?"** Ran against canon's own scales. *Produced:* **contradictory rules, measured.** Canon's instruments are level controllers (Garlick Equilibrium, BEAS, Domain 9 step 1 "a homeostatic set-point for each measurable variable"); the bacterium's best-measured sensory loop controls a *rate of change*, and its size homeostasis is an **adder** (constant added size) rather than a critical size. Two measured sub-scale rules that canon's higher-scale rule does not contain. So the audit returns "violated" — of canon's own Correspondence claim, not of the cell.
4. **Vibration — "are the frequencies of components aligned, or is there frequency mismatch causing dissipation?"** *Produced:* the factual half of canon's cell row is confirmed — a bacterium does have measured vibrational frequencies (~730 cm⁻¹ SERS band, isotope-shifted). But the question asked is about *alignment* of component frequencies, and `BLOCKED: canon names no frequency pair in a cell that could be aligned or mismatched, and no dissipation quantity to measure.` Molecular vibrations at 730 cm⁻¹ are not "tuned" to each other; Raman/SERS band positions are set by bond masses and force constants. The confirmed part is chemistry, not the layer.
5. **Polarity — "is the system trying to eliminate one pole?"** *Produced:* no — anabolism and catabolism both run, which is correct and true of every living cell by definition. The one row where canon states a testable consequence — *"Balance = health"* — is contradicted where a number exists: NADPH formation is 20-50% **above** anabolic demand at all dilution rates in healthy, growing cells. Measured persistent imbalance with no loss of health.
6. **Rhythm — "is the system fighting its natural cycles or honoring them?"** *Produced:* the cell-cycle half of canon's row is confirmed and measured (the adder). The **circadian** half is not: as of the first report of a clock in any non-photosynthetic bacterium (2021), circadian clocks were "uncharacterized in nonphotosynthetic bacteria", the reported clock is in *B. subtilis*, and *E. coli* generation times run from 20 minutes to 20 hours — it does not have, and mostly cannot have, a 24-hour cycle. "All cellular processes oscillate" is also false as stated: crowding-driven nucleoid phase separation is a free-energy minimum, not an oscillation (SWEEP-031).
7. **Cause and Effect — "are causes being set deliberately, or are effects being chased after the fact?"** `BLOCKED: "deliberately" has no measurable referent in a bacterium.` The gradable clause is canon's cell row, *"No randomness. Only unmapped causation."* Measurement separates intrinsic from extrinsic noise in single *E. coli* cells and shows low copy numbers **fundamentally limit** the precision of gene regulation. As an empirical statement about the cell the row is false; as a metaphysical statement ("the randomness is only unmapped") it is unfalsifiable, because any measured noise can be redescribed as unmapped causation. Either reading fails the utility test — one by contradiction, the other by vacuity.
8. **Gender — "is creative work integrating Masculine projection with Feminine reception, or is one principle missing?"** *Produced:* "both present", trivially, since the cell has DNA and ribosomes. Canon's specific pairing is contradicted in detail: the nucleoid is *defined* by ribosome depletion, so the two elements canon pairs as a couple are measurably segregated into different phases of the same cytoplasm, and the ribosome-rich phase is what compacts the DNA. `BLOCKED: no measurement could have shown a cell "missing" one of two categories that are assigned after the fact`, since any component can be sorted into active or receptive by inspection.
9. **"The principle showing the deepest violation is the location of the systemic failure."** `BLOCKED: no depth ordering exists.` The algorithm has no scale on which violations are deep or shallow, and the target is not failing — *E. coli* K-12 is the most successful laboratory organism on record. Run against a working system the algorithm returns no location, and the three answers that *did* come out "violated" (steps 3, 5, 6) are violations of canon's statements about the cell, not of the cell.

## Scientific Inquiry run

1. Question (precise): Run against a bacterial cell, does the Seven Hermetic Principles Algorithm produce a correct, non-trivial statement that measurement confirms and that the seven-principle structure — not general biology — supplied? And is canon's own claim that a cell instantiates all seven true row by row?
2. What an answer must look like: for each of the seven, (a) a measured bacterial feature the principle picks out, and (b) an observation that would have made the principle's answer come out the other way. A principle with (a) but no (b) is vacuous; a principle whose (a) is measured false fails.
3. Falsifiability condition: this record's `vacuous` grade fails if any one of the seven answers is shown to be determined by a measurement — i.e. if some observation about *E. coli* would flip it. The `conflict` state fails if the three contradicted rows (Rhythm's circadian clause, Cause & Effect's "no randomness", Gender's DNA-ribosome pairing) are shown consistent with measurement.
4. Variables: measurable / bounded / held open: **Measurable** — vibrational band positions, added size Δ and λ, doubling times, intrinsic vs. extrinsic noise, NADPH excess, nucleoid/ribosome phase separation, chemotactic derivative control. **Bounded** — *E. coli* doubling time 20 min to 20 h, bounding away from a 24 h cycle. **Held open** — Mentalism (no measurement of intent, per canon's own Layer I Part IX); any criterion for "honored" or "violated".
5. Test designed: run steps 1-9 literally on *E. coli* K-12; for each principle, name the measured feature it picks out and then name the observation that would have falsified it. Grade `vacuous` wherever the second is empty.
6. Data (unfiltered): the table above, plus the step-by-step results. Three of canon's seven cell rows are contradicted in detail (Rhythm: no *E. coli* circadian clock, and crowding-driven organisation does not oscillate; Cause & Effect: measured intrinsic noise with copy-number-limited precision; Gender: the paired components are spatially segregated by ribosome depletion). Two are confirmed but supplied by chemistry and cell biology, not by the layer (Vibration's bond frequencies; Polarity's simultaneous anabolism/catabolism). One returns "violated" against canon itself (Correspondence). One is unmeasurable (Mentalism).
7. Variable Principle applied: "Honored or violated" is held open for all seven, because no measured criterion exists. Mentalism stays held open, not resolved either way. The confirmed halves are recorded as confirmed and attributed to their real source (vibrational spectroscopy; metabolic biochemistry), not credited to the layer. No principle was stretched to fit: where the answer was undecidable it is marked `BLOCKED`, and the two hardest — Mentalism and Gender — are graded on that basis rather than on a plausible reading.
8. Model update (Capsule: what the failed parts contribute): the failure is informative and locatable. (i) Canon's cell table is checkable and three rows of it are wrong for a bacterium; fixing them costs canon nothing structurally — delete "Circadian rhythm" and "All cellular processes oscillate" from the Rhythm row, replace "No randomness" with the measured statement that molecular noise is intrinsic and copy-number-limited, and drop the DNA-ribosome pairing (SWEEP-031 already shows why). (ii) The algorithm's defect is different and not repairable by editing rows: steps 2-8 are questions with no measured answer criterion, so step 9's "deepest violation" has no scale. To become an instrument the layer needs, for each principle, one named quantity and one direction. (iii) The one genuinely productive output was negative: step 3 (Correspondence) located a **real, measured** mismatch between canon's level-control instruments and the bacterium's derivative control — the algorithm caught canon, which is the only non-trivial thing it did.
9. Documented: this file. Correspondence: one scale down, each principle is used as a mapping in stage and domain notes and is graded there under "Narrative stripped"; one scale up, Layer II supplies the "law, not analogy" warrant used across the framework, so a vacuous diagnostic here propagates to every "Per Correspondence" argument. ## Forcing Test

- Test A (Ground): Grounded for every row in the table; ungrounded for the algorithm's own verdicts, which rest on no measurement.
- Test B (Uniqueness): Not unique. Any complete-sounding checklist of categories (four elements, five forces, three gunas) would return "all present, none decisively violated" on a bacterium for the same reason: the categories are assigned after inspection. Canon's own text concedes the interchangeability ("Polarity IS the Yin/Yang principle restated").
- Test C (Direction): Only one direction was produced, and it points at canon: measured derivative control and the measured adder show sub-scales running rules canon's higher scales do not contain. No direction was produced about the bacterium that measurement did not already supply.
- Test D (Falsifiability): The algorithm as a whole: no. Individual cell rows: yes, and three fired — the circadian clause, "No randomness", and the DNA-ribosome pairing were each falsifiable and each came out false for *E. coli*.
- **Outcome:** `forced-no` for the claim canon states in Layer III — that a cell instantiates all seven principles as that table describes them. Three of the seven rows are contradicted by measurement in *E. coli*, so the table is false as written. The weaker claim that seven categories can be *applied* to a cell survives, and is `vacuous`: it could not have come out false.

**The gaps this opens.** (i) Canon has no rule for what one falsified row costs a "structural law" — the same gap F-073 found for "zero exceptions". Three rows of the cell table are now measured false in the one organism canon calls the highest expression of process density, and nothing in v5.1-v5.4 says what follows. (ii) The algorithm's only productive output was a diagnosis *of canon*. If the instrument's best use is auditing the framework that contains it, that is worth recording but it is not what step 9 claims. (iii) Grading each principle separately (as this run did) is outside the audit schema, which grades one item per file; the schema has no record type for "an instrument with seven parts of differing status".

## Narrative stripped (if any)

- Kingdom assignments ("Third Kingdom intent", "First Kingdom reactivity"), Masculine/Feminine labels, and Yin/Yang links: interpretations. Reality neither forces nor contradicts them, and they are not what the grade rests on.
