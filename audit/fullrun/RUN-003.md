## Canon says

Layer I.B's algorithm (v5.1 line 2270), quoted in full:

> Use when: building any infrastructure (organizational, civic, technical, personal) at scale.
> 1. Identify what role each of the four pillars plays in this infrastructure: Correctness, Complexity, Patience, Resilience.
> 2. Map your current infrastructure to each pillar. **Score each pillar honestly.**
> 3. Locate the weakest pillar. **The infrastructure fails at exactly the point of weakest pillar adherence.**
> 4. Identify what completing that pillar would require. Be specific.
> 5. Build the missing pillar component before scaling further. **Per Patience: defer growth until the pillar is in place.**
> 6. Re-score after completion. Move to the next-weakest pillar.
> 7. Continue until all four pillars score at acceptable levels for current scale. Then scale.

Same law at a different scale.**" The pillars were derived from civil evidence: Roman concrete versus Portland cement (Correctness, "process correctness compounds forward"); the Interstate system versus multi-modal European cities (Complexity, "oversimplification creates the gaps that become… predictable failure points"); the New Orleans levees (Patience, "**Impatience never reduces total cost. It defers cost with interest**"); ERCOT February 2021 (Resilience, "the business whose fulfillment, technology, or revenue depends entirely on a single vendor, platform, or person has built the Texas grid. **The question is only when, not if**").

## Algorithm run (literal, step by step, subject = a bacterial cell, *E. coli* K-12)

**Step 1 — "Identify what role each of the four pillars plays in this infrastructure: Correctness, Complexity, Patience, Resilience."**
Produced, for all four. This is the Correspondence test the task names, so each is reported with what it picked out and whether it required stretching.

- **Correctness — picks out a real, measured feature, no stretching.** *E. coli* buys the stability of its code with three serial discrimination stages: base selection (2×10⁵–2×10⁶), exonucleolytic proofreading (40–200 fold), postreplicative mismatch repair (20–400 fold), for a derived 1.6×10⁸–1.6×10¹¹ fold total, yielding ~1×10⁻³ mutations per genome per generation. Canon's Pillar I claim that "process correctness compounds forward" has a literal molecular counterpart: the stages **multiply**. This is the closest thing to a genuine hit in the run.
- **Complexity — picks out a real, measured feature, no stretching.** The cell maintains simultaneous alternative routes rather than one optimised route: many dehydrogenases and terminal reductases linked by three quinones, isoenzymes on both donor and acceptor sides, H⁺/e⁻ anywhere from 0 to 4 depending on the route taken, switched by FNR over 1–5 mbar O₂ and by ≥62 two-component systems. And the measured selection rule is *not* maximal efficiency: under O₂ the non-coupling dehydrogenases are used preferentially. Canon's "oversimplification creates the gaps that become predictable failure points" has a real counterpart.
- **Patience — requires stretching, and the pillar's stated direction is measurably reversed for the dominant case.** Canon's Patience is deferral of growth to protect the system. *E. coli*'s best-measured allocation decision goes the other way: above λ_ac ≈ 0.76 h⁻¹ it excretes acetate, throwing away usable carbon and energy to grow faster, threshold-linear with a slope unchanged (≈10 mM/OD) under perturbations that move the threshold by a factor ≈3.7, for the directly measured reason that respiration costs more proteome than fermentation. That is impatience as the measured optimum. Occupants of the pillar *as canon states it* do exist — the 1.1%-of-proteome inactive-ribosome reserve rising above 20% at slow growth, and NADPH formation held 20–50% above anabolic demand — so the pillar can be filled either way, which is the problem: both a feature and its opposite score.
- **Resilience — picks out a real, measured feature, with a measured exception canon's phrasing forbids.** Layered independent defence is real (restriction–modification plus adaptive CRISPR, the former worth ~10⁴-fold on plating efficiency in the comparator organism), as is route redundancy in respiration. But canon's Pillar IV says a system with a single-point dependency "has built the Texas grid. The question is only when, not if." *E. coli* is built out of non-redundant single points — FtsZ is rate-limiting for constriction, one PilT complex powers one retraction event — and has persisted through an enormous number of generations. Essential non-redundancy is the normal condition of the cell, not a pathology.

**Result of step 1: three of four pillars land on real measured features without stretching; one (Patience) has its direction reversed for the dominant measured case. But see the grading question below — nothing observable about a living cell could have made "these four roles are played here" come out false.**

**Step 2 — "Map your current infrastructure to each pillar. Score each pillar honestly."**
`BLOCKED:` canon supplies no scale, no units, no scoring procedure, and no threshold for any pillar, here or anywhere in v5.1. "Honestly" is not a method. The four candidate quantities measurement does supply — a discrimination factor (dimensionless, 10⁸–10¹¹), a count of alternative routes (dimensionless, order 10), a reserve fraction of proteome (1.1%–>20%), a redundancy multiplicity (1 for FtsZ and PilT) — are in four incommensurable units, so even with all four in hand there is no operation that ranks them. This is the load-bearing blocker: steps 3–7 all consume step 2's scores.

**Step 3 — "Locate the weakest pillar. The infrastructure fails at exactly the point of weakest pillar adherence."**
`BLOCKED:` depends on step 2. Recorded for the record, because this is the algorithm's only falsifiable claim and it can be probed informally even while blocked: on any reading of step 1, *E. coli*'s weakest pillar is Patience — it is the one the organism measurably violates, deliberately and reproducibly. Its measured dominant killing modes are osmotic lysis when wall synthesis is compromised, phage, and envelope- or translation-targeting antibiotics: i.e. failures at Resilience and at single-point mechanical dependency, which is where the cell is comparatively best-provisioned. **The one falsifiable step in the algorithm is not confirmed, and cannot be properly tested because step 2 has no metric.**

**Step 4 — "Identify what completing that pillar would require. Be specific."**
`BLOCKED:` depends on step 3. Note in passing that it cannot be satisfied even informally: "completing Patience" for *E. coli* would mean abolishing overflow metabolism, and the measurement shows that this trades away growth rate for yield — a change in strategy, not a completion of a pillar.

**Step 5 — "Build the missing pillar component before scaling further. Per Patience: defer growth until the pillar is in place."**
`BLOCKED:` depends on step 4. Its independent normative content is contradicted at this scale: the organism does not defer growth, the non-deferral is threshold-linear and reproducible, and the direct proteomic measurement explains why it pays.

**Step 6 — "Re-score after completion. Move to the next-weakest pillar."**
`BLOCKED:` depends on steps 2 and 5.

**Step 7 — "Continue until all four pillars score at acceptable levels for current scale. Then scale."**
`BLOCKED:` depends on step 6, and additionally on a definition of "acceptable levels for current scale", which canon does not give at any scale.

8. Model update (Capsule: what the failed parts contribute): the failed parts localise the defect precisely, and it is not the pillars. ## Forcing Test
- Test A (Ground): grounded. Every pillar occupant is a value reused from an `audit/sweep/` file with its original primary source named in the table; nothing is asserted from memory. The blockers are grounded in the absence of a metric in the quoted canonical text.
- Test B (Uniqueness): not unique, and this is the decisive finding. Each pillar admits multiple *and opposite* occupants in the same organism: Patience is filled by the inactive-ribosome reserve and simultaneously violated by acetate overflow; Resilience is filled by layered defence and simultaneously violated by FtsZ's non-redundancy. Nothing in the pillar definitions selects which measurement counts.
- Test C (Direction): canon runs from civil infrastructure down to the cell ("the same algorithm that runs a cell runs a city"). Measurement runs from the molecule up and finds trade-offs — fidelity against speed, yield against rate, redundancy against proteome cost — where canon finds four independent virtues to be maximised in turn. A trade-off cannot be scored pillar by pillar, which is why step 2 has no metric rather than merely lacking one by oversight.
- Test D (Falsifiability): condition (i) was **not** met — every pillar found an occupant, and no observation of a functioning cell could have left one empty, since any cell has some fidelity mechanism, some alternative route, some reserve and some layered defence. Condition (ii) was met in the wrong direction for canon: the organism's measured failure modes are not at the pillar it most conspicuously violates. Condition (iii) was met: non-deferral is reproducibly optimal here. **Because no observation could have falsified the step-1 mapping, the mapping grades `vacuous` however true it sounds — SCHEMA §3b.**
- **Outcome:** `stipulation`. Step 3's law ("the infrastructure fails at exactly the point of weakest pillar adherence") is not forced and is not testable without a metric canon does not supply; it is an operator's organising choice. `utility: vacuous` for the algorithm's product. `diff_state: methodology-gap`, diagnosis `not-reached`: reality supplies four candidate pillar quantities in four incommensurable units and canon has not grown a scale to hold any of them. `intake_result: open`.

## Anti-Operation (the gap this opens)
1. **A pillar with no unit is not an instrument.** Four candidate proxies now exist and are measured (discrimination factor; route multiplicity; reserve fraction of proteome; redundancy multiplicity). None is in canon, and they cannot be compared. Canon needs either units per pillar plus a commensuration rule, or an explicit statement that the pillars are a checklist and step 3's law is withdrawn.
2. **The pillars are not independent at the scales either side of this one.** Proofreading trades Correctness against speed in one enzyme; mutation rate trades Correctness against population Resilience (canon's own collision, F-068). An algorithm that scores four pillars separately and fixes the weakest first presumes independence that measurement denies.
3. **Patience needs an exchange rate, not a prohibition.** Overflow metabolism is measured, reproducible, mechanistically explained and optimal. "Impatience never reduces total cost" is false at this scale unless "cost" is redefined; canon does not define it.
4. **What does one exception cost a Correspondence claim?** Canon has no rule (this is F-073's question, reached again from Layer I.B's side). Three pillars landing and one reversing is either a strong result or a refutation, and nothing in v5.1–v5.4 says which.
5. Held open: whether any measured biological quantity can rank two pillars against each other. A directed search for a single currency (proteome fraction is the obvious candidate, since fidelity, alternative routes, reserves and defence systems are all paid for in it) is the next test, and would be the first genuinely quantitative version of this layer.
