## Algorithm run (Layer IV, literal, step by step)

**Subject: a bacterial cell (*E. coli* K-12).**

1. *Identify your most selfish creative desire, the one with no ceiling, generative not consumptive.* **BLOCKED: the step's input does not exist in the system.** It requires a represented desire, i.e. an internal representation of a state that is not the current state. The nearest measured decision variable in *E. coli* is the chemotaxis comparator, which has no set-point at all: the cell weights the past 1 s positively and the previous 3 s negatively and responds to the *difference* (audit/sweep/SWEEP-005, F-071). A system with no represented target state cannot name a ceiling, let alone remove it. Nothing was produced.
2. *Identify your most selfless creative desire.* **BLOCKED: same blocker as step 1, plus "for others" requires a represented other.** Measured bacterial interaction is molecular and unaddressed: conjugation transfers DNA to whatever cell the F pilus contacts, and in the recipients that do not recombine it, RecBCD destroys it. Nothing was produced.
3. *Find their convergence.* **BLOCKED: no inputs from steps 1–2.**
4. *Test the convergence point.* **BLOCKED: no convergence point.**
5. *The convergence point is your structural purpose.* **BLOCKED: no convergence point.**
6. *Apply the filter to every decision.* **BLOCKED: no filter criterion.** Note what a forced reading would do here: growth-rate maximisation is available as a substitute criterion, but it is *not* what canon's step says (it is not a convergence of two named extremes), and measurement contradicts it as a universal filter — slow-growing *E. coli* holds more than 20 percent of its proteome as non-translating ribosomes and makes NADPH 20–50 percent above anabolic demand at all dilution rates (F-079). Substituting a criterion here would be filling a held-open variable with assumption; not done.
7. *Per the Five Modes of Propagation, choose how the work outlasts you. "Most lasting work uses all five."* **RAN.** This is the only step of the seven that has inputs in a bacterium, because propagation channels are measurable without a subject. Result:

**The channel canon has no mode for.** The dominant measured propagation channel of a bacterium is vertical inheritance by binary fission, running on the measured adder rule with two parameters and no adjustable parameters, reproduced in *E. coli*, *B. subtilis* and *C. crescentus*. It has no Mode. The Five Modes were abstracted from a vessel that does *not* reproduce — a founder whose process must escape him — so the case where the vessel copies itself entire is outside the model. That is the methodology gap graded in the front matter.

## Grading (utility)

`utility: not-applicable`, and the reason is specific rather than a shrug.

**Steps 1–6: canon has a real scope gate, so `not-applicable` here costs canon nothing.** Canon locates co-creator identity at Appendix A Stage 37 — "Conscious being recognizes itself as focused instance of Omni Force's Third Kingdom" — and the glossary entry names "each conscious being". A bacterium is a Stage-14-order system in canon's own ordering. Canon is therefore not caught claiming that steps 1–6 run on a cell, and this audit does not charge it with that.

**Step 7 is a different matter: canon does claim the Five Modes below the organizational scale, and there the run is vacuous.** Canon makes Propagation Mode 3 the governing principle of Shepherd's Way Step 06 and then assigns Shepherd's Way Steps 06–07 to "biological level" (memory formation) and "biochemical scale" (Krebs), and Correspondence is stated as law with no scope gate ("the patterns running a cell run a civilization… This is law, not analogy"). Run at that scale, every mode-to-mechanism assignment above was made *after looking*:

- Mode 3 predicts "a medium"; a bacterium has one; so does a crystal. The claim cannot fail.
- Mode 4 predicts "systems that run without the originator"; that is what an organism is.
- Mode 5 was assigned to biofilm stratification because stratification was already in hand; no measurement separates it from Mode 1.
- Mode 2 has no occupant, which should reduce "the cardinality of complete propagation" from five to four at this scale — and does not, because nothing in canon is at risk from an empty mode.

## Forcing Test

- Test A (Ground): Is the claim grounded in something measured? Partly. The five modes are grounded in observed human institutional practice, which is real. At cellular scale the ground is absent: four modes were matched to mechanisms already known, one to nothing.
- Test B (Uniqueness): Does measurement leave one candidate? No. The measured propagation channels of a bacterium — vertical fission, conjugation, transduction, CRISPR write-back, restriction-modification as a gate on all of them — partition differently from the five modes, and the largest channel is unmatched. At least two partitions of the same data are available, so the five-mode partition is not unique.
- Test C (Direction): Does it point one way? Yes, weakly: it points at "how does this process survive the death of its instance?", which is a real question for a cell as much as a founder. That is the layer's surviving value at this scale.
- Test D (Falsifiability): Does an attached observation exist that would refute it? For "most lasting work uses all five", no — the hedge "most" absorbs every counterexample, including *Buchnera*. For "each mode addresses a different failure mechanism", yes, and it was met: Mode 2 is empty here and Mode 5 is not separable from Mode 1.
- **Outcome:** `stipulation`. The cardinality five is an operator's presentation choice, not a forced result. Steps 1–6 are correctly scoped by canon to conscious subjects, so the layer's verdict against a cell is `not-applicable`; step 7, which canon *does* use below that scope, is `vacuous` at cellular scale and cannot be graded higher because no observation could have contradicted it.

## Anti-Operation (the gap this opens)

1. **Canon has no rule for an empty slot in a fixed-cardinality list.** Mode 2 has no bacterial occupant. Canon states "the cardinality of complete propagation" and nowhere says what an unoccupied mode at some scale costs the claim. Same structural hole as F-073 (what one exception costs a Correspondence claim).
2. **The Five Modes have no channel for a vessel that copies itself entire.** Every mode presupposes an originator who will stop while the process continues. Binary fission is the opposite case and it is the dominant channel in the domain canon calls its strongest Correspondence evidence. Either a sixth mode (Replication) is named, or the Five Modes are scoped to non-replicating vessels.
3. **A hedge is doing load-bearing work.** "Most lasting work uses all five" cannot be falsified by any single lineage. Canon should either state the fraction it means or drop the sentence; as written it is a belief by the standard of canon's own Layer ◇ step 2.
4. **Where Layer IV's boundary actually is has never been stated.** This run locates it: a represented state that is not the current state. Canon should either adopt that boundary or name a different one, because "structurally, not metaphorically" plus exceptionless Correspondence currently implies a bacterium has a structural purpose, which this run could not derive.
