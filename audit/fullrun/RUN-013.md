Canon's justification for monthly — "the middle moves; the assessment must move with it" — is exactly right and exactly why the period fails here: canon fixes the period instead of tying it to the system's cycle time.

## Forcing Test

- Test A (Ground): Is it grounded? The Four Pillars and the nine departments are grounded in observed business and civil practice. At cellular scale the ground is partial: Correctness and Resilience have measured counterparts; Complexity and Patience do not; one department has no counterpart at all.
- Test B (Uniqueness): Does measurement leave one candidate? No, and this is decisive. Twenty-five of 45 cells admit 0, 0.5 or 1 with equal justification, and the totals range over 7–32 of 45. An instrument whose output spans 56 percent of its own scale on fixed data has not narrowed to one candidate.
- Test C (Direction): Does it point one way? It points, but it pointed wrong: step 5 named a department the organism does not have and a pillar whose low score is the organism's performance mechanism.
- Test D (Falsifiability): Is an observation attached that would refute it? For the grid as a whole, yes, and it was met — an empty department, a 56-percent discretion range, and an interpretation verdict ("Collapse Risk / almost no documented systems") contradicted by a 4.64 Mbp record copied at 1e8–1e11-fold discrimination. For the quadrant analysis, no: "both quadrants populated" cannot fail for any organism.
- **Outcome:** `forced-no` for the claim that the nine-department decomposition and the 45-point grid assess a system at cellular scale. The draw position (Layer V's epigraph, Layer I.F's property) is not covered by this verdict and is separately supported by measured persistence; it is not part of the seven steps.

## Anti-Operation (the gap this opens)

1. **Canon has no coordination metric at any scale.** "0–1 per department for how well it integrates with the other eight" is nine of 45 points — 20 percent of the instrument — with no anchor, no unit and no example. This run could not fill one of the nine cells. The gap is not cellular; it is in the instrument.
2. **Two of the Four Pillars have no measurable content outside business.** Complexity is operationalized as "nine integrated departments" (circular), Patience as "quarterly BEAS assessment" (self-referential). Correctness and Resilience survived the scale change. Canon should say which pillars are substrate-independent and which are business practice.
3. **The interpretation table is calibrated to one substrate and is not marked as such.** A table that returns "Collapse Risk — almost no documented systems" for a bacterium either needs a per-substrate calibration or needs its bands withdrawn as universal.
4. **The re-scoring period is fixed where it should be relative.** "Re-score monthly" should read "re-score every N system cycles"; canon's own justification for the step implies the relative form and its text fixes the absolute one.
5. **A department may be empty and canon says nothing about what that costs.** This is the same architectural hole as F-073 (what one exception costs a Correspondence claim) and F-075 (which of the 100 stages are ordered in time). Canon needs one rule covering all three.
6. **Patience is mis-signed in the presence of concurrency.** Measured coupling of transcription to translation means "create before gathering is complete" is a high-performance mode, not a deficiency. Either Patience is scoped to systems where rework is expensive and information is cheap, or the pillar needs a concurrency clause.
