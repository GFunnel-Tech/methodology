## Canon says

v5.1, immediately above the 12 steps: *"Run this against any domain not explicitly covered by the operational algorithms below. This is the universal pattern from which every specific algorithm in this document is generated. If a new domain emerges that this framework does not yet name, run the Master Meta-Algorithm against it and the specific algorithm for that domain emerges by structural derivation."* [`AGENTS.md`](../../AGENTS.md) rule 4 makes this the standing instruction for any AI system: *"When no explicit algorithm exists, derive one from the Master Meta-Algorithm."*

The 12 steps, quoted in brief: 1 identify the domain (*"specific enough that its DETECT, PROCESS, and RESPOND signatures are distinguishable from adjacent domains"*); 2 locate the Three Kingdoms alignment; 3 identify the current state (*"Capture the data without filtering. Hold every variable open"*); 4 identify the desired state (*"The gap between current state and desired state is the work"*); 5 locate the dynamic middle; 6 *"Identify which Hermetic principle is being violated… The violation is the cause of the gap between current and desired state"*; 7 identify the gradient; 8 set the cause; 9 build the structure through the Kingdoms; 10 run the cycle; 11 document the result; 12 restart at higher baseline.

Canon's own Failure Modes make the sequence load-bearing: skipping step 1 *"produces noise no algorithm can process"*; skipping 4 means *"the algorithm has no target and cannot terminate productively"*; skipping 9's sequencing *"produces the wrong thing perfectly built"*. [`AGENTS.md`](../../AGENTS.md) rule 3: *"Run algorithms literally… If a step cannot be completed, stop: the blocker is the location of the work."*

**Subject of this run, as assigned:** *understand a bacterial cell.*

**Split.** *Factual core:* executed literally on that subject, do the 12 steps terminate, and is the output something a biologist would recognise as useful? *Framework mapping:* the universality claim in the quoted preamble.

## Master Meta-Algorithm run (12 steps, literal)

1. **Identify the domain.** *Produced: a rejection, and it is correct.* "A bacterial cell" fails canon's own test in this step — a cell is not a process domain with one DETECT/PROCESS/RESPOND signature; it is an object in which hundreds of process domains run concurrently, several of which ([SWEEP-025](../sweep/SWEEP-025.md): aerobic and multiple anaerobic reductases encoded simultaneously) run at the same time in the same cytoplasm. To satisfy step 1 the subject must be narrowed. **Narrowed domain adopted for the rest of the run: osmotic homeostasis in *E. coli* — the maintenance of turgor and its release under hypo-osmotic shock.** Credit where due: step 1 did real work. It refused a badly-posed subject rather than proceeding into noise, which is exactly what its Failure Mode warns about. Debit: the subject it refused is canon's own advertised input ("any domain"), and nothing in the 12 steps tells the operator how to narrow — the narrowing came from biology.
2. **Three Kingdoms alignment.** *Produced: "First Kingdom (matter, action, physical execution), with Second Kingdom organisation in the regulatory network."* **This output is vacuous.** Every mechanism inside a cell is First Kingdom by construction; no observation of a bacterium could have returned a different answer, and no subsequent step uses the answer. Named adversarially: there is no measurement whose outcome would have changed this line.
3. **Identify the current state, unfiltered.** *Produced: the six measured rows above.* Correct, and the instruction *"capture the data without filtering"* earns a small, real credit: the 38-per-cent-unannotated figure and the "opens just below lethal tension" relation are precisely the items a filtered summary drops, and both matter. But the output is "go and measure", which is the first move of every empirical method. `works-descriptive`.
4. **Identify the desired state.** *Produced: a mismatch, and it is the first sign of the structural problem.* Canon: *"Name what the domain should produce. The gap between current state and desired state is the work."* The domain already produces what it produces; *E. coli* has maintained turgor for on the order of 10⁹ years. The only desired state available is a statement about **our** knowledge — "a quantitative model that predicts survival probability from downshock magnitude and channel complement". Recorded: step 4 presupposes an operator who wants the domain to behave differently. Understanding is not that.
5. **Locate the dynamic middle.** *Produced: the best output of the run.* Yang extreme — channels always open: no turgor, no growth. Yin extreme — channels always shut: lysis on downshock (the measured phenotype of the double mutant). Dynamic middle — a **graded two-threshold release**, MscS at 5.5 dyn/cm and MscL at 11.8 dyn/cm, both set just below the tension that disrupts the cell. This is a genuine structure with measured coordinates, and it points at a quantity canon does not name: the ratio of the control set-point to the failure point. Debit: the description is post-hoc. Layer I.D names no quantity, so no measured value could have contradicted "there is a dynamic middle here" (the defect F-078 records). `works-descriptive`, not `works-forced`.
6. **Identify which Hermetic principle is being violated.** **BLOCKED: nothing in a functioning bacterial cell is violating a Hermetic principle, and canon supplies no procedure for a domain that is not malfunctioning.** The step is not decorative: canon words it *"The violation is the cause of the gap between current and desired state"*, which makes it the causal hinge that steps 7 and 8 consume. The gap identified at step 4 is in the investigator's model, not in the cell, so there is no violation to locate, and the six measured rows contain nothing that could serve as one — the seventh row records that. Per [`AGENTS.md`](../../AGENTS.md) rule 3 and SCHEMA §4, the run stops here and the later steps are left empty. **The blocker is the location of the work, and it is this: the Master Meta-Algorithm is an intervention algorithm. Steps 4, 6, 7, 8, 9 and 10 all presuppose an operator who intends to change the domain. Understanding a domain is not a special case of changing it, and canon's universality claim for this algorithm does not survive the difference.**

### Two charitable readings, tested (labelled derivations, not primary algorithm)

Neither is offered as a rescue; both are recorded because the run must not fail on a reading nobody intended.

- **Re-scope the domain to "our study of the bacterial cell."** Then step 6 has an occupant — the investigator violates Rhythm by sampling a growth-phase-dependent system at one time point, say — and steps 7–12 become a description of how to run a laboratory. This completes the algorithm, and the output is a lab-management plan. **It is not about the bacterium.** The subject has been replaced, which is the failure, not the fix.
- **Re-scope the domain to "canon's own account of bacterial osmotic homeostasis."** This one produces something real and is worth recording in canon's favour: step 6 then identifies **Correspondence violated by canon itself** — Stage 24's only named mechanism is the eukaryotic Na⁺/K⁺ ATPase, which *E. coli* does not have, and Stage 29 places pressure transduction after nervous systems ([SWEEP-008](../sweep/SWEEP-008.md), F-072). Step 6, pointed at the framework's own text instead of at the cell, locates a genuine error that the sweep also found. That is the Master Meta-Algorithm working — as a *critique* instrument applied to a document. It still is not the assigned subject.

## Scientific Inquiry run
1. Question (precise): executed literally on the subject "understand a bacterial cell", do the Master Meta-Algorithm's 12 steps terminate, and is the output something a biologist would recognise as a contribution rather than a restatement?
2. What an answer must look like: a per-step record of what was produced; a termination statement (completed, or blocked with the blocking step named); and for each completed step, the observation that would have made its output false.
3. Falsifiability condition: the universality claim in canon's preamble is falsified if a step cannot be completed on a domain canon does not name, for a reason internal to the step rather than to the operator's diligence. It survives if all 12 steps complete and at least one produces a statement about the cell that measurement supports and that was not put in by hand.
4. Variables: measurable / bounded / held open: measurable — genome length, gene count, unannotated fraction, ABC-transporter family size, cytoplasmic macromolecule concentration, the two channel midpoint tensions, the downshock survival phenotype. Bounded — the dynamic middle's coordinates at step 5 (measured, but the claim they instantiate is unfalsifiable as canon words it). Held open — whether an intervention-shaped algorithm can be re-worded to serve inquiry without becoming the Scientific Inquiry Algorithm, which canon already has ([RUN-019](RUN-019.md)).
5. Test designed: run the 12 steps in order on the assigned subject without re-scoping; record each product; where a step cannot be completed, record `BLOCKED:` and stop; then test the two most charitable re-scopings separately and label them derivations.
6. Data (unfiltered): step 1 rejected the subject as posed and forced a narrowing that came from biology, not from the algorithm. Step 2 produced an output no observation could have falsified. Step 3 produced a correct data capture with a real discipline attached. Step 4 produced a mismatch between "what the domain should produce" and "what we should know". Step 5 produced the run's one substantive structure — two measured thresholds below a measured failure point. Step 6 produced nothing and could produce nothing: a working cell violates no principle. Steps 7–12 unreachable. Of six reachable steps, one was vacuous, three were descriptive, one refused the subject correctly, and one blocked.
7. Variable Principle applied: **Measured** — the seven rows in `## Reality shows`. **Structurally derived** — the classification of the algorithm as intervention-shaped (derived from steps 4, 6, 7, 8 all presupposing an intended change); the two charitable re-scopings, both labelled derivations per AGENTS.md rule 4 and rule 7. **Held open** — whether the block at step 6 is repairable by re-wording ("identify which principle the *current account* of the domain violates") without duplicating the Scientific Inquiry Algorithm; not filled here, because it is an authorial decision and no measurement bears on it.
8. Model update (Capsule: what the failed parts contribute): the block is the most informative output of the run, and it is a *positive* result about canon's shape — it locates precisely where canon's universal instrument is not universal, and it explains something the audit had not explained: why the Scientific Inquiry Algorithm, not the Master Meta-Algorithm, is the instrument [`RUNBOOK.md`](../RUNBOOK.md) reaches for on every audit file. The audit made the right choice without stating the reason; the reason is that only one of the two is inquiry-shaped. The failed steps also contribute the second charitable reading, which turned step 6 into a working critique instrument against canon's own Stage 24 and Stage 29 — a use canon does not claim and which works.
9. Documented: this file; cross-references F-072, F-075, F-078, SWEEP-008, SWEEP-025, RUNBOOK.md, AGENTS.md rules 3–4, [RUN-019](RUN-019.md).
10. Next baseline: author question. Either restrict the preamble's universality to *intervention* domains (the honest, one-line fix, and the `proposed_label` on this record), or add an inquiry-shaped variant — noting canon already has one, so the second option is probably redundant.

## Forcing Test
- Test A (Ground): within-system. Whether an algorithm's steps can be executed on a stated subject is decided by reading the steps and attempting them; no source question arises. Grounded on the reality side by seven fetched or sweep-cited measurements, three from primary sources retrieved this run.
- Test B (Uniqueness): three candidates for why the run blocks. (i) *Operator failure* — the executor was not resourceful enough at step 6. **Eliminated**: two independent re-scopings were constructed and both complete the algorithm only by changing the subject, which is a property of the step, not of the executor. (ii) *The algorithm is intervention-shaped and the subject is inquiry-shaped.* Survives. (iii) *The subject is malformed and no algorithm could take it.* **Eliminated**: canon's own Scientific Inquiry Algorithm takes an inquiry subject and runs to step 10 on bacterial questions ([RUN-019](RUN-019.md)), ~270 times in this audit. One candidate survives.
- Test C (Direction): the direction test confirms (ii) independently. The Master Meta-Algorithm's arrow runs **operator → domain**: intent (step 8, *"set the cause"*) flows outward into structure (step 9) and execution (step 10). Inquiry's arrow runs **domain → operator**: the cell is the source and the model is what changes. Steps 4, 6, 7 and 8 are all stations on the outward arrow, which is why the block lands at 6 and not somewhere incidental.
- Test D (Falsifiability): the falsifier was fixed at step 3 of the Scientific Inquiry run before the run — *complete all 12 steps and show one output about the cell that measurement supports and that was not supplied by hand.* Executed: it **fired**, at step 6. It was a real risk, not a formality: had step 6 read "identify which principle explains the domain's behaviour" rather than "is being violated", the run would have completed and this record would have graded `works-descriptive` or better.
- **Outcome:** `forced-no` for canon's universality claim about this algorithm, as worded. The 12 steps do not run against *any* domain; they run against domains an operator intends to change. `utility: not-applicable` — and per SCHEMA §3b, *"if canon claims the layer is universal, `not-applicable` is itself a failure of that universality claim"*, which is recorded here as the finding rather than as an exemption. `diff_state: methodology-gap`, `exclusion_diagnosis: not-reached`: reality has an understandable bacterial cell and canon's universal instrument does not reach it. `intake_result: open`, not `adjusted` — the canonical fix is a one-line restriction of the preamble and that is the author's declaration to make, and the measurements here are `measured-single`.

## Anti-Operation (the gap this opens)

- **Canon has two top-level algorithms and no rule for choosing between them.** The Master Meta-Algorithm is advertised for "any domain"; the Scientific Inquiry Algorithm is filed under "Scientific and Cognitive Work" as one domain algorithm among many. This run shows the relation is the reverse of the filing for any subject one wants to *understand*. **New gap: what selects the instrument?** [`AGENTS.md`](../../AGENTS.md) rule 4 currently sends every uncovered domain to the Master Meta-Algorithm, which for an inquiry subject sends it to a step that cannot complete.
- **Step 6 presumes every gap has a culprit.** *"The violation is the cause of the gap between current and desired state."* A gap can also be produced by a constraint nobody violated — a thermodynamic bound, or the 38 per cent of *E. coli* genes with no attributed function, where the gap is ignorance and not misconduct. Canon has no step for a gap with no culprit.
- **No step in the 12 is allowed to return "nothing here".** Step 2 returned First Kingdom because it must return one of three; there is no null. An algorithm whose steps cannot decline to answer manufactures content, and step 2 did.
- **The second charitable reading is a real instrument canon does not claim.** Pointing step 6 at canon's own text found Stage 24's eukaryotic-mechanism error. Unmeasured: how much of the audit's own yield would be reproduced by running step 6 over canon's other 99 stages. That is a cheap and specific next run.

## Narrative stripped (if any)

- *"The universal pattern from which every specific algorithm in this document is generated"*: stripped as a claim about scope. It may well be the pattern from which canon's *operational* algorithms were generated — every one of those is an intervention algorithm, which is consistent with, and explains, the shape found here. It is not a universal pattern for domains.
- *"Do not skip Kingdoms. Each Kingdom must complete before the next begins"* (step 9): not reached, so not tested. Recorded as untested rather than as satisfied.
- Step 12's *"Per the Capsule: each cycle integrates ALL prior cycles"*: not reached. No claim about integration is graded here; see F-067 for the state of the Capsule claim generally.
