## Canon says

v5.1, Layer I.D — The Yin/Yang Dynamic Middle Algorithm, quoted in full as the instrument under test:

> "Use when: any system shows imbalance between activity and rest, expansion and contraction, attack and defense, output and input.
> 1. Identify the axis. What polarity is the system operating along? Active/receptive, expansive/contractive, exterior/interior.
> 2. Locate the Yang extreme of this axis. What does maximum Yang look like?
> 3. Locate the Yin extreme of this axis. What does maximum Yin look like?
> 4. Identify the current position. Is the system at extreme Yang, extreme Yin, or somewhere between?
> 5. Identify the dynamic middle for current conditions. Where SHOULD the system be operating right now? The middle is not fixed — it shifts based on stage, scale, and circumstance.
> 6. Determine the gap. How far is the system from its current correct middle?
> 7. Apply correction. If too Yang: introduce Yin (rest, reflection, infrastructure, gathering). If too Yin: introduce Yang (action, expression, output, decision).
> 8. Re-locate the middle. After correction, the middle has likely shifted. Begin the cycle again."

Supporting layer text relied on by the steps: *"This point is NOT fixed. It shifts continuously in response to system state."* · *"A fixed middle is a dead middle… the thermostat maintains a fixed set-point. The living organism adjusts its set-point."* · The Three States table (Dynamic / Rigid / Collapsed Middle). · The Tai Chi encoding, truth three: *"The boundary is a curve, not a line: The transition between states is continuous and non-linear. Systems do not flip at a threshold."*

**Split (RUNBOOK rule 4).** *Factual core graded here:* running these eight steps on a real system yields a correct, non-trivial, falsifiable statement about that system. *Framework mapping (not graded; see Narrative stripped):* the Yin/Yang pole assignment, the chess draw, the Three States diagnostics.

## The algorithm run, literally, step by step

**System:** one *E. coli* K-12 cell in aerobic glucose minimal medium growing at λ ≈ 0.9 h⁻¹ (i.e. above the measured threshold, excreting acetate).

1. **Identify the axis.** Produced: *the allocation of proteome between respiratory and fermentative energy biogenesis.* This is the only single axis in the organism for which canon's own confirming instance exists. **But the step's actual demand — "active/receptive, expansive/contractive, exterior/interior" — is not satisfiable:** neither respiration nor fermentation is measurably receptive, inward, or contracting; both are catabolic and both produce ATP. The pole assignment is a stipulation, and **the algorithm's output at steps 6–8 is invariant under swapping the labels.** Step 1 therefore does no work that the later steps consume.
2. **Locate the Yang extreme.** Produced: fully fermentative energy biogenesis. Measurable endpoint. Canon's Yang extreme is defined as pathological ("overextension", "burnout"); the measured endpoint is not — it is how *E. coli* grows fastest. The step returns a value; the value does not carry canon's meaning.
3. **Locate the Yin extreme.** Produced: fully respiratory energy biogenesis, λ < λ_ac ≈ 0.76 h⁻¹. Measurable. Again not pathological: a culture below λ_ac is carbon-limited, not dysfunctional.
4. **Identify the current position.** Produced: λ ≈ 0.9 h⁻¹, i.e. 0.14 h⁻¹ above the threshold, on the fermentative side, excreting acetate at J_ac = S_ac(λ − λ_ac) ≈ 1.4 mM/OD. Real, measurable, and the step does genuine work.
5. **Identify the dynamic middle for current conditions — "Where SHOULD the system be operating right now?"** Produced: λ_ac ≈ 0.76 h⁻¹. **The normative half of the step has no measured referent.** Nothing degrades when the cell is off λ_ac; λ is set by the medium, not chosen; and no molecule in the cell measures λ, so there is nothing that could hold a set-point. The step is completable only by renaming a measured kink "the middle".
6. **Determine the gap.** Produced: 0.14 h⁻¹. Arithmetic on step 4 minus step 5. The step works as subtraction and contributes nothing as diagnosis, because step 5 supplied no norm.
7. **Apply correction — "if too Yang: introduce Yin (rest, reflection, infrastructure, gathering)."** Produced: **a prescription measurement contradicts.** (i) There is no operator inside the cell to apply it. (ii) The measured interventions that reduce fermentative overflow — useless-protein expression, chloramphenicol — do not move the cell toward the middle: they move **the middle toward the cell** (λ_ac falls 0.76 → 0.66 → 0.48 → 0.26 h⁻¹), while the cell's growth rate falls too. (iii) The state being "corrected" is the measured proteome-efficient solution: the proteome cost of respiration exceeds that of fermentation, confirmed by quantitative mass spectrometry. Step 7 prescribes correcting an optimum.
8. **Re-locate the middle — "after correction, the middle has likely shifted."** Produced: *the middle has shifted* — and **no sign.** Forward test, run before consulting the value: remove a constraint (delete the motility proteins) — does the middle move up or down? Canon's eight steps, the Three States table and the Tai Chi encoding each permit either. Measured: λ_ac rises 0.76 → **0.96 ± 0.02 h⁻¹**. The sign is supplied by λ_ac(φ_Z) = λ_ac(1 − φ_Z/φ_max), which is not in canon and is not derivable from it. **The algorithm describes the movement after the fact; it does not predict its direction.**

**Run result:** eight steps completed, none blocked. Steps 4 and 6 produced measurable numbers taken entirely from the measurement. Steps 1, 5 and 8 produced stipulations. Step 7 produced a falsified prescription. The one statement the algorithm itself asserts — *the middle is not fixed and will have moved* — survives.

## Scientific Inquiry run

1. Question (precise): Run forward against *E. coli* K-12, does Layer I.D's algorithm predict the direction in which the measured optimum moves when a constraint is removed, or only record that it moved?
2. What an answer must look like: the eight steps executed in order with their outputs recorded; a named measured quantity standing at the "middle"; a stated sign for the movement produced **before** the perturbation value is read; and the measured sign.
3. Falsifiability condition: the algorithm has predictive content if steps 5–8, applied to a stated perturbation, yield a sign that could be wrong. It has none if both signs are compatible with the eight steps. It is refuted as vacuous if no measured value of λ_ac could have contradicted its output.
4. Variables: measurable / bounded / held open: **Measurable** — λ_ac, S_ac, φ_Z, φ_max, λ. **Bounded** — λ_ac in 0.26–0.96 h⁻¹ over the seven measured conditions; S_ac invariant. **Held open** — whether anything in the cell represents λ; whether the pole assignment of step 1 can be made non-arbitrarily for any bacterial axis.
5. Test designed: execute the eight steps on one stated condition; at step 8 state the predicted sign from canon alone; then read the flagellar-knockout value, which is a constraint *removal* and therefore the cleanest available discriminator.
6. Data (unfiltered): the table above and the eight step outputs. Recorded including the part that spoils the tidy version: three of the eight steps are satisfiable only by stipulation, one issues a prescription the measurement contradicts, and the two that produce real numbers (4, 6) take both of them from the measurement rather than from the layer.
7. Variable Principle applied: what is **measured** is that an observable optimum moves by a factor 3.7 under stated perturbations, with a measured sign. What is **structurally derived** by the algorithm is only that it moves. What is **held open** is whether the cell adjusts anything; the same data are fully reproduced by a fixed allocation rule whose optimum sits elsewhere when constraints change, and canon's own thermostat-versus-organism contrast requires exactly that distinction to be made. The three are not collapsed.
8. Model update (Capsule: what the failed parts contribute): the failing parts are the most useful output of this run. Step 7's direction of correction is backwards for this system — the intervention moves the middle, not the system — which is a general, testable claim about constraint-set optima that canon could adopt and that its business examples would also have to satisfy. Step 8 can be repaired by one sentence: *state the constrained quantity, and state that relaxing a constraint moves the middle in the direction of the relaxed constraint.* With that sentence Layer I.D would have predicted 0.96 h⁻¹ > 0.76 h⁻¹ in advance and would be falsifiable thereafter.
9. Documented: this file; extends [SWEEP-027](../sweep/SWEEP-027.md) and [FINDINGS](../FINDINGS.md) F-078 from the layer's *content* to the layer's *algorithm*.
10. Next baseline: state the sign rule in canon, then test it on a perturbation that alters respiratory rather than ribosomal proteome cost — where the fixed-rule and adjusting-set-point readings are measured to diverge. Re-review 2027-09-24.

## Forcing Test

- Test A (Ground): Grounded on the reality side — one quantity, one threshold-linear law, seven measured threshold values with uncertainties. Ungrounded on canon's side: Layer I.D names no bacterial quantity, unit, or value anywhere.
- Test B (Uniqueness): Fails. The eight steps' output ("the middle moved") is compatible with the measurement, with the opposite sign of movement, and with a fixed allocation rule that has no middle and does no adjusting. Consistency with three readings selects none of them.
- Test C (Direction): Fails as prediction, passes as description. The measured direction is signed and reproducible (burden down → threshold up; burden up → threshold down). Canon's step 8 asserts movement without sign, so it cannot be scored against either outcome.
- Test D (Falsifiability): Fails. Name the observation that would have made the run's output false: there is none. A λ_ac that had moved the other way, moved further, or not moved at all would each have been absorbed — the first two by step 8's unsigned "has likely shifted", the third by the Three States table's "Rigid Middle (Dysfunction I)", which converts a fixed optimum into a diagnosis of the system rather than a refutation of the layer. **A layer that has a named diagnostic category for its own falsifier cannot be falsified by it.**
- **Outcome:** `live-hypothesis` for the claim; **`utility: vacuous`** for the instrument. The algorithm produced a true statement about *E. coli* that no measurement could have contradicted, and supplied none of the content that makes the true statement interesting.

## Anti-Operation (the gap this opens)

1. **A new candidate conflict, not previously logged.** The Tai Chi encoding states *"Systems do not flip at a threshold"* and *"the boundary is a curve, not a line."* The single measured instance Layer I.D can claim in a bacterium is fitted, in its primary source, as **exactly a threshold**: J_ac = 0 below λ_ac and linear above, with a discontinuous first derivative, and the fit's quality is the paper's central result. The underlying proteome fractions vary continuously, so this is a conflict with the *observable*, not with the microscopics — but the observable is what canon's sentence is about. Recommend this be graded on its own as a candidate finding.
2. **The Three States table is an absorber, not a diagnostic.** Because "Rigid Middle" is available for any measured fixity, Layer I.D cannot be refuted by a fixed optimum. Canon should state what observation would falsify the dynamic middle, or withdraw the claim that it is falsifiable.
3. **Step 7's direction is untested everywhere else.** If relaxing a constraint moves the optimum rather than the system, canon's business examples (the 5-person versus 50-person middle) are claims of the same shape and should be checked the same way.

## Narrative stripped (if any)

Removed from the graded core: the Yin/Yang pole assignment (shown at step 1 to be free — the algorithm's output is invariant under relabelling, which is stronger than saying the assignment is unforced); the chess draw analogy; the Three States diagnostics (no measured counterpart — an *E. coli* culture held below λ_ac is carbon-limited, not rigid); and the "living thermostat" mechanism, which requires the cell to adjust a set-point it has no measured means of representing. Reality neither forces nor contradicts the Yin/Yang mapping itself; what this run shows is narrower and sharper — the mapping is not load-bearing, because deleting it changes no output of the algorithm.
