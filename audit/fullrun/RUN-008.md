## Canon says

v5.1, Layer I.G — The Slime Mold Integration Algorithm, quoted in full as the instrument under test:

> "Use when: integrating failures, dead ends, partial paths, or apparent waste into productive substrate for forward progress.
> 1. Identify the failed path. What was attempted? What did not work?
> 2. Extract the information. What did the failure reveal? What constraints did it map? What approaches did it eliminate from future consideration?
> 3. Identify what the failed path's information enables in the surviving network. The slime mold retracts protoplasm from inefficient tubes back into the network.
> 4. Integrate the information into current configuration. Do not delete the failure record — preserve it as load-bearing structure that informs every future decision.
> 5. Restart the exploration with the new substrate loaded. Failed paths ten steps deep collapse to step one and restart with the prior path's information available.
> 6. Trust the integration. Every failure that contributed information is part of what the present configuration is made of. Nothing is lost.
> 7. Apply at every level. Personal failures integrate into individual capability. Organizational failures integrate into institutional knowledge. Civilizational failures integrate into cultural inheritance. The mechanism is identical."

Layer text: *"Tubes along inefficient paths thin and retract. Tubes along efficient paths thicken. The retraction is not deletion — the protoplasm flows back into the network and redistributes into the surviving tubes. Nothing is lost. Failed paths become the material substance of successful paths."* · *"information is conserved across all paths; nothing is discarded."*

**The split this run is graded against** ([CLAIM-021](../claims/CLAIM-021.md), following the author clarification of 2026-09-28): **(a) physical persistence** — the information is not annihilated; stable structure carries records forward; *bounded and supported.* **(b) functional integration** — the failed path's information is retained *by the surviving system* and is load-bearing for what follows; *forced-no as written.*

**Split (RUNBOOK rule 4).** *Factual core graded here:* each numbered step, and which of (a) / (b) it requires. *Framework mapping (not graded; see Narrative stripped):* the Capsule, Fibonacci-at-maximum-extension, the theological implication, the five-tradition table.

## The algorithm run, literally, step by step

**System:** an *E. coli* K-12 cell that has just made a replication error and repaired a UV lesion. The "failed path" is the misincorporated nucleotide and the damaged base. Each step is annotated with **which half of the split it requires** — (a) physical persistence or (b) functional integration.

1. **Identify the failed path.** Produced: the misincorporated nucleotide removed by the proofreading exonuclease, and the damage-containing **13-mer ssDNA** excised by nucleotide excision repair, unwound by UvrD. Specific, measured, and directly sequenced. **Requires neither (a) nor (b). Step 1 works.**
2. **Extract the information — "what constraints did it map? what approaches did it eliminate from future consideration?"** Produced: **nothing, for this cell.** Nothing in *E. coli* reads the excised 13-mer; it is exposed to exonuclease and its sequence destroyed. What *does* encode the eliminated approaches is the discriminating machinery — serial discrimination of 1.6×10⁸–1.6×10¹¹ fold across base selection, proofreading and mismatch repair — but that structure was assembled by selection over evolutionary time, not extracted from this failure by this cell. **Requires (b). Contradicted at the scale the step is run at**, and the step does not say which scale it runs at, which is itself the defect.
3. **THE CRUX — "identify what the failed path's information enables in the surviving network. The slime mold retracts protoplasm from inefficient tubes back into the network."** This one step contains both halves, and measurement separates them cleanly **inside canon's own named organism**:
   - **MASS returns: measured true, in both systems.** *Physarum*: thinner tubes' diameters decrease with distance from the stimulus and "overall mass is redistributed from the bottom to the top of the network" (up to twofold dilation near the stimulus, established within ~15 min, N = 2,165 tubes). *E. coli*: the material of discarded nucleotides is genuinely recoverable — a strain lacking all three ribonucleotide reductase operons grows on salvaged deoxyribonucleosides, and evolved a 25-fold reduction in the concentration it needs, with mutations in *deoB* and *cdd*. **Canon's mechanism is literally correct as a statement about matter, and this deserves credit: the retraction really is not deletion.**
   - **INFORMATION does not return: measured false, in both systems.** *Physarum*'s record is measured to reside in the **surviving** tube diameter hierarchy — "stored in and retrieved from the networks' tube diameter hierarchy" — i.e. in the structure of what remains, not in the retracted material. *E. coli*: the excised strand's sequence is destroyed and unintegrated foreign DNA is degraded by RecBCD; no retrieval mechanism is measured.
   - **Result:** step 3 requires **(a) for its mass clause and (b) for its information clause**, and the slime mold — canon's strongest case, chosen by canon — supports the first and refutes the second. It also independently confirms [CLAIM-021](../claims/CLAIM-021.md)'s proposed replacement: *the system conserves the discriminating structure, not the discarded item.* The tube diameter hierarchy **is** the discriminating structure.
4. **Integrate the information — "do not delete the failure record — preserve it as load-bearing structure."** Produced: **a statement measurement contradicts.** *E. coli* deletes the failure record: exonucleolytic destruction of the 13-mer, RecBCD degradation of unintegrated DNA, and Landauer's bound (≥ kT ln 2 per bit, reproduced) quantifies why discarding is cheaper than keeping. **Requires (b). Contradicted.** *But one measured bacterial mechanism does exactly what this step asks*, and canon should have it: CRISPR **primed adaptation** — a spacer that has *failed* to confer defence still guides the acquisition machinery to the invader and restores protection. That is a failed record kept and made load-bearing, measured in two independent laboratories. Two qualifications keep it honest: the record kept is the *invader's* sequence, not a trace of the cell's own failed path; and self-spacer acquisition rises ~10-fold when RecBCD is removed, so what makes the archive useful is discrimination about what to keep — the opposite of "do not delete".
5. **Restart the exploration with the new substrate loaded.** Produced: the cell does restart — replication resumes after excision repair — but what is loaded is the **original, correct** template restored from the complementary strand, not the failed path's data. **Requires (b). Contradicted for the general case**, with primed adaptation again the single measured exception.
6. **Trust the integration — "nothing is lost."** Produced: **nothing.** This step issues no operation and returns no output. As a claim it is (b), which is forced-no; as an instruction it is unfalsifiable, since no observation contradicts an instruction to trust. **Requires (b), and is vacuous even where (b) held.**
7. **Apply at every level — "the mechanism is identical."** Produced: **contradicted at the scale canon calls its strongest.** At bacterial scale the mass half holds and the information half does not, so the mechanism is not identical across levels; it is two mechanisms that canon's sentence fuses. The layer's own "Slime Mold Stack" table marks the Biological row *"Confirmed (genomic conservation; junk DNA; immune memory)"* — and the immune-memory instance, run here, confirms the mass-and-discriminating-structure reading, not the information-retention reading.

**Run result and the split verdict.** Steps 2, 4, 5, 6 require **(b)** and (b) is contradicted. Step 3 requires **both**, and only its (a) clause survives. Steps 1 and 7 are neutral and structural respectively. **The algorithm's operative content rests on the half of canon's claim that measurement contradicts.** (a) alone would leave steps 1 and 3-mass, i.e. a recycling procedure — true, useful, and much less than canon claims.

## Scientific Inquiry run

1. Question (precise): Run against *E. coli* K-12, which of CLAIM-021's two halves — (a) physical persistence, (b) functional integration — does each step of Layer I.G's algorithm actually require, and does step 3's named mechanism ("retracts protoplasm back into the network") hold?
2. What an answer must look like: for each of the seven steps, the output produced plus a labelled (a)/(b) requirement; and for step 3, a direct measurement on *Physarum polycephalum* itself saying where the record resides and what happens to the retracted material.
3. Falsifiability condition: the (b)-dependence finding is refuted if any step can be completed using (a) alone and still produce what canon says it produces. Step 3's mass clause is refuted by a measurement showing retracted material is destroyed rather than redistributed. Step 3's information clause is *supported* if the record is measured to travel with the retracted material, and refuted if it is measured to reside in the survivor.
4. Variables: measurable / bounded / held open: **Measurable** — tube diameters and mass redistribution in *Physarum*; the excised 13-mer; deoxyribonucleoside salvage sufficiency; serial discrimination; primed adaptation; Landauer's bound. **Bounded** — *Physarum* redistribution completes within ~15 min and persists to 45 min; salvage suffices at a concentration reduced 25-fold by evolution. **Held open** — whether "structure stability" in the author clarification denotes einselection (CLAIM-021 step 10, still unanswered); the black-hole case, which canon itself files open and on which (a) partly rests.
5. Test designed: run the seven steps on one bacterial failure event; then test step 3 not by analogy but **on canon's own organism**, fetching the primary measurement of where *Physarum* keeps its record — because if the slime mold itself separates (a) from (b), the split is not an external imposition on canon but a reading of canon's chosen mechanism.
6. Data (unfiltered): the table above and the seven step outputs. Recorded including the parts that help canon: the mass clause of step 3 is measured true in both organisms, salvage is real and sufficient, and primed adaptation is a genuine measured instance of step 4 that canon does not cite and should.
7. Variable Principle applied: **Measured** — mass redistribution, record location in the survivor's structure, destruction of the excised strand, salvage sufficiency, primed adaptation, Landauer's bound. **Structurally derived** — the 1.6×10⁸–1.6×10¹¹ serial discrimination (the derivation is shown), and the inference that a discriminating structure is the retained lesson. **Held open** — "structure stability", and whether the CRISPR archive generalises beyond invader sequences. The three are not collapsed, and (a) is not allowed to underwrite (b): unitarity and einselection place the surviving record in the environment, and decoherence is precisely the loss of the system's access to it.
8. Model update (Capsule: what the failed parts contribute): the failed steps yield canon's replacement text. Step 3 should be **split into two steps** — *recover the material* (true, measured, and what a slime mold and a salvage pathway both do) and *identify the discriminating structure the failure refined* (true, measured, and what a proofreading domain, a tube diameter hierarchy and a CRISPR array all are). Step 4's "do not delete the failure record" should become *keep the discrimination, not the item* — with the 10-fold rise in self-targeting when RecBCD is removed as the measured warrant that deletion is part of the mechanism. Step 6 should be deleted: it produces no output. With those three edits Layer I.G would be running on (a) plus a measurable structural claim, and would need nothing from (b).
9. Documented: this file; [CLAIM-021](../claims/CLAIM-021.md); [FINDINGS](../FINDINGS.md) F-067. New to this run: the direct *Physarum* measurement, which moves the (a)/(b) split from an inference about bacteria to a measurement on canon's own named mechanism.
10. Next baseline: the two questions CLAIM-021 already puts to the author — whether canon should assert (b) at all, and whether "structure stability" means einselection — plus one this run adds: does canon accept *Physarum*'s tube diameter hierarchy as the discriminating structure, given it is measured to be where the memory lives?

## Forcing Test

- Test A (Ground): Grounded on both sides. Canon's mechanism is named and specific ("retracts protoplasm… back into the network"), and the mechanism has now been measured directly in the named organism, with sample size, magnitude and timescale.
- Test B (Uniqueness): Two readings of step 3 entered — "the retracted material carries the record" and "the surviving structure carries the record" — and measurement eliminates the first: the record is *"stored in and retrieved from the networks' tube diameter hierarchy"*. One reading survives, and it is not canon's.
- Test C (Direction): Signed and measured. Mass flows **inward** to the survivors ("from the bottom to the top of the network") — canon's direction, correct. Information does **not** flow with it; it is constituted by what survives. Canon fuses the two flows and gets the second direction wrong.
- Test D (Falsifiability): The falsifier for (b) fired again, now inside canon's own chosen organism rather than only in *E. coli*. The falsifier for (a) did not fire and this run does not touch it.
- **Outcome:** `forced-no` for (b) and therefore for steps 2, 4, 5, 6 and the information clause of step 3. **`utility: fails`** — the algorithm's operative steps produced statements measurement contradicts. The (a) content that survives is a recycling procedure, correctly described.

## Anti-Operation (the gap this opens)

1. **If the discriminating structure is the conserved thing, it should be measurable as such.** Three measured candidates now exist at three scales: a proofreading exonuclease plus MutHLS (1.6×10⁸–1.6×10¹¹ fold discrimination), a CRISPR array with a measured self/non-self error rate, and a tube diameter hierarchy with 2,165 measured tubes. Canon has no quantity for "how much discrimination a structure holds", and these three are the dataset with which to define one. That is a better open edge than "nothing is lost".
2. **Deletion is load-bearing.** Self-spacer acquisition rising ~10-fold without RecBCD, and Landauer's ≥ kT ln 2, together say that discarding is not a failure of conservation but a paid-for component of the mechanism. Layer I.G currently forbids what its own best instances require, and this collides head-on with Layer I.D's optimal mutation rate — the unresolved collision already logged as [FINDINGS](../FINDINGS.md) F-068.
3. **(a) cannot be firmer than its foundation.** Grounding I.G on unitarity ties it to the black-hole information problem, which canon files as open (CLAIM-021's second gap, restated here because this run's verdict depends on it: everything that survives in this layer survives on (a)).

## Narrative stripped (if any)

Two things removed from the graded core, both surviving elsewhere. **(i) "Nothing is lost" as a statement about systems** — true of the universe (bounded, per (a)), false of every open system measured, which is every cell. **(ii) The Capsule, Fibonacci-at-maximum-extension, the theological implication and the five-tradition table** — none is graded here; reality neither forces nor contradicts them, and the run's verdict does not depend on them. What is *not* stripped, and is instead promoted, is canon's slime mold image: the measurement vindicates its mass clause precisely and refutes its information clause precisely, which is a more useful outcome for canon than either wholesale acceptance or wholesale rejection.
