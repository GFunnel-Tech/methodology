## Canon says

v5.1, Scientific Inquiry Algorithm, quoted in full because this run grades it step by step:

1. *"State the question with maximum precision."*
2. *"Identify what the answer would have to look like to count as an answer. What forms of response would qualify?"*
3. *"Identify the falsifiability condition. What observation would prove the leading hypothesis wrong?"*
4. *"Map the variables. Which are measurable now? Which are bounded? Which are held open?"*
5. *"Design the experiment to maximally narrow the held-open variables."*
6. *"Run the experiment. Capture all data without filtering."*
7. *"Apply the Variable Principle to results. Do not fill remaining variables with assumption."*
8. *"Update the model. Per the Slime Mold mechanism: failed hypotheses contribute information to the integrated understanding."*
9. *"Document the experiment, the variables, the data, and the variables now bounded or resolved."*
10. *"Iterate at higher baseline. The next experiment loads with all prior experiments' information."*

**Standing of this instrument in the audit.** [`RUNBOOK.md`](../RUNBOOK.md) rule 8 makes it the compulsory instrument for every audit file — *"Fill all 10 Scientific Inquiry steps"* — and [`SCHEMA.md`](../SCHEMA.md) §4 builds it into the record format. It has therefore been executed on the order of 270 times in this repository, which makes grading it unavoidable and makes this record partly a self-audit. Note that canon files it as *one domain algorithm among many*, below the Master Meta-Algorithm; the audit uses it as its top-level instrument. [RUN-018](RUN-018.md) explains why that inversion was correct.

**The open bacterial question chosen:** *what is the smallest gene set that supports autonomous bacterial life, and how much of that set is of unknown function?*

**Split.** *Factual core:* do the 10 steps, executed literally, terminate on this question and produce a defensible statement about what is known and what is held open? *Framework mapping:* steps 7 and 8, which are canon's two additions to the ordinary statement of scientific method (the Variable Principle, and the Capsule's treatment of failure as substrate).

## Scientific Inquiry run (the instrument itself, run on the question; also serves as this record's required run)

1. Question (precise): for a chassis derived from *Mycoplasma mycoides*, grown in rich medium, with "robust growth" as the viability criterion — what is the smallest number of genes that supports autonomous replication, and how many of those genes have no attributed biological function? *Step 1's product: three qualifiers the loose question hid — chassis, medium, growth criterion. Without them the question has no answer, because "minimal" is not a property of life but of a triple. This step did work.*
2. What an answer must look like: a gene count from a **constructed and replicating** cell, not from a computational prediction or a mutagenesis inference; plus a count of unannotated genes within that set; plus the three qualifiers from step 1 attached to both numbers. *Product: a specification that rejects in advance the two commonest answers in the literature — comparative-genomics estimates and transposon-essentiality lists — because neither is a replicating cell. This is the step that made the rest of the run discriminating.*
3. Falsifiability condition: leading hypothesis before looking — a minimal cell's gene set is fully accounted for by known functions, since it was designed from known biology. That is proved wrong by any constructed minimal cell retaining a substantial set of genes whose functions are unknown. Threshold fixed here, before step 6: **more than 10 per cent of the retained genes unannotated falsifies it.** *Product: a number stated in advance. This is the step that makes the run a test rather than a summary, and it is the step canon's own Falsifiability Register omits ([RUN-016](RUN-016.md)).*
4. Variables: measurable / bounded / held open: measurable now — genome size and gene count of a constructed cell, the unannotated count, whether the first design was viable. Bounded — "minimal", bounded below by the constructed 473 and above by the 1,079 kb parent. Held open — an organism-independent minimal gene set (last row of `## Reality shows`); the functions of the unannotated genes.
5. Test designed: design a reduced genome from known biology, synthesize it chemically, transplant it, score viability, iterate on failure, then annotate the surviving set. *Product: the correct experiment — and the honest note is that this is the experiment that was actually performed, so step 5's product here is recognition, not invention.*
6. Data (unfiltered): **the literal instruction "run the experiment" is not executable in this run** — a documentary audit has no wet-lab capacity. What was executed in its place was retrieval of that experiment's published result from a primary source, fetched this run. That substitution — *design a retrieval, retrieve it* for *design an experiment, run it* — is the substitution [`RUNBOOK.md`](../RUNBOOK.md) makes in all ~270 audit files, and **canon does not license it anywhere**; it is recorded here as a declared derivation, not as compliance, and the run continues under it rather than halting. Data, unfiltered: syn1.0 1,079 kb → syn3.0 531 kb, 473 genes, smaller than any autonomously replicating natural cell; **149 genes of unknown function**; the first knowledge-based design **failed to produce a viable cell**; improved transposon mutagenesis revealed a class of **quasi-essential** genes needed for robust growth, which explained the failure; three design–synthesis–test cycles were required. For contrast, 38 per cent of *E. coli* K-12's 4,288 genes had no attributed function at genome publication.
7. Variable Principle applied: **Measured** — 473 genes, 531 kb, 149 unannotated, first design non-viable, three cycles. **Structurally derived** — 149/473 = 31.5 per cent of a minimal cell's genes unannotated; the bound 473 ≤ minimal set ≤ the parent's complement, *for this chassis, medium and growth criterion only*. **Held open** — the functions of the 149; any organism-independent minimal set; whether the quasi-essential class is a property of biology or of the chosen growth criterion. The falsifier of step 3 **fired**: 31.5 per cent is far above the 10 per cent threshold fixed in advance. Not filled by assumption — the 149 are not assumed to be regulatory, redundant or parasitic, and the temptation to call them "quasi-essential overhead" is refused, because quasi-essentiality is a separately measured category in the source and the two are not interchangeable.
8. Model update (Capsule: what the failed parts contribute): this step earns its place on this question more cleanly than anywhere else in the audit. The decisive event in the real research **was a failure** — the first design, built from *"collective knowledge of molecular biology"*, produced no viable cell, and that failure is what exposed the quasi-essential class; the paper says so in those words. Canon's step 8 names exactly that mechanism, and names it as the normal route rather than as a setback. Updated model: a minimal genome is not the set of genes whose functions are individually known to be required; it is that set, plus a class visible only through the failure of the first set, plus roughly a third of the total whose functions remain unknown.
9. Documented: this file, with every number carrying its source URL and retrieval date, the step-3 threshold recorded before step 6, and the step-6 substitution declared.
10. Next baseline: the next question loads this one's result and is sharper for it — *what is the 149?* Two cheap follow-ups are now well-posed: whether the unannotated fraction of a constructed minimal cell exceeds that of its free-living relatives (31.5 per cent here against 38 per cent for *E. coli* at publication, which is **not** a like-for-like comparison and is recorded as a question, not a finding), and whether the quasi-essential class shrinks when the growth criterion is relaxed.

## Forcing Test
- Test A (Ground): within-system as to the instrument (its steps are fixed text, executed); grounded on the reality side by two primary sources fetched this run. The *question* is an empirical one, not a ground question.
- Test B (Uniqueness): two candidates for what the 10 steps are. (i) *An ordinary statement of scientific method with canon's vocabulary pasted on.* (ii) *An ordinary statement of scientific method plus two substantive additions — step 7's measured/derived/held-open split and step 8's treatment of failure as substrate — that standard four-step summaries (question → hypothesis → experiment → conclusion) omit.* Both survive. (i) is right about steps 1–6 and 9–10, which contain nothing a working scientist lacks. (ii) is right about steps 7 and 8, and this run is a clean demonstration: step 7 forced the 149 to stay open under three separate temptations to explain them, and step 8 named the mechanism that the experimenters themselves credit for the result. Not forced; the honest verdict is that both are true of different parts of the algorithm.
- Test C (Direction): the arrow runs domain → model: the cell is the source and the model is what changes. That is the opposite of the Master Meta-Algorithm's operator → domain arrow ([RUN-018](RUN-018.md)), which is why this instrument completes on a bacterial question and that one blocks. Direction confirms the audit's instrument choice was correct, and gives the reason the audit never stated.
- Test D (Falsifiability): falsifier for the instrument, fixed before the run — *find a step whose product is unobtainable, or whose product is wrong, on a real open bacterial question.* It **half fired**, at step 6: *"run the experiment"* is unobtainable for a documentary audit, and the substitution used in its place is undeclared in canon and in the RUNBOOK (this record declares it). No step produced a wrong product. The falsifier for the *question's* leading hypothesis (step 3, >10 per cent unannotated) also fired, which is what makes the run a test and not a restatement.
- **Outcome:** `live-hypothesis` for the claim that the 10 steps are a sufficient procedure for inquiry, with the falsifier attached and one half-fire recorded. **Verdict on the ~270 uses: earned, with one declaration owed.** Earned, because the instrument forces three things an audit will otherwise skip — a falsifier written before the data (step 3), the three-way split of what is measured, derived and held open (step 7), and a use for the parts that failed (step 8) — and because F-084's sealed-prediction result and F-085's indeterminate one both came out of that discipline rather than around it. The declaration owed: **step 6 has been executed ~270 times as retrieval, not as experiment**, and that substitution is why F-085 could return neither a fired nor an unfired falsifier — a directed literature search is not an experiment, and canon's step 6 does not say what to do when the two are swapped. `diff_state: accounted`: canon's instrument and the reality of how the minimal-genome result was obtained are consistent, in detail, including the role of the failure. `utility: works-descriptive` — every statement it produced is correct, and none of them is one the algorithm's structure supplied rather than the literature; steps 7 and 8 are real added discipline but they shape how an answer is *reported*, not what is found.

## Anti-Operation (the gap this opens)

- **Step 6 needs a documentary variant, or the audit needs to stop calling its retrievals experiments.** Proposed wording, offered as a derivation: *"6a. If the experiment has been run by others, retrieve it from a primary source and record the source and retrieval date; the falsifiability condition of step 3 must be fixed before retrieval."* The last clause is what would have saved F-085. Until the author declares something here, ~270 audit files contain a step executed in a way canon does not describe — a `methodology-gap` in the audit's own instrument, disclosed rather than absorbed.
- **The algorithm has no step that ends an inquiry.** Step 10 always iterates. This is the same structural absence F-066 found in the base code — the loop has no operation that terminates a response — and it appears here at the meta-level: canon's inquiry instrument cannot output "this question is answered, stop", only "iterate at higher baseline". For a 35-mechanism sweep that is a cost, because nothing tells the operator when a variable has been narrowed enough.
- **Step 8 is asserted, not measured, when it fails.** On this question the failure genuinely carried information, and the source says so. Canon's wording — *"failed hypotheses contribute information"* — makes that universal, and nothing in the algorithm asks whether a particular failure contributed anything. A failure that teaches nothing is a real category, and canon has no slot for it. Compare F-067, where the same universality claim (*"nothing is discarded"*) is graded `forced-no` on its functional half.
- **The 149 are the standing open variable.** Held open here, deliberately. Recorded for review: if the unannotated fraction of a constructed minimal cell has fallen substantially by the review date, that is the single most informative update to this record.

## Narrative stripped (if any)

- *"Per the Slime Mold mechanism"* (step 8): the mechanism's name is stripped; what is kept is its operational content — treat the failed design as data about the space, not as a setback. Reality here supports the operational content and says nothing about slime moulds. The wider Capsule claim it belongs to is graded elsewhere (F-067) and is not rescued by this instance.
- The framing of the Variable Principle as canon's own discovery is not evaluated here; step 7's *content* — do not fill a remaining variable with assumption — is what was executed, and it held the 149 open. Whether the principle is original to canon is outside this record.
- The word *minimal* is stripped of its absolute reading throughout: what was measured is minimal **for one chassis, one medium and one growth criterion**, which is what step 1 extracted and what the last row of `## Reality shows` holds open.
