## Canon says

Layer 0's algorithm (v5.1 line 2232), quoted in full:

> Use when: applying DETECT → PROCESS → RESPOND to any input.
> 1. Identify the input. Name what just arrived in your processing space.
> 2. Locate your current Kingdom. Are you receiving from First Kingdom reactivity, Second Kingdom system, or Third Kingdom awareness?
> 3. Move up the Kingdoms. Reactive responses operate from First. Process the input through Second Kingdom organization. Receive it into Third Kingdom awareness before deciding.
> 4. Apply the processing architecture. Run the input through the appropriate framework, system, or filter for this domain.
> 5. Generate output. The response becomes the next cycle's input.
> 6. Close and reopen. The loop never ends — it returns to step 1 at a new baseline.

Layer text (lines 264–336) makes the universality claim that licenses running this on a bacterium: "The code does not change between expressions… The code is invariant across all of them… Per Correspondence: the code must be identical at every scale because reality is self-similar at every scale. **This is law, not analogy.**" The RESPOND row adds the load-bearing dynamic clause: "Per Rhythm: the algorithm is not a line. It is a spiral. **Each completion of the cycle returns to DETECT at a higher baseline.** Per Fibonacci: the baseline of each new cycle is informed by the two prior cycles. Per the Capsule (Layer I.G): the baseline integrates ALL prior cycles."

The Cross-Domain Convergence Table's Biological row: "DETECT: Homeostatic deviation or threat | PROCESS: Cellular signaling cascade + gene expression | RESPOND: Adapted equilibrium." The Self-Application Test claims Layer 0 "complies with its own governing epistemology."

**Split (RUNBOOK rule 4).** *Factual core graded here:* the six numbered steps are executable against a bacterial cell, and step 6's "returns to step 1 at a new baseline" (layer text: "at a **higher** baseline") describes what the cell measurably does. *Framework mapping (not graded here):* the Three Kingdoms names attached to processing levels, and the Rhythm / Fibonacci / Capsule attributions.

## Algorithm run (literal, step by step, subject = a bacterial cell, *E. coli* K-12)

**Step 1 — "Identify the input. Name what just arrived in your processing space."**
Produced. A whole cell has no single input, so the step forces a choice of sub-system; I took the best-measured one: a step addition of α-methyl-aspartate at the cell surface, whose occupancy of the Tar periplasmic domain is an independent observable (K_D = 2.6 ± 0.5 µM). **Result: completable, and it forces the operator to name an observable. Non-empty but not informative — naming an input is prior to any framework.**

**Step 2 — "Locate your current Kingdom. Are you receiving from First Kingdom reactivity, Second Kingdom system, or Third Kingdom awareness?"**
`BLOCKED:` the step is written in the second person and asks for a self-report. Canon's own definitions of the three levels are stated in terms that have no measurable occupant in a bacterium: First = "reacts to input without deliberate processing architecture"; Second = "processes through **intentional** structures: plans, systems, frameworks"; Third = "**aware that it is running the algorithm**". Deliberateness, intention and awareness are not measurable in *E. coli*, and no proxy is offered by Layer 0. The cell measurably has architecture (a 12-nm receptor lattice, a 4 s temporal comparison, covalent methylation memory) yet nothing measured distinguishes "architecture" from "intentional structure". The step cannot be completed without filling a held-open variable with assumption, which AGENTS.md rule 5 forbids.

**Step 3 — "Move up the Kingdoms… Receive it into Third Kingdom awareness before deciding."**
`BLOCKED:` there is no operation on a bacterium that moves it up a Kingdom. The instruction presupposes an agent that can elect its own processing level between DETECT and RESPOND. *E. coli* has one wiring diagram per genotype and cannot choose another inside a cycle. This is a structural result, not a shortage of data: **steps 2 and 3 are operator protocol, not system description, and Layer 0 claims to be system description ("law, not analogy", "invariant across all expressions").** Continued below under the explicit stipulation that steps 2–3 are skipped, in order to test whether the remainder produces anything.

**Step 4 — "Apply the processing architecture. Run the input through the appropriate framework, system, or filter for this domain."**
Produced, with measured content: the polar receptor array (12-nm hexagonal lattice, conserved across 13 species of several phyla) coupled to CheA/CheW, with measured amplification of slope −36 ± 1 between fractional occupancy change and kinase output. **Result: completable and correct. Every gram of the content came from microbiology; the step contributed the instruction "there is an architecture", which is true of any mechanism whatever.**

**Step 5 — "Generate output. The response becomes the next cycle's input."**
Produced, and here the step says something with content: CheY-P biases motor switching, giving runs of 0.64 s and tumbles of 0.19 s, and because the cell moves, its own response changes the concentration it next detects. The second clause is *not* true of every cycle in the same organism: the flagellar motor's rotation rate is proportional to the protonmotive force (0–270 Hz) and its rotation does not re-enter the pmf sensor. **Result: true here, false for another measured cycle in the same cell — a conditional, not a law.**

**Step 6 — "Close and reopen. The loop never ends — it returns to step 1 at a new baseline."** (Layer text: "returns to DETECT at a **higher** baseline"; "the baseline integrates ALL prior cycles".)
Produced — and **contradicted by measurement**, twice, independently:
1. *Chemotaxis.* Adaptation is the measured return of the output to the **same** baseline. Dose-response curves of adapted cells taken at ambient 0, 0.1, 0.5 and 5 mM MeAsp collapse onto one curve. The whole of what the cell carries forward is a 4 s window (past 1 s minus previous 3 s). Nothing is integrated from earlier cycles.
2. *Division.* The adder rule adds a **constant** volume each generation irrespective of where the cell started, in *E. coli*, *B. subtilis* and *Caulobacter*, with two parameters and no adjustable ones; critical-size models are explicitly disproved; autocorrelations of birth size, division size and generation time decay by a factor 2 per generation and mother–daughter generation-time correlation is −1/4. The measured architecture is a forgetting mechanism, and it is what makes size stable.
**Result: `fails`. Canon's "higher baseline" and "integrates ALL prior cycles" are the falsifiable part of Layer 0's algorithm, and both measured cross-cycle rules in this organism are baseline-restoring or baseline-resetting.**

## Scientific Inquiry run
1. Question (precise): run literally against *E. coli* K-12, does Layer 0's six-step algorithm complete, and does any of its steps produce a correct non-trivial statement about the bacterium that the layer's structure rather than general microbiology supplied?
2. What an answer must look like: for each of the six steps, either the product with its measured occupant, or a named blocker; then, for each product, the observation that would have made it false.
3. Falsifiability condition: the algorithm's only cross-cycle claim is step 6. It is false if a measured, repeated stimulus–response cycle in this organism returns to the *same* baseline, or resets by a fixed increment, rather than to a higher one; it survives if some measured quantity rises monotonically across cycles.
4. Variables: measurable / bounded / held open: **Measurable** — K_D, array spacing, amplification slope, run/tumble times, the collapse of adapted dose-response curves, the adder rule and its autocorrelation decay, motor speed vs pmf. **Bounded** — the memory horizon of chemotaxis (bounded at ≈4 s by the impulse-response measurement) and of the division cycle (bounded by a factor-2 autocorrelation decay per generation). **Held open** — whether a bacterium has any occupant of the Second and Third Kingdom as canon defines them (intent, awareness); this is held open, not answered, and steps 2–3 depend on it.
5. Test designed: execute the six steps in order on one named input; then look specifically for the cross-cycle quantity step 6 requires, in the two systems where cycle-to-cycle behaviour has been measured to single-cell resolution (chemotactic adaptation; size homeostasis in the mother machine).
6. Data (unfiltered): table above. Adapted dose-response curves collapse across a 50-fold ambient range. The chemotactic memory is a 4 s window. The adder adds constant volume with no adjustable parameters across three species, and correlations decay by 2 per generation. No measured monotone cross-cycle quantity was found.
7. Variable Principle applied: **Measured** — steps 1, 4 and 5 have measured occupants; step 6's requirement has measured counter-occupants. **Structurally derived** — that steps 2 and 3 are operator instructions rather than system descriptions (derived from their second-person form plus the absence of any measurable proxy for "awareness"). **Held open** — the Kingdom location of a bacterium, per canon's own ◇ on the hard problem. I do not fill it.
8. Model update (Capsule: what the failed parts contribute): the failed part is precise and useful. Layer 0 conflates two different things under one name: (a) a three-operation *parse* of a signal-transduction event, which has measured boundaries here (ligand occupancy / kinase activity / motor bias), and (b) a *spiral* claim about what happens between cycles, which measurement contradicts in this organism. (a) survives step 6's failure. The honest restatement is that the loop returns to DETECT at a baseline that may be restored, reset by a fixed increment, or raised, and that which of the three occurs is the interesting measurable — exactly the quantity canon asserts away. Note also that "each new cycle is informed by the two prior cycles" (Fibonacci) is quantitatively testable and measurably false here: the mother–daughter generation-time correlation is −1/4 and decays by 2 per generation, so a cell is informed by its immediate past with a *negative* coefficient.
9. Documented: this file. Correspondence: one scale down — the two-state equilibrium of a single receptor dimer, which has no cycle counter; one scale up — a population, where the adder's factor-2 decay is what keeps the size distribution stationary rather than drifting upward.
10. Next baseline: Layer 0's parse is retained as a lens for signal transduction (already F-064); its spiral clause is graded forced-no at bacterial scale. Steps 2–3 are recorded as inapplicable to any non-reflective system, which bears on canon's "law, not analogy".

## Forcing Test
- Test A (Ground): grounded. Every step's product or blocker is tied to a fetched measurement or to the quoted canonical wording. Genome length and gene count fetched from NCBI this run; the rest reused from `audit/sweep/` with their original primary sources named.
- Test B (Uniqueness): not unique for steps 1, 4, 5 — many parses of the same machinery satisfy them (SWEEP-001 Test B). **Unique for step 6:** given the measured collapse of adapted dose-response curves and the adder's constant increment, no reading of "returns at a higher baseline" survives in this organism.
- Test C (Direction): canon runs from the algorithm to the mechanism and expects three operations plus an ascending spiral. Measurement runs from the molecule outward and finds bounded-memory, baseline-restoring loops whose forgetting is load-bearing. The directions differ, and at step 6 they point opposite ways.
- Test D (Falsifiability): the condition set at step 3 of the inquiry was met — two independent measured cross-cycle rules return to the same or a reset baseline. Step 6 is falsified at this scale; steps 1, 4, 5 could not have been falsified by any observation of a functioning cell, and are therefore vacuous as applied; steps 2 and 3 are not evaluable.
- **Outcome:** `forced-no` for Layer 0's step-6 spiral clause at bacterial scale. `utility: fails` for the algorithm as a whole, on SCHEMA §3b's rule that a layer producing a statement measurement contradicts grades `fails` — with the explicit note that but for step 6, the grade would have been `vacuous`, since nothing observable about a living cell could have falsified steps 1, 4 or 5.

## Anti-Operation (the gap this opens)
1. **What rule replaces "higher baseline"?** Canon has no vocabulary for a loop whose function is to forget. A measured forgetting horizon (4 s; factor-2 per generation) is a candidate quantity for Layer 0 and has no home in it.
2. **How much of Layer 0 is operator protocol?** Two of six steps cannot be run on a system without self-report. Canon nowhere distinguishes "the algorithm reality runs" from "the algorithm an operator runs", yet the per-layer algorithms are written in the second person throughout. If they are protocols, the universality claim at line 264–336 is about the three operations only, and the algorithms do not inherit it.
3. **Does Fibonacci survive anywhere biological?** The one measured inter-generation coefficient found here is −1/4. Canon asserts the baseline "is informed by the two prior cycles" with no sign condition and no error term.
4. Held open: whether any measured bacterial quantity does rise monotonically across cycles (the held-open row above). A directed search for one is the next test.
