## Canon says

v5.1, Layer I.E — The Energy Continuation Algorithm, quoted in full as the instrument under test:

> "Use when: any system, project, or process appears to be running out of energy, momentum, or resources.
> 1. Identify the gradient that powered the system's previous activity. Every functional process runs on a polarity.
> 2. Determine the gradient's current state. Is it intact? Collapsing? Already collapsed?
> 3. If the gradient is intact: the system has energy; the issue is elsewhere (likely processing architecture).
> 4. If the gradient is collapsing: identify what new gradient the collapse can seed. Per Punctuated Equilibrium: collapse is the gradient that powers the next expansion.
> 5. If the gradient is already collapsed: locate the new gradient that should already be establishing. If none is establishing, the failure is upstream — trace back to the missing Second or Third Kingdom causation.
> 6. Establish the new gradient deliberately. A gradient is created by establishing differential between two states (current and desired, supply and demand, current state and vision, problem and solution).
> 7. Channel the gradient's collapse productively. The collapse releases energy. Direct that energy into the work the system is meant to do.
> 8. Anticipate the next collapse. Energy continuation is endless gradient renewal. Plan for what gradient the current collapse will seed."

The Four Resolutions the steps rest on: **One** — *"The form changed dozens of times. The energy did not diminish by one quantum."* **Two** — *"Every ATP produced inside the cell creates more disorder somewhere else than the order created within… the moment throughput stops, entropy resumes."* **Three** — the total-energy question, explicitly held open. **Four** — *"every functional process in the universe runs on a polarity… gradients are renewed by the collapse of prior gradients. Stars die, but supernovae seed the next gradient. Cells die, but apoptosis feeds the next cell… The collapse IS the seed."* With *"Per Correspondence: same answer at every scale. Zero exceptions."*

**Split (RUNBOOK rule 4).** *Factual core graded here:* the eight steps, run on a real system, correctly locate what powers it and correctly say where its next power comes from. *Framework mapping (not graded; see Narrative stripped):* Punctuated Equilibrium, Mentalism, the Kingdoms, "the collapse IS the seed" as a theological figure.

## The algorithm run, literally, step by step

**System:** an *E. coli* K-12 cell in a glucose-limited chemostat whose carbon feed is being withdrawn — canon's stated use case ("appears to be running out of energy").

1. **Identify the gradient that powered the system's previous activity.** Produced: **the protonmotive force, ≈150 mV (Δψ ≈ −115 mV plus −59ΔpH ≈ 35 mV).** Correct, and confirmed by an independent measurement of the right kind: motor speed is proportional to pmf across 0–270 Hz and the stators are ion translocators, not ATPases. This step could have produced the wrong answer — it does not name ATP — and canon's own Appendix A Stage 23 ("ATP… Universal currency") does produce the wrong answer for this device. **Step 1 works.**
2. **Determine the gradient's current state.** Produced: collapsing, and measurable as such (pmf is a measurable quantity; carbon withdrawal collapses it). **Step 2 works.**
3. **(Conditional, not entered — the gradient is not intact.)** Recorded for completeness: had it been intact, the step would have produced "the issue is elsewhere", which is a disjunction, not a location.
4. **If collapsing: identify what new gradient the collapse can seed.** Produced: **nothing.** Measured behaviour on pmf collapse is (i) F₁F_o runs in reverse, hydrolysing ATP to pump protons — the cell spends a *second* currency to rebuild the *same* gradient, with a measured anti-waste ratchet to stop it doing so needlessly — and (ii) the stringent response reduces demand, the cell idling >20% of its proteome as non-translating ribosomes rather than establishing anything new. No new gradient is seeded by this collapse. **Step 4's premise — "collapse is the gradient that powers the next expansion" — is contradicted at this scale.** Canon's own biological instance for it ("cells die, but apoptosis feeds the next cell") fails independently: the bacterial candidate mechanism is measured fully reversible, its toxins share no mechanism, the one demonstrated function is phage defence, and the commonest measured death is osmotic lysis, which seeds nothing on purpose.
5. **If already collapsed: locate the new gradient that should already be establishing; if none, trace back to the missing Second or Third Kingdom causation.** `BLOCKED: no measurable referent exists for "Second or Third Kingdom causation" in a bacterium.` The step's only diagnostic branch terminates in an unmeasurable category, so a literal run cannot complete it. Steps 6–8 are therefore reported below as the *counterfactual* continuation, flagged as such, not as completed steps of the literal run.
6. *(after the blocker)* **Establish the new gradient deliberately.** `BLOCKED: the step presumes an operator outside the system.` All four of canon's named differentials — current/desired, supply/demand, current state/vision, problem/solution — require an agent that holds a desired state. A bacterium is the system, not its operator. This is a structural, not incidental, non-runnability: **the algorithm is not executable on the system canon's Correspondence claim most needs it for.**
7. *(after the blocker)* **Channel the gradient's collapse productively.** This is the one step with a precise measured referent: F₁F_o couples the collapse of the pmf to ATP synthesis at a structural 10/3 = 3.33 H⁺/ATP in *E. coli*. Note what the measurement adds and canon does not carry: in the one enzyme where both ratios exist, the thermodynamic ratio (4.0 ± 0.1) exceeds the structural ratio (4.7 ⇒ ratio 0.85), i.e. channelling the collapse costs **15% inside the device itself.**
8. *(after the blocker)* **Anticipate the next collapse.** No referent: nothing in the cell anticipates, and the measured response to imminent carbon exhaustion is demand reduction, not gradient scheduling.

**The conversion-premium test (asked explicitly of this layer).** Does the Four Resolutions structure accommodate a conversion premium? **Partly, and not where canon puts it.**
- Resolution One actively obstructs it: *"the energy did not diminish by one quantum"* is true of energy and false of *available* energy, and every measured conversion in the cell loses availability — 15% inside one enzyme, and the cell runs three non-equivalent currencies (ATP, PEP, Δp) between which conversion is lossy.
- Resolution Two **is** the correct slot and canon did not connect it: *"Every ATP produced inside the cell creates more disorder somewhere else than the order created within"* is exactly a conversion premium, stated qualitatively. Canon has the right home and never names an exchange rate in it.
- No resolution can express an exchange rate, which is the quantity that matters once there are three currencies.

This refines [FINDINGS](../FINDINGS.md) F-074 ("Layer I.E's 'did not diminish by one quantum' cannot express it") by locating the fix: F-074 proposes *adding* conversion loss to Layer I.E; this run finds the slot already exists in Resolution Two and that Resolution One must be amended to stop contradicting it.

## Scientific Inquiry run

1. Question (precise): Run against *E. coli* K-12 in carbon limitation, do Layer I.E's eight steps locate what powers the cell and correctly state where its next power comes from — and can the Four Resolutions express the measured cost of converting between its energy currencies?
2. What an answer must look like: the eight steps executed with outputs recorded and blockers named; for step 1 a named gradient checkable against an independent measurement; for step 4 a named successor gradient checkable against measurement; and for each Resolution, whether a conversion premium can be stated inside it.
3. Falsifiability condition: step 1 is refuted if the cell's measured activity tracks a stock (e.g. [ATP]) rather than a gradient. Step 4 is refuted if a measured collapse seeds no new gradient. The accommodation claim is refuted if no Resolution can hold a measured loss.
4. Variables: measurable / bounded / held open: **Measurable** — pmf and its components, motor speed, structural and thermodynamic H⁺/ATP, ATP per substrate imported, idle-ribosome fraction. **Bounded** — pmf ≈150 mV in an energised cell; in-enzyme conversion efficiency 0.85 in the one case measured. **Held open** — the *E. coli* reverse-flux magnitude; whether lysate measurably seeds a successor; whether the 0.85 figure transfers to *E. coli* (it is not entered as if it did).
5. Test designed: take the layer's strongest claim (processes run on polarities, not stocks) and its strongest exceptionless claim ("the collapse IS the seed… zero exceptions") and run both against the same organism in the same condition, so that one instrument is scored twice under one set of measurements.
6. Data (unfiltered): the table above plus the step outputs. Recorded including the part that spoils the tidy version: step 1 is a clean hit, and steps 4, 5, 6 and 8 comprise one contradicted premise and three unmeasurable referents in a row.
7. Variable Principle applied: **Measured** — the pmf powers the motor and the motor's speed tracks it; three currencies exist; one in-enzyme conversion premium of 15%. **Structurally derived** — that the cell's activity is gradient-powered generally (step 1's output, and it is also standard chemiosmotics, not a proprietary derivation). **Held open** — the reverse-flux rate and the lysate question, both left empty rather than estimated. The 0.85 figure is explicitly *not* transferred to *E. coli*.
8. Model update (Capsule: what the failed parts contribute): three usable products. (i) "The collapse IS the seed" should be restated as a disjunction — collapse either seeds a new gradient, is paid to rebuild the same gradient from another currency, or is met by demand reduction — and the bacterium exhibits the second and third. (ii) Resolution One should distinguish energy from available energy, and Resolution Two should carry a named exchange rate. (iii) Steps 5, 6 and 8 should be marked as requiring an operator, which makes the algorithm explicitly a *management* instrument and therefore not a Correspondence-universal one — a restriction canon can state honestly and which costs it less than the unfalsifiable version.
9. Documented: this file; extends [SWEEP-003](../sweep/SWEEP-003.md), [SWEEP-024](../sweep/SWEEP-024.md), [SWEEP-028](../sweep/SWEEP-028.md), [SWEEP-034](../sweep/SWEEP-034.md) and [FINDINGS](../FINDINGS.md) F-070, F-073, F-074 from content to algorithm.
10. Next baseline: an in-vivo *E. coli* reverse-flux measurement, and a measured *E. coli* exchange rate between PEP and ATP — the two numbers that would let Resolution Two carry a rate. Re-review 2027-09-24.

## Forcing Test

- Test A (Ground): Step 1 is grounded — a named gradient with an independently measured proportionality (motor speed ∝ pmf, 0–270 Hz, two laboratories). Steps 5, 6 and 8 are not grounded at all: their terms have no measurable referent in the target system.
- Test B (Uniqueness): Step 1 survives uniquely: the alternative candidate (ATP) is measurably ruled out for the motor, so exactly one reading of "what powered the previous activity" stands. Step 4 does not survive: measurement leaves *no* candidate successor gradient, so the step's premise is not merely unselected but empty here.
- Test C (Direction): Signed and measured for step 7 (collapse of a 150 mV pmf, channelled through a c₁₀ rotor, yields ATP at 3.33 H⁺ per ATP structurally, with a 15% in-device premium where measurable). Unsigned for step 4, whose direction — collapse *outward* into a successor — is the opposite of what is measured (inward, rebuilt at cost).
- Test D (Falsifiability): Mixed, and the mixture is the result. Step 1 was genuinely falsifiable and passed: had motor speed tracked [ATP] it would have failed, and it does not. Step 4's "zero exceptions" is falsifiable in principle, was tested, and **failed** — one exception is enough, and canon has no rule for what one exception costs a Correspondence claim. Steps 5, 6 and 8 are unfalsifiable because unrunnable.
- **Outcome:** `forced-no` for Resolution Four's exceptionless form and for step 4 at bacterial scale. **`utility: works-descriptive`** for the instrument: step 1's output is correct, confirmed, and non-trivial *relative to canon's own Appendix A* — but it is standard chemiosmotics, so general biology supplies it too, which is what `works-descriptive` means. The layer earns real credit for being right where canon's own stage table is wrong; it does not earn `works-forced`.

## Anti-Operation (the gap this opens)

1. **What replaces "the collapse IS the seed"?** The measured alternatives — pay another currency to rebuild the same gradient, or reduce demand — are both cheaper than seeding a successor and both are what the cell does. If they are the general case and seeding is the special case, Resolution Four's ordering is inverted, and canon's stars/supernovae instance may be the outlier rather than the archetype. That is testable one scale up and one scale down and is now the layer's live question.
2. **Exchange rates are the missing quantity.** With three currencies measured in one organism, "conservation" says nothing operational; the exchange rate does. Layer I.E has no place to put one, and Resolution Two is the place to make.
3. **Three of eight steps require an operator.** Canon should state which of its algorithms are executable by the system on itself and which require an outside agent. Nothing in v5.1–v5.4 makes that distinction, and it decides whether Correspondence carries the algorithms or only the principles.

## Narrative stripped (if any)

Removed from the graded core: Punctuated Equilibrium as the warrant for step 4 (the measured bacterial response to collapse is reversible dormancy or demand reduction, neither of which is a maximum contraction enabling a maximum expansion); the Kingdoms in step 5 (unmeasurable in this system — this is why the step is blocked rather than merely unsupported); and the theological reading of "the collapse IS the seed". Reality neither forces nor contradicts the Mentalist framing of Resolution Three, which canon itself holds open and which this run does not touch. What reality does contradict is narrower: the exceptionless *biological* instance of Resolution Four.
