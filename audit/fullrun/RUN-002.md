## Canon says

Layer I's algorithm (v5.1 line 2250), quoted in full:

> Use when: diagnosing the Three Kingdoms alignment of any system, person, organization, or process.
> 1. Identify the system. Name what you are diagnosing.
> 2. Locate its First Kingdom. What physical, material, measurable expression does it have?

Canon pre-fills the answer for this subject. "Three Kingdoms Across All Scales", **Cellular** row: "Third Kingdom = Homeostatic intelligence / organism intent | Second Kingdom = Genetic code / signaling pathways | First Kingdom = Cellular machinery / expressed proteins." The hierarchy is directional: Second Kingdom is "Everything capable of directing the First Kingdom… this is where causes are SET"; First Kingdom is "the **receptive medium** that receives the Second Kingdom's direction and manifests it into form."

PART V and PART IX are scoped explicitly away from the bacterium: PART V's Human Role clause locates local Third Kingdom expression in "each conscious being", and its ◈ holds open whether the Omni Force is conscious or impersonal; PART IX's ◈ holds open whether the Third Kingdom continues post-dissolution. Both are recorded here as held open and are **not** graded against *E. coli*.

**Split (RUNBOOK rule 4).** *Factual core graded here:* (a) a bacterium has a physically separable directing layer that is not identical with the machinery it directs; (b) the direction runs Second → First; (c) the cellular Third Kingdom is "homeostatic intelligence", i.e. regulation of measurable variables toward set-points. *Framework mapping (not graded):* the words "intelligence", "intent", "consciousness", and the Gender/Mentalism attributions.

## Algorithm run (literal, step by step, subject = a bacterial cell, *E. coli* K-12)

**Step 1 — "Identify the system."**
Produced: *Escherichia coli* K-12 MG1655; 4,641,652 bp, 4,672 curated gene records. **Completable; no content contributed by the layer.**

**Step 2 — "Locate its First Kingdom. What physical, material, measurable expression does it have?"**
Produced: the envelope, the proteome, the cytoplasm at 0.3–0.4 g/ml macromolecules, the nucleoid occupying about half the cytosol, the flagellar motor. **Completable. Unfalsifiable: any physical object satisfies it.**

**Step 3 — "Locate its Second Kingdom. What organizing thought, system, framework, or strategy directs the First Kingdom?"**
Produced, and this is the layer's one real hit. Canon's cellular row says "genetic code / signaling pathways"; measurement supplies a directing layer that is *physically separable at domain resolution* — at least 62 two-component ORFs (32 response regulators, 23 orthodox and 5 hybrid kinases) whose proteins have "an intrinsic modularity that separates signal input, phosphotransfer, and output response", with only ~3% cross-talk between non-cognate pairs. The slot is genuinely occupied, and occupied by a modular architecture rather than by an undifferentiated mush. **Completable and correct.** But see the grading question: step 3 asks only that *something* organise the machinery, which is satisfied by any regulated system; and the step is measurably *not* satisfied for a large part of this cell — the dominant organiser of the cytosolic interior is macromolecular crowding (excluded volume), i.e. physics with no encoded direction, and cell size is set by an adder with no size-sensing regulator at all (RUN-004, SWEEP-030).

**Step 4 — "Locate its Third Kingdom. What awareness, vision, identity, or consciousness grounds the Second Kingdom?"**
`BLOCKED:` no measurement reaches awareness, vision, identity or consciousness in a bacterium, and canon's own registers hold the question open (Domain 3's ◇; PART V's ◈). Canon nevertheless pre-fills the slot in the Cellular row with "**homeostatic intelligence** / organism intent". That phrase has exactly one measurable reading — regulation of a measured variable toward a set-point — and that reading is **contradicted** in this organism's best-characterised sensory system: rotational bias tracks the *rate of change* of receptor occupancy, not its level, and adapted dose-response curves collapse across a 50-fold ambient range. The second measured candidate behaves the same way: the acetate-overflow threshold is reproduced by a *fixed allocation rule whose optimum moves*, not by a cell adjusting a set-point. So the step is blocked on its stated terms and its only testable proxy fails. Continued below under the stipulation that step 4 is skipped.

**Step 5 — "Test alignment. Does the First Kingdom express the Second? Does the Second Kingdom serve the Third?"**
First half: partly yes — expressed proteins are encoded, and two-component output domains act on physiology. But the step tests a **one-way arrow**, and measurement shows a closed loop: conjugative transfer is integrated into the recipient chromosome in up to 96% of recipients; CRISPR spacer acquisition writes environmental experience into the chromosomal array; and the code itself changes at ~1×10⁻³ per genome per generation. The First Kingdom writes into the Second, routinely and measurably. Second half: `BLOCKED:` step 4 has no occupant, so "does the Second serve the Third" has no truth value here.

**Step 6 — "Identify the misalignment. If output is poor, the misalignment is somewhere in steps 2–4."**
`BLOCKED:` "poor output" is undefined for a bacterium and canon offers no criterion. The obvious candidate, growth rate, is disqualified by measurement in both directions: slow-growing cells hold over 20% of the proteome as inactive ribosome equivalents (readiness, not malfunction), and fast-growing cells excrete acetate — a usable carbon and energy source — above λ_ac ≈ 0.76 h⁻¹. Neither is a misalignment; both are measured optima.

**Step 7 — "Correct upward. Clarify Third Kingdom intent, then rebuild Second Kingdom systems to serve it."**
`BLOCKED:` there is no operation on *E. coli* that clarifies intent. The corrective half of the algorithm requires an operator inside the system; a bacterium has none. Note what this costs canon: Layer I.B's opening sentence is "the same algorithm that runs a cell runs a city. Not analogy. Same law at a different scale." Four of this algorithm's seven steps cannot be run on the cell.

## Forcing Test
- Test A (Ground): grounded. Genome length and gene count fetched from NCBI this run; all other values reused from `audit/sweep/` files with their original primary sources named in the table.
- Test B (Uniqueness): not unique for steps 2 and 3 — any regulated physical system can be split into "material expression" and "what organises it", and several such splits of *E. coli* are equally consistent with the data (the split could be put at the genome, at the regulatory proteome, or at the domain boundary inside one kinase). Nothing measured selects one. For step 4's proxy the data *are* decisive in the negative: no reading of "homeostatic" survives a system with no set-point.
- Test C (Direction): canon runs top-down (clarify intent, rebuild systems, let matter follow). Measurement in this organism runs bottom-up as often as top-down: the environment writes into the chromosome by conjugation, spacer acquisition and mutation. The directions differ, and step 7 has no bottom-up counterpart.
- Test D (Falsifiability): the condition set at inquiry step 3 was met on both testable parts. (i) The reverse arrow is measured. (ii) The set-point reading of "homeostatic intelligence" is contradicted. The Kingdom labels themselves remain unfalsifiable by any observation of a cell — had canon not pre-filled the Cellular row with a regulatory claim, the whole run would have graded `vacuous`.
- **Outcome:** `forced-no` for canon's cellular Third Kingdom on its only measurable reading, and for step 5's one-way direction test. `utility: fails` per SCHEMA §3b. `intake_result: open` rather than `adjusted`: the contradicting measurements (Segall 1986; Sourjik & Berg 2002; Basan 2015) are graded `measured-single`, and substrate item 8 forbids a weak observation driving an adjustment.

## Anti-Operation (the gap this opens)
1. **Canon has no rule for a Kingdom slot with no occupant.** Layer ◇ holds variables open at the level of *claims*; nothing tells an operator what to do when step 4 of a mandatory diagnostic returns nothing. The algorithm's remaining steps (5–7) all depend on it, so one empty slot disables four steps.
2. **No output-quality criterion for a non-commercial system.** Step 6 fires "if output is poor". Canon's examples are all organisations with revenue. For a cell, measured waste (>20% inactive ribosome equivalents; acetate excretion) is optimal, so the trigger cannot be evaluated.
3. **The second arrow.** If matter writes into code at measured rates, canon must state what constrains the upward arrow, or the Kingdoms are a cycle rather than a hierarchy — which would change Layer I's "Foundation, not peak" claim from a structure to a choice of viewpoint.
4. Held open: whether "homeostatic intelligence" was ever meant as a measurable claim. This is a question for the author, not a gap measurement can close.
