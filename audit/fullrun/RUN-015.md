## Algorithm run (Layer VII, literal, step by step)

**Subject: a bacterial cell (*E. coli* K-12). Target process: nutrient acquisition and conversion — the measured process that most closely matches "attention must be converted into commitment, opportunity into outcome" without a human counterparty.**

**Step 1 — Acquisition: "Centralize attention channels. Tag at capture. Automate first response. Track ROI per channel. Reallocate to highest converters."** **RAN, with one striking result and three qualifications.**
- *Centralize channels:* half true, measurably. Sensing is centralized — multiple chemoreceptor species converge on one shared kinase output with gain of 27–36 and single-molecule sensitivity. Capture is *not* centralized: solute import runs on three independent energization currencies (ATP via ABC, PEP via PTS, the gradient via GalP/LacY). Canon's instruction "centralize all lead channels" is contradicted for capture and confirmed for sensing.
- *Tag at capture:* **this is the genuine hit of the file.** The PTS phosphorylates the sugar *during* transport — "the sugar arrives inside already phosphorylated" — so the capture event and the tagging event are one event, exactly as canon prescribes and for the same reason (an untagged capture cannot be attributed or routed). Better still, the same protein closes canon's ROI loop: unphosphorylated IIA^Glc inhibits several non-PTS uptake systems and phosphorylated P-IIA^Glc activates adenylate cyclase, so *the capture device is also the sensor that reallocates the other channels*. Canon's three separate instructions — tag at capture, track ROI at capture, reallocate to highest converters — are implemented by one measured protein.
- *Automate first response, zero lag:* confirmed at the measured scale — under 30 s from kinase to cognate regulator for every pair tested, against a standing inventory of at least 62 transducers.
- *Reallocate to highest converters:* contradicted in the general case. Across 91 regulator mutants, two thirds changed absolute fluxes but "the partitioning between different pathways remained largely stable". The cell reallocates at one branch point, not across the portfolio.

**Step 2 — Creation: run the seven-step script (Permission, Discovery, Pain Excavation, Vision Pull, Solution Bridge, Offer Stack, Close).** **BLOCKED: every step requires a counterparty that has a situation it can report, a cost it can quantify, a desired state it can describe, and the standing to grant permission.** No bacterial counterpart exists for any of the four. Recorded so it is not later mistaken for a gap in the search: the available forced reading was to map "Permission + Frame" onto type IV pilus contact (PilT motors engage almost only on surface contact; adhesion is tension-reinforced; stall force 110 ± 30 pN), which does look like consent followed by escalating commitment. That mapping is rejected here as exactly the move method 3 of this run forbids — no observation would have made it false, because any contact-then-engage mechanism satisfies it, and the resemblance was noticed only after the mechanism was in hand.

**Step 3 — I.A.C.E. objection loop.** **BLOCKED: the loop's premise is a stated objection that differs from the real one ("Prospect says 'it's too expensive' — that is rarely the actual objection").** That requires a counterparty capable of misreporting its own state; no measured bacterial mechanism does this (held-open row above).
One sub-step is separately testable and is the more interesting result. Canon's *Acknowledge* step is a law: "Match frequency… Per Vibration: **match before you shift**." Measured chemotaxis does exactly this — receptor methylation resets the cell to the ambient level before it responds to further change, so hard that dose-response families collapse onto one curve across a 50-fold range of ambient attractant. But the same organism supplies the exception: cheRcheB cells cannot adapt at all, make no short-term comparisons over 12 s, and still respond. So "match before you shift" is **not** exceptionless even inside the organism canon would cite for it; it is a design that improves dynamic range, not a law.

**Step 4 — Expansion: "automated referral sequences, monthly result reporting, quarterly review sessions. Expansion comes from served clients, not pursued ones. Lifetime value is created here."** **BLOCKED, and the blocker is structural rather than technical.** ACE's economics require the acquired party to *survive the transaction and transact again*. In the measured cell the acquired party is destroyed by the act of acquisition: the PTS commits the substrate irreversibly at the moment of capture. Lifetime value is zero by construction, referral is impossible, and "served clients" has no referent. This is not a missing bacterial organ; it is an inversion. A cell's growth comes entirely from *pursued and consumed* opportunities, which is the configuration canon names as the failure mode.

**Step 5 — "Document every interaction in CRM at capture. If not written, did not happen."** **RAN and is contradicted.** The cell writes a *subset* of captures: CRISPR adaptation writes captured foreign sequence into the array, with measured hotspots and a measured self/non-self error rate. Every other capture — each sugar molecule, each ion, each transported amino acid — is never written anywhere, and those interactions demonstrably happened, at a rate that supports a 20-minute doubling. Read strictly, canon's sentence denies the existence of the bulk of the organism's traffic.

**Step 6 — "Measure conversion at each step in the funnel. Identify the lowest-conversion step. Improve that step before optimizing elsewhere."** **RAN and returned a wrong prescription.** Two blockers and one error:
- No measured agent inside the cell computes a funnel conversion rate. The step presupposes an observer that the system does not contain.
- Assembling the funnel externally from the measured channels available, the lowest conversion is P1-mediated generalized transduction at about 1e-6, against conjugative integration at up to 96 percent.
- Canon's instruction is therefore to improve transduction first. Transduction is phage-mediated: raising its efficiency raises the cell's exposure to the vector that carries it. The step's rule — always improve the lowest-conversion step first — has no test for whether a low conversion rate is a defect or a defence, and here it is a defence.

**Step 7 — "Close the loop with Repetition. Each cycle informs the next."** **RAN and is contradicted, with the same measurement that contradicts Layer VI Step 07.** The measured division cycle is an adder: constant volume added per generation irrespective of starting size, two parameters, no adjustable parameters, reproduced across three species. Lineage memory is negative at depth one (mother–daughter generation-time correlation −1/4) and decays by a factor 2 per generation. Each cycle does not inform the next; each cycle actively discards the last.

**Was the hit structural or assigned after looking? (task method 4.)** The PTS result deserves the serious treatment the method asks for, because it is the only place in these four layers where canon's instruction and a measured mechanism agree on a *non-obvious* design choice. Canon's reason for tagging at capture is that attribution is destroyed if it is deferred; the PTS's measured consequence is the same — the sugar cannot be lost or misrouted once phosphorylated, and the phosphorylation state of the transporter *is* the signal that reallocates the other channels. Neither statement is derivable from "a cell eats"; both could have been otherwise, and in the same cell they *are* otherwise (ABC imports unmodified substrate at 1–2 ATP each, with a separate sensing path).
That last clause is what caps the grade. A prescription satisfied by one of three co-existing measured routes is a good design pattern, not a law, and canon states it as a law. And the mapping direction matters: ACE does not predict that a capture-and-tag device must exist — the PTS was looked up first and the resemblance noticed second. Tested predictively, ACE gets the cell's acquisition architecture wrong in three of four sub-instructions.

**Consequence for the universality claim.** Canon scopes ACE commercially in four places and simultaneously opens it, in the algorithm's own "Use when", to "any process where attention must be converted into commitment… opportunity into outcome", inside a registry it calls "Zero Exceptions". Run against the nearest real process at cellular scale, four of seven steps ran and five distinct produced statements are contradicted, one hit is descriptive, and one canonical law ("match before you shift") has a measured exception in the same organism. Graded as canon's "Use when" states it, `forced-no`.

## Forcing Test

- Test A (Ground): Is it grounded? Yes, in observed commercial practice, and one instruction (tag at capture with the capture device as sensor) is additionally grounded in a measured molecular mechanism.
- Test B (Uniqueness): Does measurement leave one candidate? No. The same cell implements capture three ways, only one of which follows canon's prescription, and it reallocates at one branch point rather than across a portfolio. At least three architectures satisfy the function, so canon's is not forced.
- Test C (Direction): Does it point one way? Partly, and where it does it pointed wrong: step 6 directed effort at the 1e-6 channel, which is a phage route whose low efficiency is protective.
- Test D (Falsifiability): Is an attached observation available that would refute it? Yes for five instructions, and all five observations exist in this one organism: three parallel capture currencies; stable flux partitioning across 91 regulator mutants; undocumented captures at the bulk of traffic; a protective low-conversion channel; and an irreversible commitment that leaves no counterparty to expand. "Match before you shift" has its own counterexample in cheRcheB cells.
- **Outcome:** `forced-no` for the claim that the ACE cycle describes any process converting attention into commitment at any scale. Two elements survive at reduced status: tag-at-capture with the capture device as reallocating sensor (`works-descriptive`, one of three measured routes), and zero-lag first response (confirmed but trivial).

## Anti-Operation (the gap this opens)

1. **Canon states ACE's scope twice, incompatibly.** "The commercial scale" (layer text, Stage 42, Sales Hub) versus "any process where attention must be converted into commitment" (the algorithm) inside a registry titled "Zero Exceptions". One of the two must be withdrawn. This run supplies the missing gate: a counterparty that can represent its state, can misreport it, and survives the transaction.
2. **ACE has no clause for a consumed counterparty.** Where acquisition destroys what it acquires, Expansion is not merely absent, it is inverted — and canon names that configuration a failure mode. Canon needs to say what replaces Expansion in consumptive systems, or scope Expansion explicitly.
3. **"Improve the lowest-conversion step first" has no defence test.** A low conversion rate can be a filter rather than a leak. This is the same hole as Layer VI's "filter nothing" (RUN-014) and F-068's collision between an optimum over discard and a prohibition on discard: canon lacks one rule distinguishing a deliberate loss from a defect.
4. **"Match before you shift" is asserted per Vibration as a law and has a measured exception in the organism that best exemplifies it.** Per F-073, canon still has no rule for what one exception costs a Correspondence claim; this is a third instance.
5. **"If not written, did not happen" is asserted in two layers and contradicted in both.** Here by undocumented bulk traffic, in RUN-014 by inherited unwritten process. The sentence needs an exception clause or a scope.
6. **The funnel step presupposes an observer the system need not contain.** Canon nowhere distinguishes instructions addressed to an operator *of* a system from properties *of* a system. At cellular scale that distinction becomes load-bearing, and four of ACE's seven steps are operator instructions rather than system properties.
