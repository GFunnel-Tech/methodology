## Canon says

v5.1, Layer I.F — The Multi-Level Observation Algorithm, quoted in full as the instrument under test:

> "Use when: anything that needs to persist must be observed at multiple nested levels simultaneously.
> 1. Identify what needs to persist. Is it a project, a relationship, a memory, a business, an idea, a culture?
> 2. Map the observation levels currently maintaining it. Quantum (physical record), Biological (lineage continuity), Cognitive (someone remembers), Civilizational (institutional record), Cosmological (structural fit), Meta-Cosmological (recursive recognition).
> 3. Identify which observation levels are absent. The missing levels are where continuation will fail.
> 4. Build observation at each missing level. Cognitive: someone must remember and care. Civilizational: there must be institutional record. Physical: there must be First Kingdom artifact.
> 5. Test for perfect process or draw position. The system must be running at one of two stable configurations — perfect optimization (Yang stable) or equilibrium that cannot be lost (Yin stable). Anything between collapses out of the observed lineage.
> 6. Confirm the recursion. Is observation observing itself at this level? Does the system maintain awareness of its own continuation?
> 7. Reinforce the weakest observation level. The recursion is only as strong as its weakest nested layer."

The layer text the steps rest on: Level 1 Quantum is defined as *"Wave function collapse upon measurement. Observer-participancy (Wheeler)"* — note that step 2 silently re-glosses it as *"physical record"*. The Recursive Stack: *"Each observation level requires the prior level's success… The stack is load-bearing all the way down… it is also load-bearing all the way up."* And: *"Everything outside them collapses out of the observed lineage."*

**Split (RUNBOOK rule 4).** *Factual core graded here:* a system missing observation levels fails to continue; a persisting system is running at perfect process or at the draw; a persisting system maintains awareness of its own continuation. All three are empirical and all three are graded below. *Framework mapping (not graded; see Narrative stripped):* Mentalism, the Omni Force, the anthropic argument, and "the bare fact of articulation as evidence".

## The algorithm run, literally, step by step

**System:** an *E. coli* K-12 lineage. "What needs to persist" is the lineage, which is what canon's Level 2 names.

1. **Identify what needs to persist.** Produced: the *E. coli* K-12 lineage. Unambiguous. **Step 1 works.**
2. **Map the observation levels currently maintaining it.** The Six Observation Levels run against a bacterium:
   - **Level 1, Quantum.** Canon's layer text defines this as *wave function collapse upon measurement / observer-participancy*; step 2 re-glosses it as *"physical record"*. Under the gloss: present — the 4.6 Mbp chromosome is a physical record. Under the definition: **absent** — no measurement locates a collapse event in bacterial physiology, and the one measured quantum record of a system (quantum Darwinism) resides in the *environment*, not in the system. **The step is completable only by equivocating between the two readings**, and the equivocation is inside canon's own text, not imported by this run.
   - **Level 2, Biological.** **Present and measured.** Differential survival of lineages is exactly what mutation accumulation over 50,000 generations measures (μ ≈ 10⁻³ per genome per generation, 264 genomes), and CRISPR spacer acquisition is a measured mechanism by which the lineage registers a past encounter. This level is real and canon's description of it fits.
   - **Level 3, Cognitive ("someone remembers").** **Present only in a degenerate form.** *E. coli* has a measured memory — a 4 s window, past 1 s positive against previous 3 s negative. It is about receptor-occupancy rates, lasts four seconds, and is not about the lineage. Nothing in the cell remembers the thing step 1 named.
   - **Level 4, Civilizational ("institutional record").** **Absent as defined.** A derivation is available and is labelled as one (AGENTS.md rule 7): the horizontally transferred gene pool functions as a population-level archive — conjugation integrates arriving DNA in up to 96% of recipients. This is a *derived* mapping, not a primary one, and canon does not make it.
   - **Level 5, Cosmological (anthropic structural fit).** **Absent.** No referent; nothing measurable.
   - **Level 6, Meta-Cosmological (recursive recognition).** **Absent.** No referent; nothing measurable.
3. **Identify which observation levels are absent — "the missing levels are where continuation will fail."** Produced: Levels 4, 5 and 6 absent; Level 1 present only by equivocation; Level 3 present only in a 4-second form unrelated to what must persist. **Canon's prediction: continuation fails. Measured: it does not.** Twelve *E. coli* populations were propagated 50,000 generations, and the taxon persists in the wild. **Step 3 produced a statement measurement contradicts.**
4. **Build observation at each missing level.** `BLOCKED: the step presumes an operator who can add observers to the system.` Recorded because the blocker is informative rather than merely obstructive: the *only* way to fill Levels 3 and 4 for *E. coli* K-12 is to count the humans who study it — the strain is held in institutional stock centres and its genome is archived. That makes the algorithm's output depend on whether the auditor counts the observers *of* the system as observation levels *of* the system. Nothing in Layer I.F says which, and the answer flips step 3's verdict. Steps 5–7 are reported below as the counterfactual continuation, flagged as such.
5. *(after the blocker)* **Test for perfect process or draw position — "anything between collapses out of the observed lineage."** Produced: **a statement measurement contradicts.** Slow-growing *E. coli* holds **>20% of its proteome** as ribosomes that are not translating, having chosen to hold elongation rate near-constant and let the active fraction absorb the change; and NADPH formation runs **20–50% above anabolic demand at every dilution rate tested**. This is reproducible, deliberate waste across most of the organism's natural growth range: not perfect optimisation, and not an equilibrium that cannot be lost (*E. coli* is killed easily). The lineage does not collapse out of anything. **The step fails.** Note the rebuttal and close it: if "readiness" is admitted as a third stable configuration — the fix [FINDINGS](../FINDINGS.md) F-079 proposes — then every measured configuration qualifies and step 5 becomes unfalsifiable instead of false. Step 5 is therefore either **false as written** or **vacuous as repaired**; there is no third option that keeps it both meaningful and true.
6. *(after the blocker)* **Confirm the recursion — "does the system maintain awareness of its own continuation?"** Produced: **no**, and the negative is measured rather than merely unobserved. No molecule in *E. coli* measures its growth rate. Cell-size homeostasis is implemented by an adder — constant added volume per generation, irrespective of birth size, two parameters and no adjustable ones, reproduced in *E. coli*, *B. subtilis* and the distant *Caulobacter crescentus* — which is precisely a rule that requires **no** awareness of the regulated quantity, and whose primary source calls it "direct experimental evidence disproving the critical size paradigm". A system with no self-measurement and no set-point persists for 50,000 generations. **The step fails.**
7. *(after the blocker)* **Reinforce the weakest observation level.** No operation available inside the system; and the weakest level (by step 3) is one whose absence is measured not to matter.

**Run result:** of seven steps, one works (1), one is completable only by equivocation (2), one is blocked (4), and three produce statements measurement contradicts (3, 5, 6). Step 7 has no referent.

## Scientific Inquiry run

1. Question (precise): Run against an *E. coli* K-12 lineage, do Layer I.F's seven steps and Six Observation Levels produce true statements — specifically, does a system missing four of six levels fail to continue, is a persisting system at perfect process or the draw, and does it maintain awareness of its own continuation?
2. What an answer must look like: a level-by-level presence/absence verdict with a measurement for each verdict; a measured persistence figure for the lineage; and for step 5 a measured quantity that is neither optimised nor unloseable.
3. Falsifiability condition: step 3 is refuted by a lineage that persists while missing levels. Step 5 is refuted by reproducible measured waste in a persisting system. Step 6 is refuted by a persisting system with no measured self-measurement. All three falsifiers were specified before the values were read; all three fired.
4. Variables: measurable / bounded / held open: **Measurable** — generations propagated, mutation rate, the 4 s memory window, conjugation integration frequency, idle-ribosome fraction, excess NADPH, the adder's two parameters. **Bounded** — idle-ribosome burden 1.1% to >20% of proteome over the growth range; NADPH excess 20–50%. **Held open** — whether Level 1 as canon *defines* it (wave-function collapse) has any bacterial referent, and whether observers *of* a system count as observation levels *of* it (canon does not say; the answer determines step 3's verdict).
5. Test designed: enumerate the six levels against one organism, requiring a measurement for every presence claim; then run steps 5 and 6 against the two measurements most likely to break them — deliberate waste, and size control without a set-point.
6. Data (unfiltered): the table above. Recorded including what helps canon: Level 2 is genuinely present and well described, and CRISPR spacer acquisition is a better instance of "a lineage registering its own continuation" than canon cites anywhere.
7. Variable Principle applied: **Measured** — 50,000 generations of persistence; >20% idle proteome; 20–50% excess NADPH; the adder with no adjustable parameters; the 4 s memory. **Structurally derived** — the pangenome-as-archive mapping for Level 4, explicitly labelled a derivation and not used to fill the level. **Held open** — Level 1's canonical definition, left unfilled rather than satisfied by the "physical record" gloss, because accepting the gloss is what makes the step pass and the substitution is canon's, not reality's.
8. Model update (Capsule: what the failed parts contribute): three products. (i) Levels 3–6 are **not** required for continuation, and the measured counter-instance is in the domain canon calls its strongest — so the Recursive Stack's "load-bearing all the way up" is forced-no at this scale, and the layer would be stronger restricted to Levels 1–2 plus whatever it can measure. (ii) Step 5's dichotomy must be replaced by something that admits reproducible waste bought for readiness; the honest version is a *third* configuration, and canon must then say what the dichotomy was for. (iii) Step 6 should be recast as a question with a measurable answer — *does the system measure the quantity it regulates, a level or a rate or neither?* — which is the same repair [FINDINGS](../FINDINGS.md) F-071 and F-080 propose elsewhere, and which the adder answers cleanly with "neither".
9. Documented: this file; extends [SWEEP-005](../sweep/SWEEP-005.md), [SWEEP-026](../sweep/SWEEP-026.md), [SWEEP-030](../sweep/SWEEP-030.md), [SWEEP-032](../sweep/SWEEP-032.md) and [FINDINGS](../FINDINGS.md) F-064, F-071, F-076, F-079 from content to algorithm.
10. Next baseline: put to the author whether observers *of* a system count as its observation levels. Until answered, step 3 has no determinate output for any system studied by anyone, which affects every application of the layer, not only this one. Re-review 2027-09-24.

## Forcing Test

- Test A (Ground): Well grounded on the reality side for Levels 1–3 and steps 5–6 (named quantities, uncertainties, reproduced measurements). Ungrounded on canon's side for Levels 4–6, which name no measurable in any system.
- Test B (Uniqueness): Fails for step 2 — two readings of Level 1 are live simultaneously in canon's own text ("wave function collapse" versus "physical record"), and the step passes under one and fails under the other. Survives for step 6: the adder's primary source states it as disproving the alternative reading, so one reading stands.
- Test C (Direction): The measured direction runs against the layer. As growth slows, the cell becomes measurably *less* optimised (idle fraction rises from 1.1% to >20%) and it persists perfectly well; canon's step 5 requires the opposite association between optimisation and continuation.
- Test D (Falsifiability): Genuinely falsifiable, tested, and **falsified three times** — steps 3, 5 and 6. This is to canon's credit as a design matter: Layer I.F, unlike Layer I.D, makes claims sharp enough to lose. It lost.
- **Outcome:** `forced-no` for the Recursive Stack's upward load-bearing, for step 5's dichotomy as written, and for step 6's awareness requirement, at bacterial scale. **`utility: fails`** — the run produced statements measurement contradicts, which is the definition of that grade.

## Anti-Operation (the gap this opens)

1. **Does observation *of* a system count as an observation level *of* it?** Canon must answer. *E. coli* K-12 has institutional archives and human rememberers, so under one reading it has all six levels covered and under the other it has two — and step 3's verdict inverts. As written the algorithm returns different answers depending on who is asking, which is worse than returning a wrong answer.
2. **Level 1 is two claims wearing one name.** The layer text's definition (wave-function collapse) and the algorithm's gloss ("physical record") are not the same requirement and are not interchangeable. Splitting them would let the physical-record half be used honestly and would stop the collapse half being credited where no collapse is measured.
3. **A third configuration is now required.** Readiness — paid for in >20% idle proteome and 20–50% excess NADPH, reproducibly, across most of an organism's natural range — is neither pole. Naming it costs canon the two-pole symmetry that connects Layer I.F to Layer I.D and to the chess insight; not naming it leaves step 5 false. Canon should choose explicitly rather than leave the choice implicit.

## Narrative stripped (if any)

Removed from the graded core: Mentalism and the Omni Force (Level 6 has no referent in this system, so the run neither supports nor refutes them); the anthropic argument at Level 5; and "The Bare Fact Of Articulation As Evidence", which is not a statement about *E. coli* and is not gradable here. Also removed: the identification of "perfect process" with Yang and "the draw" with Yin, which adds no constraint to step 5 — the step fails on the optimisation claim alone, before any pole assignment. Reality neither forces nor contradicts the Mentalist mapping; what it contradicts is the empirical half canon put in the algorithm.
