# How To Use This Methodology (Humans)

This is a **living operational doctrine** — designed to be read, run, and applied, not just studied. This page is the human on-ramp. (AI systems: see [`../AGENTS.md`](../AGENTS.md).)

## The three ways to read it

- **Sequentially (to learn it):** [README](../README.md) → this page → the [Variable Principle](../framework/registers.md) → [Layer 0](../framework/layers.md) → Layers I → ◇ in order → appendices → algorithms → axioms. The full text is [v5.1](../versions/v5.1/GFunnel-Methodology-v5.1.md).
- **For a specific problem:** go straight to the [Algorithm Index](../framework/algorithms.md), find your symptom, follow the named algorithm, reference its layer for context.
- **To verify a claim:** locate the layer, then check the [Registers](../framework/registers.md) for the framework's own falsification conditions. The framework's strength is that it names what would prove it wrong.

## The seven-point self-audit (are you using it correctly?)

1. Have I identified **which algorithm** applies to my current operation?
2. Am I running the algorithm **in order**, without skipping steps?
3. Have I **held open** the variables the framework names as open, rather than filling them with assumption?
4. Have I **documented** the run? (Undocumented work is dependency, not process.)
5. Am I applying **Correspondence** — checking whether what I learned here also applies one scale up and one scale down?
6. Am I respecting the **Variable Principle** — distinguishing measured from assumed?
7. Have I **integrated failed paths** from prior runs (Layer I.G), or am I starting from scratch each time?

If all seven check, you're applying it correctly. If any fail, that failure is the location of your next correction.

## What the framework cannot supply (and won't pretend to)

1. **The choice of action** at any decision point. It maps and weights options and surfaces the dynamic middle — it cannot choose. The choice is structurally yours; that is what makes you a co-creator rather than a computation.
2. **The naming of the outcome** after the action. It constrains which names are honest; it cannot select among them.
3. **Empirical measurements** specific to your situation. It can tell you *what* measurement would resolve a variable; it cannot take the measurement for you.
4. **The variables it explicitly holds open.** These are honest bounds, not gaps.
